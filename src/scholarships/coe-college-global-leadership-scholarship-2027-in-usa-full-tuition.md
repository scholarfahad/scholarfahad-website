The **Global Leadership Scholarship** at **Coe College** is a full-tuition award for one outstanding international student starting a bachelor's degree. Coe is a private liberal arts college in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, USA.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** at the rate for the year you start
- Renewable for **3 more years** (4 years in total), with a USD 500 increase each year

**Not covered:** housing, meals, fees, health insurance and travel. Coe asks scholarship applicants to show they can pay at least **USD 25,000 a year** towards these costs.

Only **one** Global Leadership Scholarship is awarded each year. It replaces any other Coe scholarship or grant you were offered.

## Who can apply

- You are **not a U.S. citizen or permanent resident**
- You are attending **high school outside the United States**
- You will be a **first-time, first-year** college student
- You will study full-time and **live on campus**
- You meet all Coe admission requirements
- You can show funding of about USD 25,000 a year

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to Coe College as an international student.
2. **Log in to the Coe applicant portal** and complete the separate Global Leadership Scholarship form.
3. **Upload your CV or resume** and **write the scholarship essay**.
4. Submit both your admission application and the scholarship form by the deadline.

## Deadline

**15 February 2027** for students starting in fall 2027.

## Other aid for international students at Coe

If you don't win the full-tuition award, Coe also offers:
- **International merit scholarships** of up to USD 16,000 a year, based on your grades and test scores
- **Arts scholarships** of up to USD 1,500 a year for art, music or theatre
- **Need-based grants** (amounts vary)
- **On-campus work** of about USD 2,200 a year

## Tips

- Use your essay to show real leadership: a project you started, a group you led, or a problem you helped solve in your community.
- Keep your CV to one page and focus on achievements, not just positions.
- Apply early so you have time to fix any problems with documents.

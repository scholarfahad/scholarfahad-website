The **University of Perugia** (Università degli Studi di Perugia), one of Italy's oldest universities, founded in 1308, offers **funded PhD positions** every year in a wide range of subjects. Each position comes with a **PhD scholarship**.

**Status:** the 2026/27 call (XLII cycle) closed on **10 August 2026**. The next call, for 2027/28, is expected around **June–July 2027**. Prepare now.

## What the PhD scholarship covers

- **€16,243 per year** (gross) for the **3 years** of the PhD, about €1,350 a month before tax
- The scholarship is **increased during approved research periods abroad**

Scholarship holders may also do limited paid work outside the PhD, within the rules set in the call.

## Who can apply

- Holders of a **master's degree** (Italian *laurea magistrale* or equivalent), or students finishing one
- **Foreign degree holders**, if the degree is comparable to an Italian second-cycle degree (EQF level 7) and gives access to doctoral study in the country that awarded it
- **Any nationality**
- Some PhD places are **reserved for candidates with foreign qualifications**

## How selection works

Each PhD course sets its own selection, usually based on:

- Your CV and academic record
- A **research project proposal**
- An **interview**, often possible online

## How to apply

1. When the call comes out (around June–July 2027), read it carefully, especially **Annex 1**, which lists the PhD courses, number of places and scholarships.
2. Choose your PhD course and prepare your documents.
3. Apply online through the **PICA portal**: https://pica.cineca.it/unipg/
4. Attend the interview if invited.

## Prepare now

- Your **master's degree certificate and transcripts**, translated into English or Italian if needed
- A strong **research proposal** that matches one of Perugia's PhD courses
- An academic **CV**
- **Reference letters** from your lecturers

## Tips

- Look at Perugia's current PhD courses now and pick the one closest to your research interest.
- Contact potential supervisors early. Shared interests can strengthen your proposal.
- The call is written in English and Italian. Read the English version carefully for each course's requirements.
- Never pay anyone to "secure" a PhD place. Applying is done only on the official portal.

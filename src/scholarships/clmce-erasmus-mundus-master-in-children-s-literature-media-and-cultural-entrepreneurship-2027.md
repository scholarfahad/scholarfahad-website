The Erasmus Mundus International Master in **Children's Literature, Media and Cultural Entrepreneurship (CLMCE)** is a two-year master's degree for students who love stories for young people and want to work with them, whether in research, publishing, media, libraries, education or the creative industries. Applications are now open for the **September 2027** intake.

## Where you study

CLMCE is run by five European universities:

- **University of Glasgow** (Scotland, United Kingdom), the coordinator
- **Aarhus University** (Denmark)
- **Nantes University** (France)
- **University of Wrocław** (Poland)
- **Tilburg University** (Netherlands)

Students spend their **first semester in Glasgow** and their **second semester in Aarhus**, then move to a third partner university for semester three, depending on the pathway they choose. The fourth semester is for the dissertation. There are also optional summer schools in Canada or Germany. Graduates receive a **joint or triple degree** (120 ECTS), depending on their pathway.

## What the scholarship covers

The programme offers Erasmus Mundus scholarships to the strongest applicants. Erasmus Mundus scholarships generally cover:

- **Tuition (participation) costs** for the full two years
- A **monthly allowance** (Erasmus Mundus scholars typically receive up to €1,400 per month)
- **Insurance**

Check the official website for this programme's exact scholarship package. Self-funded places are also available.

## Who can apply

- An **academic bachelor's degree** in a relevant **arts, humanities or social sciences** subject
- **Proof of English language proficiency**
- The selection panel also looks at your **motivation, references, grades and extracurricular activities**

Check the official website for the minimum English test scores before you apply.

## Documents you'll need

- Bachelor's degree certificate and **transcripts**
- **English language** test result
- **CV**
- **Motivation letter**
- **Reference letters** (academic or professional)
- **Passport** copy

## How to apply

Applications are made online through the **University of Glasgow**, which coordinates the programme. Go to the official CLMCE page, choose the Erasmus Mundus scholarship route, and submit before **22 January 2027**. Applying for the scholarship is free.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Show your passion for children's and young adult literature or media.** Mention books, writing, teaching, storytelling, library or publishing experience, or creative projects.
- **Connect your goals to the programme's mix of literature and entrepreneurship.** Explain what you want to create or research after graduating.
- **Choose referees who know your work well.** Ask them at least a month before the deadline.

The **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Imaging (EMJM Imaging)** is a 2-year master's degree funded by the European Union. It trains experts in **image processing, visual AI, machine learning and immersive media**, skills used in cameras, medical imaging, virtual reality, self-driving cars and more.

## Partner universities

- **Tampere University (TAU)**, Finland
- **Politecnico di Milano (POLIMI)**, Italy
- **Mid Sweden University (MIUN)**, Sweden

## Study tracks

You spend year 1 at one university and year 2 at another:

| Track | Year 1 → Year 2 |
|---|---|
| **Visual AI** | Politecnico di Milano → Mid Sweden University |
| **Image Modelling and Data-intensive Imaging** | Politecnico di Milano → Tampere University |
| **Immersive Imaging** | Mid Sweden University → Tampere University |

**Duration:** 2 years (120 ECTS), leading to a Master of Science (Technology).

## What the scholarship covers

- **Tuition (participation costs)**
- **EUR 1,400 per month** for up to 24 months
- **Medical insurance**
- A contribution towards **travel and installation**

About **15 scholarships** were awarded per year in the last call. Students without a scholarship pay **EUR 8,000 per year** (non-EU/EEA).

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- A **bachelor's degree** in **ICT, computer engineering, electrical engineering** or a related field
- About **30 ECTS in mathematics** (including probability, statistics and linear algebra)
- About **60 ECTS in computer engineering**, including object-oriented programming
- **English** proficiency (see the official website for accepted tests)

## Documents you need (PDF)

- Passport (preferred) or ID card
- **CV** (maximum 2 pages)
- **Motivation letter**
- Degree certificates and transcripts, with English translations
- **One recommendation letter**
- Proof of English

## How to apply

1. Read the requirements and choose your **track** on the official EMJM Imaging website.
2. Prepare your documents.
3. Apply online when the portal opens on **23 November 2026**.
4. Submit before **12 January 2027**.

Applications are scored on academic excellence and CV, the motivation letter and the recommendation letter.

## Deadline

**12 January 2027** (applications open on 23 November 2026)

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, mention projects in image processing, computer vision, machine learning or programming.
- Choose the track that best matches your background and career goals, and explain why.
- Make sure your transcript clearly shows your maths and programming courses.

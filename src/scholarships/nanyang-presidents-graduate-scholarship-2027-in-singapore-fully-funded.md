The **Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship (NPGS)** is the most prestigious PhD scholarship at **Nanyang Technological University (NTU)** in Singapore. NTU is one of the top universities in Asia and the world. The scholarship is for outstanding graduates who want to become leaders in research, and it is open to students of **all nationalities**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees**
- **Monthly stipend:**
  - International students: **SGD 4,300**
  - Singapore Permanent Residents: SGD 4,800
  - Singapore Citizens: SGD 5,300 (plus CPF contributions)
- **Overseas conference allowance:** up to SGD 5,000 per year
- **Book allowance:** SGD 500 per year
- **IT allowance:** SGD 1,500 (one time)
- **Thesis allowance:** SGD 600 (one time)
- **Priority for campus accommodation**

The scholarship is given for one year at a time and renewed if you make good progress, for up to **4 years**. There is **no bond**, so you are free to work anywhere after graduating.

International scholars take part in the Graduate Assistantship Programme, which involves some teaching or lab supervision.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- You have an **Honours (Highest Distinction) or equivalent** bachelor's degree. That usually means First Class or the very top of your class.
- Final-year students can apply if their university confirms they are on track for this result.
- Strong research potential

This scholarship is **by nomination only**. You apply for a PhD at NTU, and your school nominates its strongest candidates. Shortlisted applicants are interviewed.

## Documents you need

- Passport or identification
- Degree certificates and transcripts (with English translations if needed)
- **Research proposal**
- **Two referee reports**
- CV or resume
- Research publications, if you have any
- Test scores where required (for example IELTS, TOEFL, GRE or GMAT)

## How to apply

1. Choose a PhD programme at NTU and read its specific requirements.
2. Apply online through the NTU graduate admissions portal.
3. Upload your research proposal and documents, and give the contact details of your two referees.
4. If your school nominates you, you will be invited for an interview.
5. Successful candidates receive the offer by email.

## Deadline

**31 December 2026** for the 2027–2028 academic year (applications opened on 1 November 2026).

## Tips

- Your research proposal matters a lot. Make it clear, focused and linked to work at NTU.
- Look for an NTU professor whose research matches yours, and mention their work in your proposal.
- Ask your referees early, and choose people who know your research and academic work well.

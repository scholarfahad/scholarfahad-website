The **UCL African Graduate Scholarship** helps talented African students in financial need study the **MSc Global Health and Development** at **University College London (UCL)**, one of the world's top universities. The scholarship is offered by the **UCL Institute for Global Health**.

## What the scholarship covers

- A **substantial contribution towards tuition fees**
- Help with **accommodation** and **some living costs**

UCL does not publish a fixed amount. **Travel to and from the UK is not covered**, so you must show how you will pay for your flights.

The award is for **one academic year** (September to September), which is the length of the full-time MSc.

## Eligible programme

**MSc Global Health and Development (full-time)**, UCL Institute for Global Health, London

## Who can apply

- You are an **African national** and a **permanent resident of an African country** when you apply
- You hold an **unconditional offer** for the full-time MSc Global Health and Development
- You have an **outstanding academic and professional background**
- You can show **genuine financial need**
- You can show **other funding for your travel** to and from the UK

## How to apply

1. **Apply for the MSc Global Health and Development** through UCL's graduate application portal.
2. **Meet all the offer conditions** so your offer becomes **unconditional**.
3. **Complete the separate scholarship application form** on the UCL Institute for Global Health funding page.
4. Submit before the deadline.

Applicants are selected on academic excellence and financial need.

## Deadline

**29 May 2027** (scholarship application). Apply for the MSc **early**, because you need an unconditional offer before the scholarship deadline.

## Tips

- In your scholarship form, explain clearly why you need financial support and how the MSc will help you improve health in your community or country.
- Show relevant experience, such as work in health, NGOs, research or public policy.
- Plan for your flight costs early. They are not covered.

## Other UCL scholarships for Africans

UCL also offers awards such as the **Bartlett Promise Sub-Saharan Africa Master's Scholarship** and the **UCL Global Masters Scholarships**, which cover other courses.

The Ontario Institute for Studies in Education (OISE) is the University of Toronto's graduate school of education, one of the best-ranked education faculties in the world. Students admitted to its **full-time PhD and full-time MA programmes** (the "funded cohort") automatically receive a graduate funding package, including international students.

> **Deadline:** Applications for Fall 2027 close on **2 November 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern Time (Toronto)**. Applications are open now.

## What the funding covers

**PhD students**
- At least **CA$40,000 per year**, including tuition and incidental fees
- Usually funded for **years 1 to 4**
- International students' packages are increased to cover the mandatory **UHIP health insurance**

**MA students (full-time)**
- At least **CA$26,892** in year 1 (2026–27 figure)
- Usually funded for **year 1 only**. Plan how you will pay for your second year.

Packages can combine scholarships, fellowships, awards, grants and paid work as a teaching assistant (TA) or graduate assistant (GA).

## How you get it
There is **no separate application**. If you are admitted to an eligible programme, you receive a funding letter with your admission offer, and OISE prepares a personalised package. Letters usually go out between 15 August and 30 September.

## Eligible programmes (Fall 2027)

**Full-time PhD**
- Adult Education and Community Development
- Clinical and Counselling Psychology
- Curriculum and Pedagogy
- Developmental Psychology and Education
- Educational Leadership and Policy
- Higher Education
- Language and Literacies Education
- School and Clinical Child Psychology
- Social Justice Education

**Full-time MA**
- Adult Education and Community Development
- Curriculum and Pedagogy
- Educational Leadership and Policy
- Higher Education
- Language and Literacies Education
- Social Justice Education

## Conditions to keep your funding
- Register full-time in fall, winter and summer
- Do not work full-time (35+ hours a week) in fall or winter
- Keep at least an A− average
- Apply for and accept major external awards (CA$15,000+) you qualify for
- Accept TA or GA positions offered as part of your package

## How to apply
1. Choose a full-time PhD or MA programme at OISE and check its admission requirements.
2. Apply online through the University of Toronto School of Graduate Studies before **2 November 2026**.
3. Prepare your transcripts, statement of intent, CV, references and English test results if required.
4. If admitted, your funding package comes with your offer.

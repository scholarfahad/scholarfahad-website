**Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)** in Wuhan is one of China's top research universities, strong in engineering, computer science, medicine and management. Through the **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) – Chinese University Program**, HUST offers **fully funded** master's and PhD places to international students.

> **Opening soon:** HUST has not yet published its 2027 graduate guide. Last year, applications ran from **15 October 2025 to 1 March 2026**. Expect a similar window, and prepare your documents now.

## What the scholarship covers
This is a **full scholarship**, covering:
- Tuition fees
- Accommodation on campus
- A monthly stipend
- Comprehensive medical insurance

## Who can apply
- International students of **any nationality**, in good health
- **Master's:** a bachelor's degree, **under 35**
- **PhD:** a master's degree, **under 40**
- **English (for English-taught programmes):** IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 80, GRE 310 or equivalent. If your previous degree was taught in English or Chinese, you don't need a certificate.
- Some schools require a **supervisor's acceptance letter** (check the programme list)

## Key information
- **HUST agency number:** **10487**
- **Programme category:** Type B
- **Application fee:** 600 CNY or **US$100** (non-refundable)

## How to apply
1. **Apply online** on the HUST Online Applicant System and upload your academic documents.
2. **Take the online language test** if required, then **pay the application fee**.
3. **Academic review:** the school reviews your file. If you pass, you receive a **Pre-admission Letter**.
4. **Apply on the Chinese Government Scholarship Information System** (CSC portal) using agency number **10487**. Upload the Pre-admission Letter and print the application form.
5. **Upload the remaining documents** to the HUST system and wait for the nomination result.

## Tips
- Contact a potential supervisor early. A supervisor's support strengthens your application, even where it's optional.
- Prepare your passport, degree certificates, transcripts, study plan, two recommendation letters and a medical (physical examination) form well before the deadline.
- Apply early in the window rather than at the last minute.

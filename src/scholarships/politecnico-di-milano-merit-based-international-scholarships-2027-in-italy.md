**Politecnico di Milano (PoliMi)** is Italy's top technical university, ranked among the world's best for engineering, architecture and design. Each year it offers **merit-based scholarships** to talented **international students** starting an **English-taught Master of Science**.

> **Apply early:** Only **Early Bird** applicants can get these scholarships. Last year, the Early Bird application fee had to be paid between **1 October and 1 December**, and the scholarship form submitted by **15 December** (noon, Italian time). Expect similar dates for 2027/28.

## The scholarships (2026/27 call)
Every scholarship includes a **full tuition fee waiver**. You only pay a small administrative fee (about €170).

| Scholarship | Number | Yearly amount (gross) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platinum** | 10 | **€10,000** + tuition waiver | Milan (Leonardo or Bovisa) |
| **Gold** | 18 | **€8,000** + tuition waiver | Milan (Leonardo or Bovisa) |
| **Gold Piacenza** | 16 | **€5,000** + tuition waiver | Piacenza: Sustainable Architecture and Landscape Design, or Energy Engineering |
| **Bronze Piacenza** | 10 | **€2,500** + tuition waiver | Piacenza: Mechanical Engineering (Mechatronics for Manufacturing) |
| **Silver** | 10 | Tuition waiver only | Cremona, Lecco, Mantova or Piacenza campuses |

The money is paid in two instalments (except Bronze); the second depends on the exams you pass. Scholarships last at least one year and can be **renewed** for the second year if you meet the credit requirements.

## Who can apply
- **International students** applying to the **first year** of an **English-taught Master of Science** at PoliMi
- You must apply in the **Early Bird** admission round
- You must be **admitted**, accept your offer and provide a **valid English certificate** by the deadlines
- Selection is based mainly on your **GPA and academic achievements**; some programmes ask for a portfolio

## How to apply
1. **Apply for admission** to an English-taught MSc in the **Early Bird** round, and pay the application fee in time.
2. Go to **PoliMi Online Services** → *Benefits and Agreements* → *Economic benefits* → *Scholarships for International Master of Science Students*, and complete the **scholarship application**. No other method is accepted.
3. Once admitted, **accept your offer** and upload your **English certificate**.

## Tips
- Apply as early as possible in the Early Bird window.
- Your GPA counts most, so include clear transcripts and any rankings or awards.
- Consider the Piacenza, Lecco or Cremona campuses too, which have their own scholarships.

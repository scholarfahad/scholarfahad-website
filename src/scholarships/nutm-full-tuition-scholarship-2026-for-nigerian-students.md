The **Nigerian University of Technology and Management (NUTM)**, a private university in Lagos, is offering **full tuition scholarships** through the **NUTM Scholars Program** to Nigerian students starting an undergraduate degree in 2026.

NUTM launched the Scholars Program in 2020 to develop future leaders for Nigeria and Africa.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** for your undergraduate degree at NUTM. Tuition for Computer Science, Cybersecurity and Information Technology is ₦2,000,000 per year.

**Not covered:** accommodation (₦500,000 per year), the ₦250,000 acceptance fee, feeding, transport and course materials. Plan for these costs before you accept an offer.

## Courses

All are B.Sc. programmes:

- Computer Science
- Cybersecurity
- Information Technology
- Accounting
- Business Administration
- Economics
- Entrepreneurship

## Who can apply

- **Nigerian citizen** and an **indigene of an eligible state**. The list of eligible states isn't published, so check the application form or email admissions@nutm.edu.ng.
- Seeking admission to an undergraduate programme at NUTM
- **Minimum of 180 in the 2026 UTME (JAMB)**
- Five O'Level credits, including **English, Mathematics and Physics**, in **one sitting**
- Shows academic promise, personal integrity and a commitment to contributing to their community and Africa

## Deadline

**10 November 2026**, as reported. Confirm on the application form. NUTM reviews admission applications on a rolling basis, so applying early is better.

## How to apply

1. Read the requirements on NUTM's undergraduate programme page: https://www.nutm.edu.ng/academics/undergraduate-program/
2. Fill in the NUTM Scholars Program application form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc0P-Hlj3DFMYEJSsGMtXDQ6Lj2Ry7oYo8LqKHFemVw8FYq5A/viewform
3. Apply for admission to your chosen course. There is **no application fee**.
4. Shortlisted applicants are interviewed. NUTM says results come by email within three weeks of the interview.

Questions: **admissions@nutm.edu.ng**

## Also at NUTM: scholarship for young women from Asaba

NUTM also runs the **JC, CM, FN Edozien Scholarship** for young women from **Asaba** who want to study science and technology. Details are in the brochure on NUTM's undergraduate programme page.

## Tips

- NUTM never asks you to pay anyone to "secure" a scholarship. Only pay official fees after admission, through NUTM's official channels.
- Prepare a short, honest answer on why you chose your course and how you want to contribute to your community. Selection looks at character as well as grades.
- Get your O'Level result and JAMB slip ready before you start the form.

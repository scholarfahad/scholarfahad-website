The **Senator Abiola Ajimobi Foundation (SAAF)**, set up in memory of the late former Governor of Oyo State, Senator Abiola Ajimobi, is offering its **2026 Educational Scholarship** to support brilliant students from Oyo State who need financial help.

This scholarship is open only to **indigenes of Oyo State**.

## Award

- **₦150,000** education grant for each successful applicant

## Who can apply

- An **indigene of Oyo State**
- Enrolled at a **tertiary institution in Nigeria** (university, polytechnic or college of education)
- Undergraduate or postgraduate students
- A strong academic record
- **Genuine financial need**

## Documents you need

1. A completed **application form**
2. Your **academic transcript** or statement of results
3. A **reference letter from your Head of Department**, signed and stamped
4. A **personal statement** of no more than **500 words** about your academic goals, your financial situation and how the grant will help you

## How to apply

1. Visit the official **[Abiola Ajimobi Foundation website](https://www.abiolaajimobifoundation.com/)**.
2. Open the **SAAF Scholarship 2026** application form.
3. Fill in your details and upload your documents.
4. Submit before **5 November 2026**, and keep a copy of your confirmation.

## Deadline

**5 November 2026**

## Tips

- **Get your HOD letter now.** It must be signed and stamped, which can take a few days.
- **Write an honest personal statement** that explains your financial need and your goals clearly.
- **Apply only through the foundation's official website.**
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who asks for a fee.

**King Mongkut's University of Technology North Bangkok (KMUTNB)**, through its Center for International Engineering Programs (CIEP), is offering **direct admission with tuition-waived scholarships** to outstanding students for the 2027 academic year. All programmes are taught in English.

## What the scholarship covers

- **100% or 50% tuition waiver** for the four-year programme, based on merit
- The full tuition is about **480,000 baht** (about USD 15,000) for the whole programme
- You must keep a GPA of at least 2.85 out of 4.00 to keep the scholarship

**Not covered:** accommodation, living costs, flights and visa fees.

## Programmes (English-taught)

- Aerospace Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering for Design and Innovation
- Robotic Engineering and Automation System
- Materials Engineering and Design
- Electrical Engineering and Smart System (with an option of 3 years at KMUTNB plus a 2-year Master's in Taiwan)
- Artificial Intelligence and Communication Engineering (new programme)

## Eligibility

- Open to international students of any nationality
- A high school diploma or equivalent, or in your final year of high school
- Strong results in mathematics, physics and chemistry
- One standardized test result: SAT, GED, IGCSE/A Level or equivalent
- An English test: IELTS, TOEFL or equivalent (native speakers may be exempt)

## Required documents

- Application form
- High school transcripts
- Standardized test results
- English proficiency test results
- Recommendation letter
- Cover letter
- Passport or ID copy

## How to apply

1. Choose your engineering programme.
2. Apply through the CIEP online application portal. Email applications are not accepted.
3. Upload all documents.
4. Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview.

## Deadlines (2027 intake)

- **Round 1:** 16 November 2026 to **10 January 2027**
- **Round 2:** 1 to 31 March 2027

Apply in Round 1 for the best chance, as scholarship places are limited.

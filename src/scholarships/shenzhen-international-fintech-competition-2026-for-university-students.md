The **2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (FinTechathon)** invites university student teams from around the world to build **AI-powered finance tools**. It is hosted by **Tsinghua University's PBC School of Finance** with **WeBank**, a leading Chinese digital bank.

## Prizes

Total prize pool: **RMB 250,000** (about US$35,000)

- **First prize:** RMB 100,000
- **Second prize:** RMB 80,000
- **Third prize:** RMB 50,000
- **Best Innovation Award:** RMB 20,000
- Several Excellence Awards

## Benefits for finalists

- **Free round-trip international flights and accommodation** to Shenzhen for invited finalists
- A visit to WeBank's headquarters
- Fast-track job interviews with WeBank for outstanding teams

## Theme: "AI as a Financial Participant"

Choose **one** topic:

- **A.** Personal finance assistant
- **B.** Wealth advisory agent
- **C.** Cross-border and student finance assistant
- **D.** Insurance assistant
- **E.** SME finance copilot
- **F.** Agent wallet (an autonomous on-chain wallet, with bonus points)

Everything is in **English**: submissions, presentations and judging.

## Who can enter

- **Full-time students** (bachelor's, master's or PhD) at universities or research institutions **outside mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Chinese Taipei**
- Teams of **2 to 5 students**. You can mix years, courses, universities and countries.
- At least **60% of team members must be non-Chinese nationals**
- Each student can join only **one team**

**Confirm your eligibility first.** An earlier announcement said the International Track was for teams from APEC economies. The latest notice doesn't mention this. Email the organiser to confirm your team qualifies before you start building.

## Entry fee

**Free.**

## Timeline

- **Registration and submission deadline:** 20 October 2026, 11:59 PM Beijing time
- **Online review:** 20–31 October 2026
- **Finalists announced:** 31 October 2026
- **WeBank visit and check-in:** 20 November 2026
- **Grand final and awards:** 21–23 November 2026 in Shenzhen

## How to enter

1. Form a team of 2 to 5 students and choose a topic.
2. Download the official registration form from the competition notice: https://ird.oou.cmu.ac.th/2026-shenzhen-international-fintech-competition/
3. Email the completed form and each member's **passport bio page** to **WANG Yiran: wangyr@pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn** to confirm your team's slot.
4. Build your project and email your full submission before **20 October 2026**.

Problem details are on the official site: https://fintechathon.g-ican.com

## Tips

- Register and email the organiser **today**. There isn't much time.
- Keep your project focused: one clear user problem, a working demo and a short video.
- Make sure every member has a valid passport. Finalists need a visa to travel to China.

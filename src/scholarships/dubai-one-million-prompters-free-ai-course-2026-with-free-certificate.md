**One Million Prompters** is a free online training programme launched by the **Dubai Centre for Artificial Intelligence (DCAI)** under Dubai's Universal Blueprint for Artificial Intelligence. Its goal is to train **one million people** worldwide in **prompt engineering**: how to give AI tools clear instructions that get useful results.

## What you will learn

The course has **four short modules** (about one hour each):

1. **AI basics:** what artificial intelligence is and how it works
2. **Chatbots:** how to write better prompts for AI chatbots
3. **Productivity tools:** using AI to work faster and smarter
4. **Generative AI creative tools:** creating text, images and more with AI

## What you get

- **100% free.** No course or registration fees
- A free **certificate in AI prompt literacy** after you finish the modules and quizzes
- **Self-paced** learning: study any time, on your phone or computer
- Available in **English and Arabic**

## Who can join

- **Anyone, from any country**
- Students, teachers, job seekers and working professionals
- **No AI experience** or special qualification needed
- No IELTS or other test

## How to register

1. Go to the official website: **dub.ai/en/omp**
2. Click **"Sign Up"**.
3. Fill in the form: name, email, phone, password, nationality, date of birth, country, city, organisation or university, job title and experience level.
4. Accept the terms and click **"Register"**.
5. Open the **confirmation email** and verify your account.
6. Log in and start the first module.

## Deadline

**No deadline.** The programme is open now, and you can start any time.

## Tips

- Finish one module a day. You can complete the whole course in less than a week.
- Practise each skill right away with a free AI tool you already use.
- Add the certificate to your **CV and LinkedIn** to show your AI skills.

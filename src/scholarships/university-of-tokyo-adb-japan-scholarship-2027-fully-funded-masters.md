The **University of Tokyo** is one of the universities taking part in the **Asian Development Bank–Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP)**. The scholarship fully funds a Master's degree for professionals from developing countries in **Asia and the Pacific**. At UTokyo, applications go through the **Graduate School of Frontier Sciences (GSFS)** for study starting in October 2027.

## Who is eligible

This scholarship is for citizens of **ADB borrowing member countries**, which are developing countries in Asia and the Pacific, such as Bangladesh, Pakistan, Indonesia, the Philippines, Viet Nam, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Mongolia, Uzbekistan and Pacific island nations. **African countries are not eligible.**

You must also:
- Hold a bachelor's degree with a strong academic record
- Have at least **2 years of full-time work experience** after your bachelor's degree, related to your field of study
- Be **35 years old or younger** when you apply
- Have good written and spoken English
- Be in good health
- Live and work in your home country (not abroad) when you apply
- Not already be enrolled in a graduate degree
- Agree to return home and contribute to your country's development after graduating

## What the scholarship covers

- Full tuition
- A monthly subsistence and housing allowance
- Economy-class airfare to and from Japan
- Medical and accident insurance
- Allowances for books and research or thesis costs

The scholarship covers the scholar only, not family members.

## Programmes

Master's programmes at the Graduate School of Frontier Sciences, including Materials Science, Advanced Energy, Integrated Biosciences, Environment Systems, Natural Environmental Studies, International Studies and Sustainability Science. PhD study is not covered.

## How to apply

1. Choose a GSFS Master's programme and, if possible, contact a potential supervisor.
2. Submit the GSFS ADB-JSP application form, a study and research plan, and a recommendation letter by the deadline.
3. GSFS screens the applications and shortlists candidates in mid-February 2027.
4. The University of Tokyo nominates shortlisted candidates to ADB in mid-March 2027.
5. ADB announces the final results around May 2027.

## Key dates

- **Application deadline:** 10 December 2026 (most GSFS departments)
- Integrated Biosciences applicants follow the department's own November entrance exam schedule
- Shortlisting: mid-February 2027
- Nomination to ADB: mid-March 2027
- ADB results: around May 2027
- Study begins: 1 October 2027

**Another route:** the University of Tokyo's Graduate School of Public Policy (GraSPP) also offers ADB-JSP places for its Master of Public Policy, International Program (MPP/IP). It requires an admission application through GraSPP; check its admissions page for dates.

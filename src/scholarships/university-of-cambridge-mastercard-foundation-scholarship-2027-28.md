The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Cambridge** is open for **2027/2028**. It gives talented young **Africans** the chance to study a **fully funded one-year master's degree** at one of the world's best universities.

The programme's themes are **climate resilience and sustainability**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees**
- A **monthly maintenance allowance** for living costs
- **Visa and immigration health surcharge** (per the programme FAQ)
- **Travel**, including flights and transfer to Cambridge
- **A laptop**

## Who can apply

- A **citizen of an African country**
- **Living in Africa**, unless you're temporarily away for study or work. Internally displaced people and refugees are exempt.
- Applying for a **full-time master's course of about one year**
- Meeting your course's academic requirements. **Most courses ask for a first-class degree**, and some accept a good second-class degree.
- A commitment to **giving back** and to **sustainability and climate resilience** in Africa
- **Not eligible:** dual African/UK citizens. The programme does not fund PhDs.

**Excluded courses:** MBA, Master of Corporate Law (MCL), Master of Finance (MFin), Master of Accounting (MAcc).

## Key dates

- **Information webinar:** Tuesday 3 November 2026, 11:00 UK time (register on the programme page)
- **Application fee waiver request**, if your country isn't automatically covered: **18 November 2026**
- **Course application and funding deadline:** usually **early December or early January**, depending on the course. Use the **funding deadline**, not the later standard deadline.
- **Department selection:** December to February
- **Offers:** late March or early April

You can apply **without IELTS** and get a conditional offer. English results are usually due by 31 July.

## How to apply

1. Find a full-time one-year master's in the Cambridge course directory: https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses
2. Click **Apply Now** and start your application.
3. Request an **application fee waiver** if your country isn't automatically covered.
4. In the funding section, **opt in for funding** and choose the **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program**.
5. Write your **scholarship statement**: your education and career, leadership potential, how the scholarship helps your goals, and how you'll contribute to sustainable futures in Africa.
6. Submit before your course's **funding deadline**.

Programme page: https://www.mastercardfoundation.fund.cam.ac.uk/news/2027-applications-now-open

## Tips

- Check your course's funding deadline **today**. Some courses close in early December.
- Link your scholarship statement clearly to **climate and sustainability** in your community.
- Strong academic references matter a lot at Cambridge.
- Cambridge warns: **don't pay anyone** to help with your application.

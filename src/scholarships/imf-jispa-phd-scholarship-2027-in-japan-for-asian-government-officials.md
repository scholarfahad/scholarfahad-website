The **Japan-IMF Scholarship Program for Asia (JISPA)**, funded by the Government of Japan and run by the **International Monetary Fund (IMF)**, offers fully funded PhD scholarships to **government officials** from Asia and the Pacific who want to do a **PhD in economics** at a university in Japan.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Admission and tuition fees**
- A **monthly stipend** for living costs
- **One round-trip economy flight** to Japan
- The university's required **health insurance** package (paid from the stipend)
- Possible support for **field research** and **international conferences**

Family members' costs are not covered, and you cannot hold another scholarship at the same time.

## Eligible countries

Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Lao P.D.R., Malaysia, Maldives, Mongolia, Nepal, Pacific Island countries, Papua New Guinea, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, Tajikistan, Thailand, Timor-Leste, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan and Vietnam.

## Who can apply

- A **citizen** of one of the eligible countries
- **Currently employed** by, and **nominated** by, a key economic agency, such as a **central bank**, **ministry of finance, economy, planning or trade**, **tax administration**, **statistics bureau** or **financial regulator**
- A **master's degree** (or equivalent), with about 17 years of formal education
- **English:** TOEFL iBT 79–80 or higher, or IELTS 6.0 or higher
- **Priority** for applicants **under 38** with at least **two years** of public-sector experience
- You must **return to your agency** after your studies
- You must not have been on leave for more than one consecutive month in the 12 months before 1 September 2027

## How to apply

1. **Apply for PhD admission** to a doctoral programme in economics at an accredited university in Japan. The IMF does not handle university applications.
2. **Get nominated** by your agency, with the signed nomination and certification form (including a promise to reinstate you after your studies).
3. **Read the 2027–28 Application Guidelines**, expected by early March 2027.
4. **Submit your JISPA application** between **1 May and 1 June 2027** (23:59 Japan time).
5. Shortlisted applicants may be invited to an **interview**.

Studies start in **September/October 2027** or **April 2028**. An extra round, closing on 1 December 2027 for April 2028 entry, may open if places remain.

## Tips

- **Start your university admission now.** Japanese PhD admissions can take months.
- **Talk to your HR or training department early** about the nomination.
- **Prepare your English test** in advance.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises an IMF scholarship.

**AMASE** (Joint European Master Programme in Advanced Materials Science and Engineering) is an **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master** run by the European School of Materials (EUSMAT). Students spend their two years at **two of six leading European universities** and graduate with a **double master's degree**.

Full and partial **Erasmus Mundus scholarships** are available for the 2027 intake, and the programme is open to students from all countries.

## Partner universities

You study your first year at one university and your second year at another:

- **Saarland University** (Saarbrücken, Germany)
- **Université de Lorraine** (Nancy, France)
- **Luleå University of Technology** (Luleå, Sweden)
- **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya** (Barcelona, Spain)
- **Montanuniversität Leoben** (Leoben, Austria)
- **University of Padua** (Padua, Italy)

## What the Erasmus Mundus scholarship covers

- **Monthly allowance of €1,400** for the full 24 months of the programme
- **Participation (tuition) costs** covered
- **Health insurance**
- Support from the programme throughout your mobility

**Partial scholarships** are also available. Admission does not guarantee a scholarship, because places are competitive.

### Participation fees (without a scholarship)

- Non-EU students: €4,500 per semester
- EU students: €1,500 per semester

Self-funded students can also apply for Erasmus+, Franco-German University (DFH) grants and student assistant jobs.

## Who can apply

- **All nationalities** (EU and non-EU)
- A **bachelor's degree** in materials science, materials engineering, physics, chemistry or a related engineering or natural science
- Good **English skills**, plus at least basic knowledge of the language of your second university (check the official language requirements)

## Application deadlines for 2027

**Scholarship applicants:**

| First university | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Luleå University of Technology (Sweden) | **15 January 2027** |
| Saarland, Lorraine, UPC, Leoben or Padua | **15 February 2027** |

**Self-funded applicants:**

| First university | Non-EU students | EU students |
|---|---|---|
| Luleå | 31 March 2027 | 31 March 2027 |
| Saarland, Lorraine, UPC, Leoben or Padua | 31 March 2027 | 31 May 2027 |

## How to apply

1. **Choose your two universities** and the specialisation that matches your interests.
2. **Check the language requirements** for both universities.
3. **Prepare your documents:** degree certificate, transcripts, CV, motivation letter, recommendation letters, English test results and passport.
4. **Apply online** through the AMASE application page on [eusmat.net](https://www.eusmat.net/international-studies/master/amase/).
5. **Select the Erasmus Mundus scholarship** in your application and submit before your deadline.

## Tips

- **Apply before 15 January** if Luleå is your first choice.
- **Explain your choice of universities** in your motivation letter, and how they fit your career goals.
- **Highlight lab work, research projects and internships** in materials, physics or chemistry.
- **Learn some basics of your second university's language.** It strengthens your application.
- Applying for an Erasmus Mundus scholarship is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed scholarship.

Nigerian music producer and Mavin Records founder **Don Jazzy** has launched the **Don Jazzy 2027 Tech Scholarship** with **TS Academy**, to help young Nigerians learn in-demand tech and digital skills. He announced it on his X (Twitter) page on 26 October 2026.

## What the scholarship offers

- **Up to 100% tuition discount** (up to ₦447,000 off course fees), depending on the award
- **A discount on the application fee** (a reduced fee still applies)
- **Laptops** (about 50) and **data support** for selected beneficiaries, based on need and availability
- Beginner-friendly courses, with internship and career support from TS Academy

## Courses available

- Artificial Intelligence and Automation
- Data Analytics
- Data Science
- Cybersecurity
- Software Development
- Frontend Development
- Backend Development
- DevOps Engineering
- Product Design
- Product Management
- Project Management
- Digital Marketing
- Content Creation
- Graphics Design
- Virtual Assistance

## Who can apply

- **Nigerians** interested in learning tech or digital skills
- **Beginners are welcome.** Most courses don't need any prior tech experience.
- A working email address and phone number

## How to apply

1. Go to the official scholarship portal, **[donjazzy.tsacademyonline.com](https://donjazzy.tsacademyonline.com)**, or use the link in Don Jazzy's social media bio.
2. **Choose your course.**
3. **Fill in the application form** with your personal details.
4. **Request a laptop or data support** in the form if you need it.
5. **Watch your email and phone** for updates after assessment.

## Deadline

No closing date has been announced. Slots are **limited**, so apply as soon as possible.

## Tips

- **Choose one course** that matches your interests and career goals.
- **Explain your need clearly** if you're asking for a laptop or data.
- **Use only the official portal** linked from Don Jazzy's verified social media accounts.
- **Be careful of scammers.** Don't pay anyone who claims they can "guarantee" you a slot.

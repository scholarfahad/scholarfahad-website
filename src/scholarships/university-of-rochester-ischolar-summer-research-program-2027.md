**iScholar** is the **International Student Summer Research Program** run by the **Department of Chemistry at the University of Rochester** in New York, USA. It gives outstanding chemistry students from outside the United States two months of hands-on research experience in a Rochester lab. It's an excellent step towards a funded chemistry PhD in the US.

## What the programme covers

- A **weekly stipend** for living expenses
- **On-campus accommodation**
- **Health insurance**
- **Registration and tuition fees** linked to the programme
- Up to **US$1,250** towards travel costs

**Not covered:** visa and SEVIS fees.

## Programme dates

Last year's programme ran from **1 June to 31 July**. If your academic calendar is different, you can say when you're available in your application and the programme will try to set dates that work for you.

## Who can apply

- **Undergraduate or master's students** studying outside the US, or recent graduates of a non-US university
- Studying **chemistry or a related field** (such as physics, biology, engineering or computer science)
- **Not** enrolled in, and never started, a PhD
- **Planning to apply for a chemistry PhD in the US** (preference for students starting a PhD soon)
- **All nationalities**
- **No English test is required**

## Documents you need

1. Online application form
2. Unofficial **transcript**, with an English translation (you can translate it yourself)
3. **CV** in PDF format
4. Contact details for **two professors** who know your work (they will be asked for reference letters)

## How to apply

1. Watch the official **[iScholar page](https://sas.rochester.edu/chm/undergraduate/i-scholar.html)** for the 2027 call.
2. Prepare your transcript, CV and two referees now.
3. Submit the online application before the deadline. Last year it closed on **23 January**.
4. Decisions are usually made in late March.

For questions, email **summer@chem.rochester.edu**.

## Tips

- **Tell your professors early** so they're ready to write reference letters.
- **Highlight any lab or research experience** in your CV, such as a final-year project, lab assistant work or publications.
- **Explain your PhD plans clearly.** The programme is for students aiming for a US chemistry PhD.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone to apply for you.

The **State of Kuwait**, through its **Ministry of Higher Education** and **Kuwait University**, offers scholarships to international (non-Kuwaiti) students every year. Most places are for **bachelor's degrees**, with some master's and PhD places at Kuwait University.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition**
- A **monthly allowance** (the amount varies by institution)
- **Free or subsidised on-campus housing**, with separate housing for men and women
- **Health insurance**
- **One return flight** each year
- Visa and residency fees

## Host institutions

- Kuwait University
- Abdullah Al-Salem University
- The Public Authority for Applied Education and Training (PAAET)
- The Higher Institute of Musical Arts
- The Higher Institute of Dramatic Arts

## Who can apply

- **Non-Kuwaiti** students
- **Bachelor's applicants:** usually aged **25 or under**, with a secondary school certificate issued in the last two years
- **Strong grades:** usually **80% or above** for science subjects and **85% or above** for arts subjects
- **Language:** most programmes are taught in **Arabic**. Some programmes, such as medicine, engineering and sciences, use English and ask for IELTS (6.0+), TOEFL or a university English test.
- Medically fit, and not a previous Kuwait government scholarship holder

## Two ways to apply

1. **Direct online application** through Kuwait University or the Ministry of Higher Education portal, for applicants who meet the academic requirements.
2. **Embassy nomination** through the **Kuwaiti embassy or consulate** in your home country. This route is mainly for countries with cultural agreements with Kuwait, and places vary each year.

## Documents you usually need

- Application form
- Secondary school certificate and transcripts (or your bachelor's degree for postgraduate study)
- Passport copy
- Medical fitness certificate
- English or Arabic language certificate, if required

## When to apply

The direct online window usually opens in **March** and closes in **May**, for studies starting in **September**. Embassy nomination dates differ by country, so contact the Kuwaiti embassy in your country early.

## Tips

- **Start learning Arabic now** if you want the widest choice of programmes.
- **Contact the Kuwaiti embassy** in your country to ask about this year's nomination places.
- **Keep your documents ready**, including certified copies and a medical certificate.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a Kuwait scholarship.

The **Kyoto University Amgen Scholars Program** gives top science undergraduates from Asia a fully funded summer of hands-on research in Kyoto University laboratories. It is part of the global **Amgen Scholars** programme, which aims to inspire the next generation of scientists and biotechnology innovators.

About **10 students** are selected each year.

## Programme dates

**4 June – 5 August 2027** (about 9 weeks) in Kyoto, Japan, including the **Amgen Scholars Asia Symposium** (attendance is required).

## Research areas

Lab placements in **biosciences, chemistry, pharmacology, neuroscience** and related fields, matched to your interests and available spaces.

## What the programme covers

- A scholarship of **JPY 180,000**, paid in two parts (JPY 90,000 at the start and JPY 90,000 mid-programme)
- An **economy flight** between your nearest airport and Osaka (on a set route and schedule)
- **Accommodation** in Kyoto
- A commuting subsidy if your lab is on the Katsura or Uji campus
- Costs of official programme events, including the symposium

**Not covered:** health insurance. You must arrange your own private health insurance and join Kyoto University's Co-op Liability Insurance when you arrive.

## Who can apply

- You are an **undergraduate at a university in Asia** (Eastern, South-Eastern, Central, Southern or Western Asia; the full list of countries is on the official page)
- You have completed **at least your first year** of undergraduate study by the start of the programme
- You will **not graduate before the programme**, and you will return to your undergraduate studies for at least one more semester or quarter afterwards
- You have a **strong academic record** in science
- You are **interested in a PhD** in the future
- **English:** needed if English is not your first language or your degree is not taught in English. Minimums include **TOEFL iBT 72**, **IELTS 5.5**, Cambridge FCE, TOEIC 1095, TOEIC L&R 785 or TOEIC S&W 310.

Master's and PhD students are **not eligible**.

## How to apply

1. Read the application guidelines and FAQ on the official programme page.
2. Create an account in the **online application system** (open since 1 November 2026).
3. Fill in your personal details and write your **statement of purpose**. You cannot edit your application after you submit it.
4. Enter the contact details of **two recommenders**. They will get an email and must upload their letters by the deadline.
5. Upload proof of English, if needed.
6. If you pass the first screening (mid-February 2027), you will be asked to send a short **video** about your reasons for applying, your enthusiasm for research and your career goals.

Do not contact Kyoto University professors directly about placements. Questions go to **amgenscholars@mail2.adm.kyoto-u.ac.jp**.

## Deadline

**1 February 2027, 3 p.m. Japan time.** Everything, including the recommendation letters, must arrive by then.

## Tips

- In your statement of purpose, name the research areas that excite you and show what research or lab work you have done.
- Ask recommenders early. Their letters must arrive by the same deadline as your application.
- Also consider the **UTokyo Amgen Scholars Program**, which has the same 1 February deadline.

**Technische Universität Berlin (TU Berlin)** is one of Germany's leading technical universities, known for engineering, computer science, natural sciences and planning. Many websites advertise a "fully funded TU Berlin scholarship", but **there is no single full scholarship from the university**. What TU Berlin does offer is a big advantage: **no tuition fees**.

> **The smart plan:** Get admitted to a free programme at TU Berlin, and win a **DAAD** or other external scholarship to cover your living costs.

## No tuition fees
- TU Berlin charges **no tuition fees** for regular bachelor's, master's and PhD programmes, **including for international students**
- You only pay a **semester contribution** (it changes slightly each semester and includes a public transport ticket)
- Exception: continuing-education master's programmes charge fees

## Funding options

### 1. DAAD scholarships (best option)
- Germany's largest scholarship provider for international students
- Many programmes must be applied for **from your home country**, often a year before you start
- Search the DAAD scholarship database for programmes open to your country and subject

### 2. Deutschlandstipendium: €300 a month
- For **high-achieving students**, including **international students**
- Funded jointly by private donors and the German government
- Apply through TU Berlin once you are admitted or enrolled

### 3. German foundation scholarships
- Germany's foundations for gifted students (for example Avicenna, Cusanuswerk, Friedrich Ebert, Heinrich Böll, Konrad Adenauer, Studienstiftung) fund some international master's and PhD students
- Based on grades, social commitment and personality; good German usually helps
- See our guide: [Hanns Seidel Foundation Scholarships 2027](https://scholarfahad.com/scholarships/hanns-seidel-foundation-scholarships-2027-in-germany-for-international-students/)

### 4. For students already at TU Berlin
- **STIBET** scholarships for international degree students (to promote equal opportunities)
- **Elsa Neumann Scholarship** for excellent doctoral candidates (funded by the state of Berlin)
- **DAAD Prize** for outstanding international students

## Tips
- Apply for **DAAD** first. It's your best chance of real funding.
- Budget for **living costs** in Berlin, plus the semester contribution.
- Learning German increases your chances for foundation scholarships and part-time jobs.

**Cyprus International University (CIU)** is a private university in **Nicosia (Lefkoşa), Northern Cyprus**, with many programmes taught in English. It offers **tuition scholarships** to international students, including an automatic-sounding 50% discount for every international undergraduate.

> **Location note:** CIU is in **Northern Cyprus**, not Turkey. It uses a Turkish web address and phone code, and most students travel there through Turkey. Northern Cyprus is recognised as a state only by Turkey, so **check how your home country recognises the degree** before you enrol.

## Scholarships at CIU

### 1. International Student Scholarship: 50%
- **All international undergraduate students** receive a **50% tuition fee scholarship**

### 2. Academic (merit) Scholarship: 50% to 100%
- For prospective students with strong academic results
- Covers **50% to 100%** of tuition, depending on your performance

### 3. Athletic Scholarship
- For students with **national-level sports** performance, members of top sports leagues, or students who will play for a CIU team

### 4. Graduate Scholarship (Master's and PhD)
- For applicants with a bachelor's degree and a **CGPA of 3.50 or higher** (CIU's page mentions CIU graduates; confirm whether it applies to you)

### 5. Progression scholarships
- For current students; ask the Registration Office

## What the scholarships do not cover
These are **tuition discounts only**. You will still pay:
- The rest of your tuition (if your scholarship is less than 100%)
- Accommodation, food and transport
- Health insurance, residence permit and other fees

## How to apply
1. Choose your programme on CIU's website.
2. Apply online as an international student.
3. Ask the admissions office which scholarship you qualify for, **and get the percentage in writing** in your offer letter.
4. Confirm the total yearly cost after the scholarship before you pay any deposit.

## Tips
- Compare costs: a 50% discount on a high fee can still be expensive.
- Check degree recognition with your country's education ministry, especially for professional fields like medicine, law or engineering.

**UNICEF**, the United Nations Children's Fund, offers **paid internships** for students and recent graduates in its offices around the world. Interns work on real projects in areas such as education, health, child protection, communications, data, finance and innovation.

> **No single deadline:** UNICEF posts internships **throughout the year**. Each vacancy has its own closing date, so check the jobs site regularly.

## What you get
- A **monthly stipend** towards your living costs (paid by UNICEF or a partner institution)
- A possible **one-time payment for travel and visa costs**, when funding is available
- Hands-on experience with a UN agency and a strong addition to your CV
- **Full-time or part-time** options; some internships can be done **remotely**

**Duration:** usually **6 to 26 weeks**.

## Who can apply
- At least **18 years old**
- Enrolled in an **undergraduate, master's or PhD** programme, **or** graduated within the **last two years**
- Proficient in at least one of UNICEF's working languages: **English, French or Spanish** (some offices also need the local working language)
- No relatives working in UNICEF in your reporting line
- **All nationalities** can apply

## How to apply
1. Go to the **UNICEF vacancies site** (jobs.unicef.org) and filter for **internships**.
2. Read each vacancy carefully. Requirements and deadlines differ by office and role.
3. **Tailor your profile and CV** to the specific internship.
4. Click **Apply Now** before the vacancy's deadline.
5. Only **shortlisted** candidates are contacted. Don't email UNICEF to ask for an interview.

## Tips
- Apply to internships that match your field of study.
- Check the vacancies site every week. New internships appear all the time.
- Beware of scams: UNICEF never charges fees to apply.

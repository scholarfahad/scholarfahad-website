**Dufil Prima Foods Plc**, makers of Indomie noodles, is offering the **Indomie Scholarship Program 2026** to Nigerian students starting their studies in the **2026/2027 academic session**. The scholarship covers **100% of tuition fees** for the whole programme.

This scholarship is open to **Nigerian students only**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees** for the entire duration of your programme
- **Renewed every year**, as long as you maintain the required grade point average

## Who can apply

- **Nigerian students** entering tertiary education in the **2026/2027 session**
- Admitted to a **public or state-owned institution** in Nigeria:
  - universities
  - polytechnics
  - colleges of education
  - specialised institutions
- **WAEC/NECO and JAMB results** (you can upload them after registering, before the deadline)
- **All courses** are eligible

## How to apply

Applying is **free**.

1. Go to the official **[Indomie Scholarship page](https://www.indomie.ng/arewa/scholarship)**.
2. Click **"I Am a New Applicant"**.
3. **Enter your personal details** and upload a passport photograph.
4. **Upload your JAMB and O'Level results**, or skip this step and add them later by logging in.
5. **Verify your email or phone number** and submit your application.

## Selection process

1. **Shortlisting** of applicants
2. **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** for shortlisted candidates
3. **Physical verification** of successful candidates
4. **Final selection** and award

## Deadline

**30 October 2026.** Register before the deadline. You can finish uploading your results later if needed.

## Tips

- **Register today**, even if your results aren't ready yet.
- **Use an email address and phone number you check often**, so you don't miss your CBT invitation.
- **Prepare for the CBT** with general aptitude and English practice questions.
- **Never pay anyone** to apply for you. The scholarship is completely free.

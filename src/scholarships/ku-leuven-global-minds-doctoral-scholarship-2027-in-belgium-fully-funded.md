The **Global Minds Doctoral Scholarship** at **KU Leuven** in Belgium supports talented researchers from developing countries to do a PhD with a strong development focus. It is a **sandwich PhD**: you split your research between KU Leuven and your home university, and you receive a **KU Leuven doctoral degree**.

## How the sandwich PhD works

- The PhD lasts **4 years** in total.
- The scholarship funds **up to 24 months at KU Leuven**, spread over several stays.
- Your **home university** employs you and funds the time you spend at home.
- You have a **KU Leuven supervisor** and a **co-supervisor at your home university**.

## What the scholarship covers

- At least **EUR 2,400 a month** while you are in Belgium (the full net pay of a research assistant)
- Up to **6 months of preparation** at EUR 1,900 a month, if needed
- **Tuition fees waived**
- **Health, accident and liability insurance** during your stays in Belgium
- **4 return economy flights**

## Eligible countries (from the last call)

**Africa:** Benin, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Guinea, Kenya, Madagascar, Morocco, Mozambique, Niger, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zimbabwe

**Asia:** Cambodia, Indonesia, Palestinian Territories, Philippines, Vietnam

**Latin America:** Bolivia, Cuba, Ecuador, Haiti, Nicaragua, Peru

Check the official KU Leuven page for the 2027 country list, as it can change.

## Who can apply

- You are a **national and resident** of an eligible country
- You are not an EU citizen or long-term EU resident
- You got your **master's degree in February 2017 or later**, with **High Distinction** or the equivalent (lower second-class results are not considered)
- Your **home university** agrees to employ and fund you for the home part of the PhD
- Your research is **development-related**, ideally linked to the Sustainable Development Goals
- You have not previously worked in an EEA country other than Belgium, and you are not already a KU Leuven-funded PhD student
- English: **IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT or paper-based (not ITP), Cambridge CAE or CPE**

You can apply at most twice in total.

## How to apply

1. **Find a KU Leuven supervisor** whose research matches yours, and agree on a research project together.
2. **Find a co-supervisor at your home university** and get your university's written commitment to employ you.
3. **Apply for admission** to a KU Leuven doctoral or predoctoral programme. Aim to be admitted by **November or December 2026**, because admission can take about 2.5 months.
4. **Your KU Leuven supervisor submits the Global Minds scholarship application** online before the deadline.
5. Results are sent by email in **June 2027**.

## Deadline

**4 February 2027, 5 p.m. GMT** (scholarship application, submitted by your KU Leuven supervisor)

## Tips

- Start contacting professors now. Your supervisor must submit the application, so you need them on board early.
- Make the development impact of your research clear. Explain the problem in your country and how your PhD will help solve it.
- Talk to your home university early. Their commitment to employ you is required.

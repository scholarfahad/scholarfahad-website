The **RIKEN Center for Brain Science (CBS)** is one of the world's leading neuroscience institutes, based in **Wako, near Tokyo, Japan**. Every year its **Summer Program** invites students from around the world to do hands-on brain research in RIKEN laboratories.

## Two options

- **Plan A: Internship.** About **2 months** of lab research, **8 June – 5 August 2027**, ending with a final presentation.
- **Plan B: Lecture Course.** **5 days** of lectures, **5–9 July 2027**.

## What is covered

If your **financial support request is approved**, RIKEN covers:
- **Return airfare** (or train fare within Japan)
- **Accommodation**: RIKEN International House for Plan A, or a hotel near Wako-shi Station for Plan B

You first **pay for your own ticket** and are **reimbursed** after the programme, so you need to plan for that cost upfront. Funding is **not guaranteed**. You must request it in your application.

## Who can apply

- Applicants of **any nationality**
- **Beyond high school level** (undergraduates, master's and PhD students, and recent postdocs; most participants are graduate students)
- You must be **enrolled at a university or employed by a company** when you apply and throughout the programme
- You can attend the **entire programme in person**
- Non-Japanese applicants need a valid passport and must arrange their own visa

## Documents you need

- **Internship project choice / description** (pick a RIKEN CBS lab and project)
- **CV**
- **Motivation letter**
- **Letter of recommendation**
- **Letter requesting financial support** (only if you need funding)

## How to apply

1. Visit the RIKEN CBS Summer Program website and look at the list of labs and projects.
2. Choose the project that best matches your interests.
3. Prepare your CV, motivation letter and recommendation letter.
4. Write a financial support request if you need flights and housing covered.
5. Apply online before the deadline.

## Deadline

**8 January 2027** (from recent listings). Confirm on the official RIKEN CBS Summer Program page.

## Tips

- Read about the lab you choose and explain clearly why its research excites you.
- Show any experience with neuroscience, biology, psychology, computing or data analysis.
- If you need funding, explain your situation clearly in your request letter.

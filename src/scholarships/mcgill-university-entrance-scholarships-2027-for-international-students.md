**McGill University** in Montréal, one of Canada's top universities, offers **entrance scholarships** to outstanding students starting their first undergraduate degree, **including international students**.

## Scholarship types

| Award | Value (CAD) | How you're considered |
|---|---|---|
| **One-year entrance scholarship** | $3,000, one year | **Automatic**, based on your admission application |
| **Major entrance scholarship** | $3,000, $5,000 or **$10,000 per year**, renewable for 3–4 years | **Separate application** required |

The $12,000 major scholarship is only for Canadian citizens and permanent residents, so international students can get up to **$10,000 a year**.

You can receive only **one** of McGill's central entrance scholarships. If you qualify for more than one, you get the highest. Some faculties also offer their own entrance awards.

## Who can get it

- A **first-time university student** (you haven't started a degree before)
- Entering a **full-time bachelor's programme** at McGill
- Transfer, mature, part-time, diploma, exchange and visiting students are **not eligible**
- **Major scholarships** look first at **academic achievement**, then at **leadership**. McGill says leadership means more than simply taking part in extracurricular activities.

## Deadlines (applicants from schools outside Canada)

- **Apply for admission to McGill:** 15 January 2027
- **Submit the major entrance scholarship application:** 21 January 2027

The scholarship application opens on 1 October in the Undergraduate Admissions Applicant Portal.

## How to apply

1. Apply for undergraduate admission to McGill before **15 January**.
2. Include all your grades, and send new results as soon as you get them. This is how you're considered for the one-year award.
3. For a **major scholarship**, complete the separate scholarship application in the **Applicant Portal** soon after you apply, and before **21 January**.
4. Describe your leadership roles clearly: what you started, led or changed.

Questions: **scholarships@mcgill.ca**

## Important: not fully funded

These scholarships cover **part** of the cost of studying at McGill. International students also need to pay the rest of tuition, housing and living costs, and show proof of funds for a Canadian study permit.

## Looking for full funding?

The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at McGill** offers **full scholarships** (tuition, housing and a stipend) to young Africans with financial need. The Fall 2027 round is **closed**, and McGill says the next cycle will be announced in **spring 2027**. Follow our WhatsApp channel and we'll post it as soon as it opens.

## Tips

- Leadership matters as much as grades for the major awards. Show real impact, like a project you led or a club you started.
- Apply early. Missing documents can cost you scholarship consideration.
- Be careful of websites asking for payment. McGill says to trust only information on mcgill.ca.

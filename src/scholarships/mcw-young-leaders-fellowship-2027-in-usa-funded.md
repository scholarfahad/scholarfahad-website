The **MCW Global Young Leaders Fellowship** is a one-year leadership programme run by **MCW Global**, a non-profit based in New York. It helps young people from around the world turn an idea for helping their community into a real, working project.

Fellows focus on one of three areas: **education**, **health** or **economic security**.

## Programme structure

### 1. Community needs assessment (June 2027, online)
For about a month, you map and study your own community to understand the problem you want to solve.

### 2. In-person training (20–29 July 2027, USA)
Ten days of workshops, speakers, group work and project planning with young leaders from many countries.

### 3. Project implementation (about 10 months, at home)
You return home and carry out your community project, with support from **MCW alumni mentors** through regular online check-ins.

## What is covered

Based on the last edition (2026, held in New York City and Burlington, Vermont), MCW Global covered:
- **International economy flights**
- **Housing** during the in-person programme
- **Three meals a day**
- Local travel during the programme, including airport transfers

**Not covered:** passport fees, visa fees and costs in your home country (for example, travel to your airport and extra baggage). Check the 2027 terms on the official MCW Global website.

## Who can apply

- Young people from **any country**
- Aged **18 to 26** on **1 June 2027**
- **Fluent in English**, written and spoken
- You show **commitment to your community** and have identified a **local problem** in education, health or economic security
- You can commit to the **whole year**: the June needs assessment, the July training and the project work afterwards
- You have reliable **email and internet** access
- You can get the **visa and travel documents** needed to attend

## How to apply and timeline

1. **Round 1:** complete the online application by **3 December 2026**.
2. **Around 21 December 2026:** first-round decisions.
3. **Round 2** (shortlisted applicants): submit by **8 January 2027**.
4. **3–16 February 2027:** interviews with MCW alumni.
5. **23 February 2027:** acceptances sent.
6. **2 March 2027:** confirm your place.
7. **16 March 2027:** confirm your US visa interview.

## Deadline

**3 December 2026, 11:59 p.m. New York time (EST)**

## Tips

- Describe a **specific** problem in your community, with real facts and numbers if you can.
- Show what you have already done to help, even small steps.
- Explain clearly how the fellowship will help you build a project that lasts.
- Check early whether you can get a US visa in time.

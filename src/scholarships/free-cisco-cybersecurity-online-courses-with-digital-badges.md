**Cisco Networking Academy**, run by the global technology company Cisco, offers **free online cybersecurity courses** that anyone can take, from complete beginners to people who want a career in cybersecurity. The courses are self-paced, and you can earn **digital badges** to show your new skills.

Cybersecurity is one of the fastest-growing career fields in the world, and these courses are a great place to start.

## Free cybersecurity courses

### 1. Introduction to Cybersecurity
**Level:** beginner · **Time:** about 6 hours

Learn what cybersecurity is, the common threats people and organisations face, how to protect yourself online and the career opportunities in the field. No experience needed.

### 2. Endpoint Security
Learn how to secure computers, mobile devices and other "endpoints" connected to a network.

### 3. Network Defense
Learn how to monitor networks, protect them from attacks and evaluate security alerts.

### 4. Cyber Threat Management
Learn about cybersecurity governance, risk management and how organisations respond to threats.

### 5. Ethical Hacker
**Level:** intermediate · **Time:** about 70 hours

Learn penetration testing: how ethical hackers find weaknesses in systems before criminals do.

### 6. Junior Cybersecurity Analyst career path
A guided learning path that combines several courses to prepare you for an entry-level cybersecurity job and for Cisco's cybersecurity certification.

## Benefits

- **Free** courses on the official Cisco Networking Academy platform
- **Self-paced:** learn whenever it suits you
- **Digital badges** you can share on LinkedIn and your CV

## Who can join

- **Everyone**, from any country
- Students, graduates, career changers and professionals
- No previous experience needed for beginner courses
- You only need an internet connection and a free account

## How to enrol

1. Go to **[netacad.com](https://www.netacad.com)** and create a free account.
2. Search the course catalogue for **"cybersecurity"**.
3. Choose a course. If you're new, start with **Introduction to Cybersecurity**.
4. Click **Enroll** and start learning at your own pace.
5. Complete the course and pass the final assessment to earn your **badge**.

## Suggested learning order

1. Introduction to Cybersecurity
2. Endpoint Security
3. Network Defense
4. Cyber Threat Management
5. Ethical Hacker (once you have the basics)

Or follow the **Junior Cybersecurity Analyst** career path for a complete, guided programme.

## Tips

- **Study a little every day.** Even 30 minutes adds up quickly.
- **Do the labs.** Hands-on practice is what employers value most.
- **Share your badges on LinkedIn** to show your skills to employers and scholarship committees.
- The courses are free on netacad.com. Never pay anyone to "register" you for them.

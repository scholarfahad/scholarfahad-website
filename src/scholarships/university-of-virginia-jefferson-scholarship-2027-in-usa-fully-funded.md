The **Jefferson Scholarship**, awarded by the Jefferson Scholars Foundation, is one of the most generous undergraduate scholarships in the United States. It covers the **full cost of studying at the University of Virginia (UVA)** for four years, and **you don't need to be a US citizen** to be considered.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition and fees**
- **Housing and food**
- **Books and materials**
- **Personal and travel expenses**
- Enrichment funding for **international travel, research and career development**

**Value:** over **US$83,000 a year** (more than US$405,000 over four years) for students from outside Virginia.

## Who can apply

- Students in their **final year of secondary or high school**, applying to start a bachelor's degree at UVA in fall 2027
- **All nationalities.** US citizenship is not required.
- Outstanding achievement in **leadership, scholarship and citizenship**
- Financial need is **not** considered, and SAT, ACT, AP and IB scores are not used in the scholarship selection

## How the nomination works

You **cannot apply on your own**. You must be nominated by your school.

1. **Talk to your school counsellor or principal** as early as possible and ask them to nominate you for the Jefferson Scholarship.
2. **Your counsellor submits** your basic details, school information, one recommendation letter, your transcript and the school profile.
3. **You submit** your contact details, activities, awards and **two essays** (a personal statement and a leadership essay).
4. **Apply to UVA for admission** separately through the Office of Undergraduate Admission. You must be admitted to UVA to receive the scholarship.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Counsellor and nominee materials due | **1 December 2026** |
| Nominees told of their status | By the end of January 2027 |
| Regional interviews | Mid-December 2026 to January 2027 |
| Final virtual interviews | Late February 2027 |
| Studies begin | Fall 2027 |

## Tips for a strong application

- **Start the conversation with your school now.** Your counsellor needs time to prepare the nomination before 1 December.
- **Show real leadership.** Describe projects you started, groups you led and the impact you made.
- **Write honest, personal essays.** Show who you are, what you care about and how you serve others.
- **Apply to UVA on time.** The scholarship only works if you are admitted.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a Jefferson Scholarship.

The **France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship** is the French government's flagship scholarship for top international students. It is run by the Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs through **Campus France**, and supports master's and PhD students at French universities and schools.

> **Important:** Students **cannot** apply to Campus France directly. You apply through the **French university or school** you want to join, and it submits your file. Each university has its own deadline, often in **November or December**, well before Campus France's **6 January 2027** deadline.

## What the scholarship covers
- **Master's:** **€1,200 a month**
- **PhD:** **€2,100 a month**
- International return travel
- Some travel within France
- Health insurance
- Help finding accommodation
- Cultural activities

**Not covered:** tuition fees. Public universities in France charge relatively low fees, but private schools can be expensive, so check before you apply.

## How long it lasts
- **Master's:** up to 12 months for a Master 2, 24 months for a Master 1, and 36 months for an engineering degree
- **PhD:** depends on the project. Mobility of 12 or 18 months must be part of a **joint or co-supervised thesis** (cotutelle or codirection). Some universities also offer a longer option. Check with the university.

## Who can apply
- **Foreign nationals** of any country, including Nigerians. French dual nationals are not eligible.
- **Age:** up to about **29 for master's** and **35 for PhD** (for the 2027 call, PhD applicants must be born after 31 March 1991)
- You must not already hold another French government scholarship
- Master's applicants should not already be studying in France
- You must meet the language level your programme requires (many programmes are taught in English)

## Eligible fields
**Sciences and technology**
- Biology and health
- Ecological transition
- Mathematics and digital
- Engineering sciences

**Humanities and social sciences**
- History, French language and civilisation
- Law and political science
- Economics and management

## How to apply
1. **Choose a master's or PhD programme** at a French university or school in an eligible field.
2. **Contact the programme or the international office** to ask about its Eiffel process and **internal deadline**.
3. **Apply for admission** and the Eiffel nomination through the university.
4. If the university selects you, it sends your complete file to **Campus France** by **6 January 2027**.
5. **Results** are announced in the **week of 12 April 2027**.

## Tips
- Start early. Many universities choose their Eiffel candidates in November.
- Apply to programmes where your profile is strong. The university only nominates its best candidates.
- For a PhD, find a French supervisor first; you will usually need their support for the Eiffel nomination.

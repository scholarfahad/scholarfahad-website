The **Murtala Muhammed Foundation (MMF)**, set up in memory of Nigeria's former Head of State General Murtala Muhammed, runs a STEM scholarship programme with the **Africa Science Academy** for talented young African women. The scholarship supports girls from low-income backgrounds who want to build careers in **science, technology, engineering and mathematics**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full university scholarship** for a **four-year degree**
- **Tuition**, **accommodation** and **living expenses**
- A **dedicated mentor** from the STEM field
- An **annual paid internship**
- An **annual leadership summer school**

## Who can apply

- A **female African** student
- Aged **16 to 19**
- Have completed **Senior Secondary School, IGCSE, O-Levels** or an equivalent
- Strong results in **Physics and Mathematics** (A or B grades in WAEC or equivalent); Further Mathematics and English are also important
- A real **passion for STEM**
- Priority for girls from **low-income backgrounds**

## Documents you need

1. Completed application form
2. Academic transcripts or results
3. A recent passport photograph
4. Personal essays
5. A recommendation letter from your Maths or Science teacher (optional)

## How to apply

1. Fill in the official application form.
2. Upload your results, passport photograph and essays.
3. Add a recommendation letter from a Maths or Science teacher if you have one.
4. Submit before **30 November 2026**.

## Deadline

**30 November 2026**

## Tips

- **Write honest, personal essays.** Explain your love for science and the challenges you've faced.
- **Show your achievements** in Maths and Physics, such as competitions, clubs or projects.
- **Ask a teacher early** for a recommendation letter.
- **Apply only through the official form**, and never pay anyone to apply for you.

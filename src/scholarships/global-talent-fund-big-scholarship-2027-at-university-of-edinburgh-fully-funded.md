The **Global Talent Fund BIG (Backing Invisible Geniuses) Scholarship** supports exceptionally talented young mathematicians and scientists to study at the **University of Edinburgh** in Scotland. It is funded by the **Global Talent Fund**, with a founding donation from **XTX Markets**, and works with the UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees**
- Support with **living costs**
- A **stipend** for academic activities outside the classroom
- Social events, networking, **internships** and **research placements**

## Eligible degrees (School of Mathematics)

- BSc Mathematics
- BSc Applied Mathematics
- BSc Mathematics and Statistics
- MMath Mathematics (direct entry to year 2 only)
- MMath Applied Mathematics (direct entry to year 2 only)

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- You won a **gold, silver or bronze medal** at one of these:
  - **International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)**
  - **International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI)**
  - **International Physics Olympiad (IPhO)**
  - **Romanian Master of Mathematics (RMM)**
- **Or** a **gold or silver medal** at the **European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad (EGMO)**
- You are **accepted for full-time study** on one of the eligible degrees

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to an eligible University of Edinburgh mathematics degree.
2. **Log in to MyEd**, the university's web portal (access can take a few working days after you apply).
3. Complete the **scholarship application in EUCLID** before the deadline.

## Deadline

Last year's deadline was **31 March, 23:59 UK time**. Expect the same date, **31 March 2027**, for September 2027 entry. Check the official page for confirmation.

## Other Edinburgh maths scholarships

International students accepted to an Edinburgh maths degree can also apply for the **Edinburgh Global Undergraduate Mathematics Scholarship** (**GBP 5,000**, deadline 31 March 2027). You don't need an olympiad medal for that one.

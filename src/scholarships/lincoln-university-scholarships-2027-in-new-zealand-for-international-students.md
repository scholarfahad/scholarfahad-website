Lincoln University is New Zealand's specialist university for agriculture, environment, business, tourism and food science, based near Christchurch. It offers several scholarships for international students starting a bachelor's or master's degree.

> **Important:** These scholarships **reduce your tuition fees**. They are **not fully funded**, so you will still need to pay the rest of your fees and your living costs, and show funds for your New Zealand student visa.

## Scholarships for international students

### 1. International School Leavers Scholarship: NZ$10,000
- **For:** new international students coming straight from secondary school (in New Zealand or overseas) into a bachelor's degree
- **Value:** NZ$10,000, credited to your tuition account after you enrol
- **Number:** up to 10 each year
- **Selection:** academic merit
- **How to apply:** email the signed application form, a statement of purpose and your offer letter to international@lincoln.ac.nz
- [Official page](https://www.lincoln.ac.nz/study/scholarships/search-scholarships/lincoln-university-international-school-leavers-scholarship/)

### 2. Taught Master Merit Scholarship: NZ$7,000 or NZ$10,000
- **For:** international students enrolling in a full 180-credit taught master's
- **Value:** NZ$10,000 for programmes such as the Master of Business (Finance) and Master of Tourism Management; NZ$7,000 for programmes such as the Master of Applied Computing and Master of Wine and Viticulture
- **Requirement:** at least a **B+ average** in the final year of your bachelor's degree
- **How to apply:** **no separate application**. Lincoln assesses you automatically when you apply for admission.
- [Official page](https://www.lincoln.ac.nz/study/scholarships/search-scholarships/lincoln-university-180-credit-taught-master-merit-scholarship/)

### 3. International Undergraduate Scholarship: NZ$3,000
- **For:** new international students starting a bachelor's degree
- [Official page](https://www.lincoln.ac.nz/study/scholarships/search-scholarships/lincoln-university-international-undergraduate-scholarship/)

### 4. International Pathway Merit Scholarship: NZ$2,500
- **For:** new international students starting the Pathway Certificate (Foundation) or First Year Pathway Diploma
- **Value:** NZ$2,500 off tuition
- [Official page](https://www.lincoln.ac.nz/study/scholarships/search-scholarships/lincoln-university-international-pathway-merit-scholarship/)

### 5. English for Further Study Award
- Complete at least **12 weeks** of English for Academic Purposes at Lincoln and get a **fee rebate** when you start your next academic programme.

## Who can apply
- International students who have not studied in an academic programme at Lincoln before
- You must meet the academic and English requirements for your programme
- You must meet New Zealand student visa requirements
- In most cases you **cannot hold another scholarship** at the same time

## Deadlines
- **31 January 2027:** for a February 2027 (Semester 1) start
- **30 June 2027:** for a July 2027 (Semester 2) start
- **31 October:** for a November (Summer School) start

## How to apply
1. Apply for admission to your chosen programme at Lincoln University.
2. Once you have your **offer letter**, download the scholarship application form from Lincoln's scholarships page.
3. Email the signed form, your **statement of purpose** and your offer letter to **international@lincoln.ac.nz** before the deadline.
4. For the Taught Master Merit Scholarship, you do not need to apply separately.

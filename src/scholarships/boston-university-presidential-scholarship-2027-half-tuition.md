Boston University (BU), one of the leading private research universities in the United States, offers the **Presidential Scholarship** to outstanding first-year students. The merit award covers **half of undergraduate tuition** every year, and international students are eligible.

There is no separate scholarship application. Every first-year applicant who completes their BU application by **1 December** is considered automatically.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Half of BU tuition** each year, about US$36,500 a year based on 2026–27 tuition of US$73,024
- Renewable for up to **four years** of undergraduate study, as long as you stay in good academic standing

The scholarship does not cover housing, food, fees or personal costs, which come to more than US$20,000 a year. International students are not eligible for BU's need-based financial aid, so plan how you will cover the remaining costs.

## Who can apply

- Students applying for **first-year (freshman) undergraduate** admission for fall 2027
- Open to **all nationalities**: US citizens, permanent residents and international students
- Excellent academic results
- Strong leadership and involvement in your school or community

There is no published minimum GPA or test score. The scholarship is highly competitive and goes to a small number of top applicants.

## How to apply

1. **Apply to Boston University** for first-year admission through the [Common Application](https://www.commonapp.org).
2. **Submit everything by 1 December 2026:** your application, school transcripts, recommendation letters, school report and any other documents BU asks for.
3. **International applicants:** include proof of English proficiency (such as TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo) if English is not your first language, and check BU's current testing policy for the SAT or ACT.
4. **Wait for your decision.** Scholarship recipients are told alongside their admission results in spring.

You can apply through Regular Decision or Early Decision, but your complete application must be in by 1 December to be considered for the Presidential Scholarship.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Show leadership with impact.** Describe what you started, led or changed, not just the positions you held.
- **Make your essays personal.** Show who you are and why BU fits your goals.
- **Ask for strong recommendations** from teachers who know your work well.
- **Submit early.** Don't let a missing transcript or recommendation make you miss the 1 December deadline.
- **Plan your full budget.** Look for other external scholarships to cover housing and living costs.
- Applying for the scholarship is free. You only pay BU's application fee, or request a fee waiver if you qualify.

The University of Pavia, founded in 1361, is one of Italy's oldest universities, with many bachelor's and master's programmes taught in **English**. International students who are admitted can apply for the **EDiSU scholarship**, a need-based award that can cover your tuition, a room, a daily meal and a yearly cash grant.

> **Two steps:** First apply for **admission** (the first window is usually in **mid-November**). Then apply for the **EDiSU scholarship** in the summer (**August or September**). Start collecting your family income documents now.

## What the EDiSU scholarship gives you
EDiSU Pavia is the University of Pavia's agency for student support. Its scholarship includes a **tuition fee waiver**, plus one of these packages (2026/27 values):

**Option 1: room + meal + cash**
- A free room in a university **Collegio** (residence)
- One free meal a day in the university canteen
- Up to **€4,479** a year

**Option 2: meal + cash (no room)**
- One free meal a day
- Up to **€7,419** a year

Students who don't meet the income limits can still apply for **accommodation only**.

How payment works: rooms are usually assigned in late September or October, free meals start in January, and the cash is paid once you have earned the required credits.

## Who can apply
- International students of **any nationality**, including Nigerians
- Admitted to a **bachelor's or master's** degree at the University of Pavia
- You must meet the **income** and **merit** requirements
- Students from countries on Italy's list of "particularly poor countries" need a statement from the **Italian embassy or consulate** in their home country instead of full income documents

## Key dates (based on last year)

**Admission for non-EU applicants living abroad (non-restricted programmes):**
- **1st window:** mid-November (last year: 12–20 November 2025)
- **2nd window:** mid-January (last year: 12–20 January 2026)
- 3rd and 4th windows (March and June): only if places remain

**EDiSU scholarship:**
- With a room: early **August** (last year: 4 August 2026)
- Without a room: mid-**September** (last year: 15 September 2026)

Restricted-access programmes (for example Medicine) have separate calls, usually from December or January.

## How to apply
1. **Choose your programme** and apply for admission on the University of Pavia's application portal during an open window.
2. **Prepare your income documents** (or the Italian embassy statement if your country is on the poorer-countries list). This can take months.
3. Once admitted, **apply for the EDiSU scholarship** on EDiSU's online student services before the deadline, selecting all the benefits you want.
4. Arrive, enrol, and keep earning your credits to receive the cash part.

## Other funding at Pavia
- **Programme scholarships**, for example for the M.Sc. in Civil Engineering for Mitigation of Risk from Natural Hazards and the Environmental Engineering REACH curriculum
- **Italian Government (MAECI) Study in Italy scholarships** for foreign students

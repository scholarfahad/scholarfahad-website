The **AYFN Winter Japan Culture Camp 2027** is a 6-day cultural exchange in Japan, organised by the ASEAN Youth Friendship Network (AYFN). Young people from around the world spend a week living together in Osaka. They explore the culture, food and history of Osaka, Kyoto and Nara.

The camp is open to **all nationalities**, and you don't need to speak Japanese, because the programme runs in English.

## Camp dates

- **25–30 January 2027**
- Based in **Osaka**, with trips to **Kyoto** and **Nara**

## What the fully funded scholarship covers

- Flight support of up to **USD 750**
- Visa fee support of up to **USD 50**
- Programme fee
- Accommodation and meals
- Activities and tours, including a heritage walking tour of Kyoto
- Local transport
- A pre-departure Japanese language class
- Souvenirs and a certificate of participation

Fully funded places are limited and competitive. AYFN also offers self-funded and partially funded places. Read the official page carefully to see which category you are applying for and whether any fee applies.

## Who can apply

- Any nationality
- Aged **17 to 40**
- Strong interest in travel and culture
- Active in social, community or youth activities
- Leadership experience
- Good spoken and written English (IELTS is not required)
- Able to travel to Japan from 25 to 30 January 2027
- In good physical and mental health

## Documents (all in English)

- **Europass CV**
- **Motivation letter** (maximum 250 words)
- **Essay** (maximum 250 words)
- **Self-introduction video**

## How to apply

1. Create your Europass CV at europass.europa.eu.
2. Write your motivation letter and essay. Keep each under 250 words.
3. Record a short self-introduction video. Speak clearly and say why you want to join.
4. Fill in the online application form on the AYFN website and upload your documents.

## Deadline

**10 November 2026.** Apply early, as places are competitive.

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, show real interest in Japanese culture and give an example of your youth or community work.
- Keep your video short, friendly and confident.
- Check your passport is valid and start thinking about the visa early, because the camp is in January.

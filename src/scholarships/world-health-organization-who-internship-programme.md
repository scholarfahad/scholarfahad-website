The **World Health Organization (WHO)** offers internships for students and recent graduates who want hands-on experience in global public health. Interns work in WHO's technical and administrative teams at its **headquarters in Geneva**, at **regional offices** (including the African Regional Office) and at **country offices** around the world.

> **No single deadline:** WHO posts internship vacancies **all year round**. Each vacancy has its own requirements and closing date, so check the WHO Careers site often.

## What interns get

- A **living allowance** for interns who need financial support. The amount depends on the duty station and is set after selection, based on a financial declaration. As an example, a recent Geneva vacancy offered up to **CHF 1,680 a month** for interns whose permanent home is outside Geneva.
- **Medical and accident insurance** for the length of the internship
- Experience at the world's leading public health organisation, and a strong addition to your CV

**Please note:** interns pay their **own travel** to and from the duty station, and there's no salary. In Geneva, accommodation is expensive and hard to find, so plan ahead.

## Internship length

**6 to 24 weeks**, full time, depending on the needs of the team.

## Who can apply

- **Aged 20 or older** on the date you apply
- **Enrolled in a university course** leading to a formal qualification, **or** a graduate who finished **within the last 18 months**
- **Fluent in at least one working language** of the office you apply to
- Many vacancies also require **at least three years of completed full-time university study**, so read each vacancy carefully

## How to apply

1. Go to the **WHO Careers** website and open the list of **internship vacancies**.
2. Choose a vacancy that matches your studies and the **office location** you're interested in.
3. Create a profile on WHO's recruitment system and **apply online** before that vacancy's deadline. WHO only accepts applications through vacancy notices.
4. If you're selected, WHO will ask you to complete a financial declaration to work out your living allowance.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Apply for roles that match your field**, such as public health, medicine, nursing, data, communications, law or administration.
- **Use your motivation statement** to explain what you'll contribute and what you want to learn, and name the unit's work specifically.
- **Check often.** New internships appear regularly, and some close quickly.
- **Consider country and regional offices**, not just Geneva. Living costs are lower, and they may be closer to home.
- **Beware of scams.** WHO never charges application fees. Apply only through the official WHO Careers website.

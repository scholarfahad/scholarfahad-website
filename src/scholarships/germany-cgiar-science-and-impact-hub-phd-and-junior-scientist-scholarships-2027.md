The **Germany–CGIAR++ Science and Impact Hub** connects German universities with the world's leading agricultural research centres (CGIAR, the World Vegetable Center and icipe). It is commissioned by Germany's **Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ)** through **GIZ**, and offers scholarships for **PhD researchers** and **junior scientists** in agricultural sciences. You do not need to be German.

> **Deadlines:** PhD scholarship: **30 November 2026** · Junior Scientists Program: **15 November 2026** (then every three months)

## 1. PhD Research Scholarship
**What you get** (based on the Hub's previous call):
- **€1,800 a month** for **three years**
- A possible **12-month extension** if you are close to finishing
- Up to **€25,000** in research funding for your PhD project
- Courses and seminars in the Hub's science support programme

**Who can apply:**
- A **master's degree** in agricultural sciences or a related field, with an **above-average final grade**
- You must **enrol in a PhD at a German university**
- Your project must be jointly supervised by a **German university professor** and a **CGIAR++ senior scientist**, and hosted by a CGIAR++ centre (the Hub calls this team a "tridem")
- English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS or equivalent)
- **Any nationality**; applicants from CGIAR++ partner countries are welcome

**Documents:** motivation letter, CV, research proposal on the Hub template, MSc certificate and transcripts (non-European degrees need a class ranking), English proof, letters from both supervisors, and a hosting agreement from the CGIAR++ centre.

## 2. Junior Scientists Program (JSP)
**What you get:**
- **€1,000 a month** for a **six-month field research stay** (€6,000 in total) at a CGIAR++ centre, the World Vegetable Center or icipe, usually in the Global South
- Courses and seminars

**Who can apply:**
- Students on a **master's in agricultural sciences** (or related) at a **German university**, or recent master's graduates not yet on a PhD
- Very good English
- A hosting confirmation from a CGIAR++ centre and a recommendation from your German supervisor

**Deadlines:** applications are accepted all year and reviewed **quarterly**. The next deadline is **15 November 2026**.

## How to apply
1. **Find your supervisors:** a professor at a German university and a scientist at a CGIAR++ centre who agree to work with you.
2. **Write your proposal** using the Hub's template.
3. **Submit on the Hub's online portal** before the deadline. Supervisor documents go by email to hub@landscapealliance.org.

> **Important:** The Hub does **not allow any use of artificial intelligence** to prepare your proposal.

The \*\*University of Global Health Equity (UGHE)\*\* in Rwanda, together with the \*\*Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program\*\*, offers fully funded scholarships to young Africans for its \*\*Master of Science in Global Health Delivery (MGHD)\*\*. The programme trains health leaders who can deliver better care in their own communities.

\> \*\*Key dates:\*\* applications open on \*\*14 December 2026\*\* and close on \*\*31 January 2027 at 5:00 PM CAT\*\* (Central Africa Time). Check the official UGHE admissions page for any changes.

\## About the programme

The MGHD is an \*\*18-month, full-time\*\* master's degree. It begins with an online semester, followed by in-person study at UGHE's campus in Rwanda. Students choose one of four tracks:

\- \*\*Global Surgery\*\*

\- \*\*Health Management\*\*

\- \*\*One Health\*\*

\- \*\*Social and Community Medicine\*\*

\## What the scholarship covers

Mastercard Foundation Scholars receive:

\- \*\*Full tuition\*\* and academic fees

\- \*\*On-campus accommodation and meals\*\*

\- A \*\*monthly stipend\*\* for personal and transport costs

\- \*\*Health insurance\*\*

\- A \*\*laptop\*\* and study materials

\- \*\*Return flights and visa support\*\* for international scholars

\- \*\*Mentorship\*\*, plus leadership, entrepreneurship and innovation training

Admission doesn't guarantee a scholarship. Awards are competitive.

\## Who can apply

\- \*\*Citizens and residents of an African country\*\*, or people with \*\*refugee status\*\* in an African country

\- \*\*Age 35 or under\*\* at the time of application (exceptions for applicants with disabilities)

\- A \*\*completed bachelor's degree\*\* in any field

\- \*\*No master's degree already.\*\* Applicants who hold one aren't eligible.

\- Good \*\*English\*\* for reading, writing and speaking

\- Basic \*\*analytical skills\*\* for subjects like biostatistics and epidemiology

\## Documents you'll need

\- \*\*Passport\*\* (non-Rwandans), \*\*national ID\*\* (Rwandans) or \*\*refugee ID\*\*

\- \*\*Academic transcripts\*\* and \*\*degree certificate\*\*

\- \*\*Two recommendation letters\*\*

\- A \*\*short CV\*\* in PDF format

If your degree is from outside Rwanda, you may need an equivalence certificate from Rwanda's Higher Education Council, but only \*\*after\*\* you're admitted.

\## How to apply

1\. Go to UGHE's admissions page when applications open on 14 December 2026.

2\. Fill in the \*\*online application\*\*. The \*\*scholarship application is part of the same form\*\*, so there's nothing separate to submit.

3\. Submit \*\*one complete application only\*\*. UGHE won't consider incomplete or duplicate applications.

\## Also at UGHE: Master's in Health Professions Education

UGHE also offers Mastercard Foundation scholarships for a \*\*Master of Science in Health Professions Education (HPE)\*\*, for people with a health-related bachelor's degree who teach or want to teach health workers. Its last round closed on 30 November 2025, so watch UGHE's website for the next one.

\## Tips for a strong application

\- \*\*Show your commitment to Africa's health.\*\* Describe a real health problem in your community and how you want to help solve it.

\- \*\*Prepare for the admissions exam\*\* by refreshing basic maths and statistics.

\- \*\*Ask your referees early\*\* so their letters are ready before 31 January.

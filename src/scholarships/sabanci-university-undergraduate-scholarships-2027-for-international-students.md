**Sabancı University** in Istanbul is one of Türkiye's top private research universities, teaching in **English**. It offers **admission scholarships** to talented international students starting a bachelor's degree. You're considered automatically when you apply.

> **Apply early:** Sabancı gives **priority to early applicants**. Last year, applications opened on **1 January** with an **early deadline of 14 March** and a final deadline of **28 August**. Expect similar dates for 2027.

## How the scholarships work
- **No separate application.** Eligible applicants are **considered automatically** (you can also tick the scholarship option on the form)
- Once awarded, your scholarship **lasts for your entire regular degree**
- Scholarship numbers are **limited**, and Sabancı doesn't publish fixed amounts
- After at least two semesters, strong students can also apply for **in-study scholarships**

**Tuition without a scholarship:** **US$36,500 a year** (2026/27).

## What the Scholarship Committee looks at
- Your **high school grades** and transcripts
- **Exam results** accepted by Sabancı
- **Reference letters**
- **Interview** results (if invited)
- **Achievements**: Olympiad certificates, competition awards and similar

**Tip:** Upload every award and certificate you have. It can make the difference.

## Documents
**Required:**
- Exam or diploma results accepted by Sabancı
- Official high school transcript (signed and stamped)

**Optional, but recommended:**
- High school diploma (notarised translation if not in English or Turkish)
- English test scores (or take Sabancı's English exam at registration)
- Motivation letter and recommendation letter
- Extracurricular certificates

## How to apply
1. Fill in the **online application** and pay the **US$30** fee.
2. Upload your documents (soft copies).
3. Apply **before the early deadline** for the best scholarship chances.

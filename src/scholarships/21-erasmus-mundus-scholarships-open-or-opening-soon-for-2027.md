Planning a fully funded master's degree in Europe? These **21 Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's programmes** are open, or opening soon, for the **2027 intake**. They're sorted by deadline so you can see what to prepare first.

Erasmus Mundus scholarships generally cover tuition, a monthly allowance and insurance, and you can apply to up to three programmes in the same year. Read more in our [Erasmus Mundus overview](/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters/).

> **Always confirm dates on the official page.** Deadlines can change, and requirements differ for every programme.

## Start preparing now

Most Erasmus Mundus programmes ask for similar documents, so you can prepare them before the deadlines arrive:

- **CV** (often in Europass format)
- **Motivation letter**, written separately for each programme
- **Two reference letters**; ask your referees early
- **Degree certificate and transcripts**, with certified English translations if needed
- **English test result** (IELTS, TOEFL or similar)
- **Passport** copy

Our guide on [how to write a motivation letter](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/) will help you get started, and you can [book a review](/services/) of your documents before you submit.

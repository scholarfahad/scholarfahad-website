**Chalmers University of Technology** in Gothenburg is one of Sweden's leading universities for engineering, technology and science. Chalmers offers two scholarships for international master's students from outside the EU/EEA who have to pay tuition fees in Sweden.

## Scholarships available

### Adlerbert Study Scholarship

- Covers **100% of tuition fees** for the two-year master's programme (four semesters)
- For citizens of **143 eligible countries** (Chalmers publishes the list on its scholarship page)
- **4 scholarships** awarded

### IPOET Scholarship

- Covers **75% of tuition fees** for the two-year master's programme
- Students who do very well in their first year may get an **85% reduction** in their second year
- About **35 scholarships** awarded

Both scholarships cover tuition only. They do not include a living allowance, so you'll need to plan how to pay your living costs in Sweden. Non-EU students also need to show proof of funds for the residence permit.

## Who can apply

- Citizens of **non-EU/EEA countries** who must pay tuition fees in Sweden
- Applying for the **first year** of any Chalmers master's programme starting in August 2027
- An excellent bachelor's degree record

## How selection works

Scholarships are awarded mainly on academic excellence, including:

- Your weighted average grade
- The standing of your home university, including global rankings
- The priority order of your Chalmers programme choices on universityadmissions.se (put Chalmers first to improve your chances)

## How to apply

1. **Apply to at least one Chalmers master's programme** on [universityadmissions.se](https://www.universityadmissions.se) between 16 October 2026 and 15 January 2027.
2. **Note your application number** from universityadmissions.se.
3. **Submit the Chalmers scholarship application** through the official scholarship page, using your application number, by 15 January 2027. The scholarship form usually opens in late November.
4. **Upload your documents** (degree certificate, transcripts, English test results and passport) to universityadmissions.se by the document deadline, usually early February.
5. **Pay the application fee** (SEK 900) on universityadmissions.se, unless you're exempt.

## Tips

- **Rank Chalmers as your first choice**, because the priority order of your choices counts in selection.
- **Check the Adlerbert country list** to see whether you qualify for the full tuition scholarship.
- **Also apply for the Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP)**, which covers tuition and living costs. Its application usually runs in February.
- **Plan your living costs**, since these scholarships don't include a stipend.
- Applying for Chalmers scholarships is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed award.

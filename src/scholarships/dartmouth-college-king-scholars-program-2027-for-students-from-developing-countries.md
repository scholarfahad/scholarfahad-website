The **King Scholars Program** at **Dartmouth College**, an Ivy League university in New Hampshire, USA, supports talented **low-income students from developing countries** who want to help reduce poverty in their home countries. King Scholars receive a **fully funded four-year bachelor's degree**, plus mentoring and leadership training.

> **Deadlines:** Early Decision **1 November 2026** · Regular Decision **1 January 2027** (financial aid documents by **1 February 2027**)

## What King Scholars receive
- **Four years of full financial aid:** tuition, room and board
- **Health insurance**
- **A new computer** in the first year
- **One trip a year** between Dartmouth and home
- Pre-orientation support and ongoing **academic mentoring**
- **Leadership training** focused on fighting poverty
- Lifelong access to an **international alumni network**

## Who it's for
- **International students from developing countries**
- From **low-income backgrounds**
- Interested in **alleviating poverty** and addressing social issues in their home countries
- Strong academic record and leadership potential

## How to apply
1. **Apply to Dartmouth** as a first-year student through the **Common App**.
   - Early Decision: **1 November 2026** (binding if admitted)
   - Regular Decision: **1 January 2027**
2. **Apply for financial aid** by the deadline (Early Decision: 1 November; Regular Decision: 1 February 2027).
3. Submit your **SAT or ACT** scores (Dartmouth offers extra testing options for students at schools outside the US).
4. In your essays and activities, make your **commitment to reducing poverty** in your community clear.

## Tips
- Dartmouth is highly selective. Your essays should tell a specific, honest story about the problems you've seen and what you've already done about them.
- Early Decision is binding: only choose it if Dartmouth is your clear first choice.
- Ask teachers for recommendation letters early.

The **University of Dayton**, a private Catholic university in Ohio, USA, offers **merit scholarships of up to $34,000 per year** to international undergraduate students.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Up to $34,000 per year** toward tuition
- The **majority of international undergraduates** receive a merit scholarship when they're admitted
- **No separate application.** You're considered automatically once your application and documents are received.

## What it costs (2026–27)

| Item | Per year |
|---|---|
| Tuition | $51,910 |
| Housing and meals (estimate) | $17,990 |
| Other expenses (estimate) | $4,320 |
| **Total before scholarship** | **about $74,220** |

Even with the top award of $34,000, you'd still need **about $40,000 per year**. International students must show **proof of funds** for the remaining costs to get the I-20 for a US student visa.

## Other support

- **Organization of American States (OAS) loans:** interest-free loans of up to $15,000 for students from OAS member countries
- On-campus jobs and a UD payment plan

## Who can apply

- International students applying for an **undergraduate degree** at the University of Dayton
- Strong academic results. Scholarship amounts are based on merit.
- Students whose first language isn't English must send proof of English proficiency

## Deadlines

- **Spring 2027 start:** 1 November 2026
- **Fall 2027 start:** 1 May 2027

## How to apply

1. Apply online on the University of Dayton website (application fee: **$50**): https://udayton.edu/apply/international/undergraduate/index.php
2. Send your **official transcripts** and **English test scores** (TOEFL code 1834, ACT code 3342).
3. Submit proof of financial support.
4. You'll be considered for a merit scholarship automatically.

## Tips

- Strong grades mean a bigger scholarship, so send your best results.
- Apply early. The earlier you apply, the more time you have to plan the remaining costs.
- Combine this with outside scholarships to reduce what you pay.
- Never pay agents for "guaranteed" scholarships.

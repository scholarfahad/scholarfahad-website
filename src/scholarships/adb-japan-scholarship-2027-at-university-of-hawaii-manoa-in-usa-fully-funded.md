The **Asian Development Bank–Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP)** gives professionals from developing countries in **Asia and the Pacific** the chance to study a fully funded **master's degree** at the **University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa** in Honolulu, USA. The programme is funded by the **Government of Japan**, managed by the **Asian Development Bank (ADB)**, and run in Hawaiʻi by the **East-West Center**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition and fees**
- **Monthly living stipend**
- **Housing** at the East-West Center
- **Round-trip travel** between your home country and Honolulu
- **Textbook allowance**
- **Health insurance**
- **J-1 visa sponsorship**
- Leadership and community programmes at the East-West Center

**Duration:** usually 18 to 24 months, depending on the programme

## Eligible master's programmes

- Business Administration (Global MBA)
- Economics
- Geography
- Natural Resources and Environmental Management
- Ocean and Resources Engineering
- Oceanography
- Pacific Islands Studies
- Public Administration
- Sociology
- Tropical Plant and Soil Sciences
- Urban and Regional Planning

## Who can apply

- You are a **citizen and current resident** of an **ADB developing member country** (in Asia and the Pacific)
- You are **35 years old or younger** when you apply
- You have a **4-year bachelor's degree** (or equivalent) with a strong academic record
- You have at least **2 years of full-time professional work** after your degree
- English: for example **TOEFL iBT 79–80, IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 95**
- You do not already have, and are not studying for, a master's degree
- You plan to **return home** after your studies to help your country's development

You are **not eligible** if you currently live or work outside your home country, or if you have previously studied outside ADB developing member countries.

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to one of the eligible master's programmes at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, and keep proof that you applied.
2. **Complete the ADB-JSP online application** on the East-West Center's Submittable page.
3. **Upload** your CV, scanned transcripts and degree certificates, English test scores and proof of your UH application.
4. **Submit** before the deadline.
5. Send **hard copies** of your transcripts and certificates if asked (last year's date was 15 December).

## Deadline

**1 December 2026, 11:59 p.m. Hawaiʻi time**, for study starting in **Fall 2027**

## Tips

- Apply to UH Mānoa early. You need proof of your admission application for the scholarship form.
- In your essays, connect your work experience to a development problem in your country and explain how the degree will help you solve it.
- Book your English test early so the scores arrive in time.

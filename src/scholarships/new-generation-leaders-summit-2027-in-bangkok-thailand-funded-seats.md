The **New Generation Leaders Summit 2027** brings together **140 young leaders** from around the world in **Bangkok, Thailand** for three days of talks, workshops and networking on the **UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**. It is organised by **Better World Charity**, an international non-profit.

## Dates and location

**23–25 February 2027**, Bangkok, Thailand

## Summit themes

Eight tracks, including climate action, social entrepreneurship, technology and AI, peace and diplomacy, education, health and gender equality.

## Participation categories

There are **40 funded seats** (fully and partially funded) and **100 self-funded seats**.

### Fully funded
- **Round-trip airfare support**
- **Shared 4-star accommodation**
- Visa support, an invitation letter and **visa fee reimbursement**
- Meals during the summit
- All sessions, workshops, keynotes and networking events
- Delegate kit and certificate
- **Bangkok city tour**

### Partially funded
- Everything above **except airfare and visa fee reimbursement**

### Self-funded
- A conference seat, 3 nights at the official venue, meals, sessions, delegate kit, certificate and Bangkok tour
- **Conference fee: USD 500**, paid after your place is offered

## Application fee

**Every applicant pays a USD 17 application fee.** Paying does not guarantee a funded seat. Think carefully before you pay.

## Who can apply

- People from **any country**, aged **18 to 40**
- Students, young professionals, activists, social entrepreneurs, NGO staff or public servants
- You already show **leadership or initiative** in your community, organisation, business or studies
- You care about at least one **SDG**
- You are comfortable with discussions and group work in **English**
- You have, or can get, a **passport valid until at least August 2027**
- You can attend the **full 3 days**

## How to apply

1. Visit the official summit website.
2. Complete the online application form (about 20 minutes).
3. Describe your leadership experience, impact and interest in the SDGs.
4. Pay the **USD 17** application fee.
5. Submit before the deadline. You will get a confirmation email and later an email with your result.

Applications are scored on leadership and impact, motivation, connection to the Global Goals, how you will share what you learn, and the balance of the group.

## Deadline

**30 November 2026**

## Important to know

- Only 40 of 140 seats are funded, and not all of those include flights.
- A visa invitation letter does not guarantee a Thai visa. Check the visa rules for your country first.
- Confirm all fees and benefits with the organisers before you pay anything.

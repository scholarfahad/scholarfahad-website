The **Universal Youth Leadership Summit (UYLS) 2027** is an international youth conference in **Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia**, from **28 to 31 January 2027**, organised by **Universal Youth Movement**, a private youth-programme organiser. The theme is **"Roots & Rights"**.

## Participation categories

### Fully funded (15 seats)
- Accommodation and meals
- Airport transfers
- Summit kit and certificate
- Cultural ceremony
- Two-way flight tickets **reimbursed after the summit** (you pay first)

### Partially funded (30 seats)
- **50% programme fee waiver**
- Accommodation, meals, airport transfers, summit kit, certificate and cultural ceremony

### Self-funded
- Accommodation, meals, airport transfers, summit kit, certificate and cultural ceremony
- You pay the full programme fee (some may be offered a scholarship after the interview)

### Summit Pass
- Meals, summit kit, certificate and cultural ceremony only

## Fees: what you need to know

- A **non-refundable application fee** applies
- Most participants pay a programme fee
- Even funded participants must **buy their own flights first** and claim them back after the event
- Check the exact fees and refund rules on the official website before paying

## Who can apply

- **All nationalities**
- Aged **18 to 40**
- Students, graduates, young professionals and emerging leaders
- Good English communication
- Interest in leadership and community impact

## How to apply

1. Visit the official **[UYLS Malaysia 2027 page](https://universalyouthmovement.org/uyls-malaysia-2027/)**.
2. Choose your category.
3. Complete the online form and pay the application fee.
4. Submit before **8 November 2026**.
5. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview.

## Before you apply

- **Plan your budget.** Even funded participants must pay for flights upfront and for their Malaysian visa if needed.
- **Read the fee and refund rules** carefully.
- **Weigh the cost.** If you can't afford the fees or travel, focus on fully funded scholarships and exchange programmes instead.

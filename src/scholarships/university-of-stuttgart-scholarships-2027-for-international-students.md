The University of Stuttgart is one of Germany's leading technical universities, known for engineering, architecture, computer science and natural sciences. Many websites advertise a "fully funded University of Stuttgart scholarship", but **there is no single full scholarship from the university for new international students**. Here is what is actually available, and what studying there costs.

> **Bottom line:** Plan to fund yourself, or win an external scholarship such as **DAAD**, before you go. Then use the university's options to top up.

## What it costs
- **Tuition (non-EU students):** **€1,500 per semester**
- **Semester fee:** €184 per semester (paid by all students)
- **Living costs:** at least **€992 a month**, according to the university
- Some groups are exempt from tuition, for example students who finished school in Germany or recognised refugees

## Scholarships you can apply for

### 1. Deutschlandstipendium: €300 a month
- Open to **German and international** degree students
- Awarded for strong grades plus at least one other quality, such as achievements, social commitment or personal circumstances
- The call is usually published in **late December**, and applications are open for **about 10 days in mid-January**
- [Deutschlandstipendium at Stuttgart](https://www.student.uni-stuttgart.de/en/financing/deutschlandstipendium/)

### 2. DAAD scholarships
- The **most important funder** for international degree students in Germany
- Programmes depend on your country and subject. Many must be applied for **from home**, with deadlines earlier than Stuttgart's
- **Note:** DAAD scholarships do **not** cover Stuttgart's €1,500 tuition fee

### 3. German foundation scholarships
- Germany's foundations for gifted students offer scholarships to international master's and PhD students, based on grades, social commitment and personality
- Good German is usually expected
- Example: [Hanns Seidel Foundation Scholarships 2027](https://scholarfahad.com/scholarships/hanns-seidel-foundation-scholarships-2027-in-germany-for-international-students/)

## Support once you are studying in Stuttgart
These are for **enrolled** international students, usually from the second or third semester, and are mostly based on need:
- **DAAD STIBET Final Stage:** for students near graduation (bachelor's from the 6th semester, master's from the 4th, PhD in the final 12 months). Typically €250–400 a month for 2–6 months.
- **DAAD STIBET Commitment:** for students active in the international community. Usually €250–300 a month.
- **DAAD STIBET Equal Opportunities:** for students facing extra challenges, such as disability, illness, parenthood or a refugee background.
- **Beihilfe and the Bürkert emergency fund:** short-term help in a financial emergency.

**Application dates:** 31 January, 30 June and 31 October (if funds remain). Contact the International Office first.

> The International Office's own advice: "Do not rely on receiving an aid or scholarship!"

## Tips
- Apply for **DAAD** or a home-country scholarship first; that is your best chance of real funding.
- Apply for the **Deutschlandstipendium** every January once you are admitted or enrolled.
- Learning German helps for foundation scholarships and part-time jobs.

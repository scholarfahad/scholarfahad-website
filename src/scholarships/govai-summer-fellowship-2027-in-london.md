The **Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI)** is a leading research organisation in London that works on how the world can manage the risks and benefits of advanced artificial intelligence. Its **Summer Fellowship** gives early-career researchers and professionals three months to work on AI governance and policy projects with GovAI's team.

**The 2027 Summer Fellowship call is expected to open around December 2026.** The details below are from the most recent rounds.

## What fellows receive (last round)

- **£12,000** for the three-month fellowship
- **Support for travel** to London
- **UK visa sponsorship** (a three-month temporary work visa) for those who need it
- A desk in GovAI's London office and weekday lunches
- Mentoring from experienced AI governance researchers

## Programme details

- **Length:** three months, full-time
- **Location:** in person in London
- **Last year's dates:** June to August
- **Two tracks:**
  - **Research Track:** for people who want to do AI governance research
  - **Applied Track:** for projects outside traditional research, such as policy, strategy or operations

## Who it's for

- People seriously interested in **AI governance and policy**
- Students, graduates and professionals from a **wide range of backgrounds**, including policy, law, economics, computer science, international relations and more
- GovAI doesn't list nationality restrictions, and it sponsors visas

## Selection process (last round)

1. **Written application**
2. A short **automated reasoning assessment** (about 20 minutes) for selected candidates
3. A **paid remote work test**
4. A **remote interview** and reference checks

## How to prepare now

- **Read GovAI's research** to see the issues it works on.
- **Prepare your CV**, focusing on research, writing and policy experience.
- **Think of a project idea** in AI governance you'd like to explore.
- **Watch the [GovAI website](https://www.governance.ai/)** for the 2027 call. Last year's deadline was in early January.

For questions, email **fellowships@governance.ai**.

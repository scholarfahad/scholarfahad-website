The **University of Bern** in Switzerland's capital city is one of Europe's leading research universities. The best way for international students to study there **fully funded** is the **Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship (ESKAS)**, offered by the Swiss Confederation. Scholars can be hosted at the University of Bern.

## Scholarship types

| Type | Duration | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **PhD scholarship** | Up to **36 months** | Master's graduates who want to do a PhD in Switzerland |
| **Research scholarship** | **12 months** | Master's graduates or PhD students doing research in Switzerland |

(There is also an **arts** master's scholarship for some countries, at Swiss art schools.)

## What the scholarship covers

- **CHF 2,450 per month** for living costs
- **Health insurance** (for non-EU/EFTA scholars)
- **Return flight allowance** (non-EU/EFTA scholars)
- **One-time CHF 600** grant for a rental deposit
- **Half-Fare Travelcard** for Swiss public transport

**Not covered:** tuition/semester fees (low at the University of Bern), conferences and fieldwork.

## Who can apply

- You hold a **master's degree** (or equivalent) before the scholarship starts
- You come from one of the **180+ eligible countries** (check your country's page)
- A **professor at the University of Bern agrees to supervise or host you** and gives you a letter
- You have a **strong research proposal** that matches your host's work
- Each country may have extra rules, such as **age limits** and how recently you graduated

## How to apply

1. **Find a professor** at the University of Bern whose research matches yours, and email them with your CV and research idea.
2. Get a **letter of support** from your future supervisor.
3. Write a **research proposal**.
4. Go to the **Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships page** (SERI website), choose **your country**, and download the application documents.
5. Submit your application to the **Swiss embassy or consulate** in your country before **your country's deadline**.

Results are announced by the **end of May 2027**, for study starting in autumn 2027.

## Deadline

**Each country has its own deadline**, set by the Swiss embassy. Many fall in **autumn**, so some may have already passed. **Check your country's page now.**

## Tips

- Start with the supervisor. Without a host letter, you can't apply.
- Make your research proposal clear, original and linked to your host's research.
- Explain how your research could build cooperation between Switzerland and your country.

**Western University** in **London, Ontario, Canada** is one of Canada's top research universities. It offers several scholarships for **international students** starting a bachelor's degree straight from high school.

## 1. International President's Entrance Scholarship

Western's most valuable entrance award for international students.

- **Value:** CAD 100,000, paid as **CAD 25,000 per year for 4 years**
- **Number:** **3 awards**
- Renewable each year if you keep strong grades and stay involved in university life

**Who can apply:**
- International students applying to the main campus **directly from secondary school**
- A minimum average of **90%** (or equivalent)
- A **nomination from your school**
- Outstanding academic results plus **leadership and community involvement**
- Full-time study

**How to apply:**
1. Apply to Western through the **Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC)**. You need your OUAC reference number for the scholarship form.
2. Ask your secondary school to **nominate** you.
3. Complete the online **National Scholarship Program** application. It opens in **mid-November 2026**.
4. Submit an original essay, your transcript and a school reference letter.
5. Attend an interview if you are shortlisted.

## 2. Automatic admission scholarships

There is **no separate application**. You are considered automatically when you apply for admission.

| Scholarship | Value | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Western Scholarship of Excellence | CAD 8,000 (CAD 6,000 in year 1 + CAD 2,000 for study abroad) | About 250 students with the highest admission averages |
| Western Scholarship of Distinction | CAD 3,500 (CAD 2,500 + CAD 1,000 for study abroad) | Final average of 92–100% |
| Western Admission Scholarship | CAD 2,500 (CAD 1,500 + CAD 1,000 for study abroad) | Final average of 90–91.9% |

International recipients of the Scholarship of Excellence may also get a **CAD 2,000 travel grant**.

## Important: cost of study

These scholarships **do not cover the full cost** of studying at Western. International tuition and living costs in Canada are high, so make a full budget before you apply. You will also need to show proof of funds for your Canadian study permit.

## Deadlines

- **15 January 2027:** apply for undergraduate admission (equal-consideration deadline)
- **President's Entrance Scholarship form:** opens mid-November 2026 and usually closes around **February 2027**. Check the official page for the exact date.

## Tips

- Talk to your school counsellor or principal early about a nomination.
- In your essay, show real leadership: projects you started, clubs you led, or service in your community.
- Keep your final-year grades high. Admission scholarships are based on your final average.

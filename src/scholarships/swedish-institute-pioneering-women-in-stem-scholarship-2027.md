The **Swedish Institute (SI)**, a Swedish government agency, offers the **Pioneering Women in STEM** scholarship: a **fully funded master's degree in Sweden** for women from ten countries, **including Nigeria**. It supports future female leaders in science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

> **Two deadlines:**
> 1. Apply to an eligible master's programme by **15 January 2027** on universityadmissions.se.
> 2. Then apply for the scholarship on SI's portal in **February 2027** (last year: 9–25 February).

## What the scholarship covers
- **Full tuition fees**, paid by SI directly to your Swedish university
- **Living allowance:** **SEK 12,000 a month** for the whole programme
- **Travel grant:** **SEK 15,000** (one-time; not for students already living in Sweden)

**Not included:** insurance. Check what your university provides.

## Who can apply
- **Women** who are citizens of one of these countries:
  **Bangladesh, Indonesia, Kenya, Malaysia, Nigeria, Philippines, Rwanda, South Africa, Thailand, Vietnam**
- At least **3,000 hours of work experience** before the SI application (from a maximum of three employers)
- Proven **leadership experience**, documented on SI's template forms
- Applying for an **eligible English-taught STEM master's programme** in Sweden in the first admission round

## Eligible programmes
SI publishes a list of eligible STEM master's programmes at Swedish universities each year (last year in **mid-November**). Examples have included programmes at the University of Gothenburg and other Swedish universities. Check the official list before you apply.

## How to apply
1. **Check the list** of eligible programmes on SI's website when it is published.
2. **Apply to the programme(s)** on **universityadmissions.se** by **15 January 2027**, and pay the application fee.
3. **Collect your proof** of work experience (3,000+ hours) and leadership experience using SI's templates.
4. **Apply for the scholarship** on SI's application portal in **February 2027**.
5. **Wait for results:** university admission results come in late March, and SI scholarship results in late April.

## Last year's timeline (2026/27), for reference
- University application: 15 October 2025 – 15 January 2026
- SI application: 9–25 February 2026
- University admission results: 26 March 2026
- SI scholarship results: 23 April 2026

## Tips
- Start your work and leadership certificates now. Employers can take weeks to sign.
- Show clearly how you will use your STEM skills to make a difference in Nigeria.
- Apply only to programmes on the SI list, or you won't qualify.

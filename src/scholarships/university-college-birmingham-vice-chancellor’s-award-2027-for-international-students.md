**University College Birmingham (UCB)** in the UK offers the **Vice-Chancellor's Award** to outstanding international students who show academic excellence and leadership. The award covers the **full tuition fee for your first year** of study.

## What the award covers

- **100% of tuition fees for the first year** of your bachelor's or master's programme
- The award does not include living costs or later years of tuition

## Who can apply

- **International students** with overseas fee status (home fee payers are not eligible)
- Starting a **full-time, on-campus** bachelor's or master's programme (part-time and distance learning are not eligible)
- **Bachelor's applicants:** at least **144 UCAS tariff points** or an international equivalent
- **Master's applicants:** a **first-class honours degree** or an international equivalent

## Conditions

- You must pay **at least £1,000 towards your deposit within 14 days** of receiving the scholarship offer, or the award will be withdrawn
- Winners agree to take part in testimonials, case studies or marketing activities if asked

## Intakes and deadlines

| Intake | Application deadline | Results |
|---|---|---|
| February 2027 | 31 October 2026 | By 30 November 2026 |
| **September 2027** | **31 May 2027** | By 30 June 2027 |
| February 2028 | 30 October 2027 | By 30 November 2027 |

## How to apply

1. **Apply to UCB** for an eligible full-time programme and **receive an offer**.
2. **Write a 500-word personal statement** covering:
   - your achievements in your field
   - your career goals
   - how you contribute to the betterment of society
3. **Submit your application** through the link for your intake on the official Vice-Chancellor's Award page.
4. **Watch your email** for the result.

## Other UCB scholarships for international students

UCB also offers the Dean's Award, Women in Leadership Scholarship, Sustainable Development Scholarship (postgraduate), Rising Talent Scholarship, NextGen Innovator Scholarship, an automatic Early Action Scholarship for undergraduates who pay their deposit early, and the GREAT Scholarship for postgraduate applicants from Nigeria and Pakistan.

## Tips

- **Apply for your course early**, so you have an offer in time for the scholarship deadline.
- **Show leadership and social impact** in your personal statement with real examples.
- **Plan your other costs:** tuition for later years, living costs and visa fees.
- Applying for the scholarship is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed award.

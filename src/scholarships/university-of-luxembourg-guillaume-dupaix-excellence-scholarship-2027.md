The **Guillaume Dupaix International Excellence Scholarship** is offered by the **University of Luxembourg (Uni.lu)** to outstanding students starting a **master's degree** in **any field**. It is open to students of **all nationalities**.

## What the scholarship gives

- **EUR 5,629 per semester** (EUR 11,258 per year)
- For up to **2 years** (the normal length of a master's)

Tuition at the University of Luxembourg is **low**, about EUR 400–800 per year for most master's programmes. Living costs in Luxembourg are high (about EUR 1,200–1,800 per month), so plan for the difference.

## Number of awards

**30 scholarships each year**, 10 per faculty:
- **15** open to students from **any country**
- **15** reserved for students from **selected countries**, including the Western Balkans, Ibero-American countries, Thailand, Vietnam, Belarus, Egypt, India, Moldova, Russia, the UK, Turkey and Ukraine

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- Admitted to a **full-time master's** at the University of Luxembourg, in any field
- An **excellent academic record**, as judged by your programme directors
- You do **not** receive Luxembourg's State Financial Aid for Higher Education

Programmes with a **compulsory semester or year abroad** are **not eligible**.

## How to apply

1. **Apply for a master's** at the University of Luxembourg through the online admissions portal.
2. Your **programme directors** assess your academic record.
3. Strong admitted students get an **email invitation** to complete the scholarship application online.
4. Submit the form and any missing documents by the deadline.
5. Results are emailed (last year by **15 May**). Winners must **accept within 3 working days**.

## Deadline

The 2027 dates have not been published yet. Last year:
- **24 March:** master's applications and scholarship forms
- **31 March:** missing documents

Expect similar dates in **late March 2027**, and **apply for your master's early**.

## Tips

- Your master's application is your scholarship application, so make it excellent: strong grades, a clear motivation letter and good references.
- Check your email (and spam folder) for the scholarship invitation.
- Budget for living costs. The scholarship helps a lot but may not cover everything in Luxembourg.

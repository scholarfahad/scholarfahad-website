The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Oxford** is run by **Africa Oxford (AfOx)** with funding from the **Mastercard Foundation**. It gives talented young Africans the chance to study at one of the world's top universities, and to return to lead change across Africa.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full course fees**
- A **living-costs grant** of at least **GBP 21,805 per year**
- **Leadership training**, mentoring and networking with other African scholars
- Support **before, during and after** your time at Oxford

## Courses

- **MBA at Saïd Business School:** up to **3 AfOx Graduate Scholarships** are confirmed for **2027–28**.
- **Other taught master's courses:** in past years the programme has also funded African students on a range of Oxford taught postgraduate courses. Check the official AfOx page for the 2027–28 list.

## Who can apply

- You are a **citizen of an African country** and **ordinarily live in Africa**
- You have a **strong academic record**
- You show **leadership** and a clear **commitment to Africa's development**
- **For the MBA:** you must **not already hold a master's degree**

Scholars are selected on:
1. Academic merit and potential
2. Leadership experience and capacity
3. Fit with AfOx's values and themes
4. Commitment to Africa's development

## How to apply

There is **no separate scholarship application** for most courses.

1. **Choose an eligible Oxford course** (for example, the MBA).
2. **Apply for admission** through Oxford's graduate application system before your **course's funding deadline**.
3. **Eligible applicants are considered automatically.** Shortlisted candidates are contacted by email and may be asked to show how they meet the selection criteria or attend an interview.

## Deadlines

- **MBA:** **6 January 2027**
- **Other courses:** each course has its own **December or January** deadline. Check your course page.

## Tips

- Apply for admission by the **December/January funding deadline**, not a later admission deadline. Later applications are not considered for this scholarship.
- In your application, show clear examples of leadership and how you will use your Oxford degree to make an impact in Africa.
- Start your references and personal statement early. Oxford applications take time.

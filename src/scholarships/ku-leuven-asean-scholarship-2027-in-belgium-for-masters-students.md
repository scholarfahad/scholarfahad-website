The **KU Leuven ASEAN Scholarship** helps outstanding students from Southeast Asia study a master's degree at KU Leuven in Belgium. KU Leuven is one of the top universities in Europe.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fee waiver**
- An allowance of **EUR 4,000**

The allowance helps, but it does not cover a full year of living costs in Belgium. Plan how you will fund the rest. You will also need to show proof of funds for your student visa.

## Who can apply

- You are a citizen of **Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste or Vietnam**
- You are applying for an eligible master's or advanced master's programme at KU Leuven in:
  - Humanities and Social Sciences
  - Science, Engineering and Technology
  - Biomedical Sciences
- You have an excellent academic record (high GPA)
- Your most recent degree is from outside Flanders, and you are not currently studying at a Flemish institution
- English: **IELTS 7.0**, **TOEFL iBT 94** or **Cambridge CPE (C2)**. Some native speakers and graduates of English-taught degrees may be exempt.

Preparatory, bridging and distance-learning programmes are not eligible. You cannot combine this award with another KU Leuven scholarship or an Erasmus Mundus scholarship.

## How to apply

1. **Choose an eligible master's programme** at KU Leuven.
2. **Apply for admission online** before the scholarship deadline.
3. **Add the KU Leuven Scholarships & Master Mind Candidacy Form** to your application.
4. **Register your scholarship candidacy** through KU Leuven's online registration tool.

Take your English test before the deadline. Results can usually be added later.

## Deadline

The 2027–2028 deadline has not been announced yet. Last year's deadline was **15 January 2026**, so expect a similar date in **mid-January 2027**. Start preparing your documents now.

## Tips

- Pick a programme that clearly matches your previous studies and career goals.
- Your motivation letter should explain why KU Leuven and that specific programme, and how you will use the degree back home.
- Book your IELTS or TOEFL early. You need a high score (IELTS 7.0).

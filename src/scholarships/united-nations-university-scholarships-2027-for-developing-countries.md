The **United Nations University Institute for the Advanced Study of Sustainability (UNU-IAS)** in Tokyo, Japan, offers scholarships to students from **developing countries** for its **MSc in Sustainability** and **PhD in Sustainability Science**.

**Status:** applications for **September 2027** entry **open in November 2026**. The last deadline was **28 January**, so expect the next one around late January 2027. Prepare now.

## Programmes

- **MSc in Sustainability:** 2 years
- **PhD in Sustainability Science:** 3 years

Both are taught in English. You choose one of four thematic areas and propose research to carry out at UNU-IAS.

## Scholarships

| Scholarship | Monthly allowance | Tuition | Travel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Japan Foundation for UNU (jfUNU)** | ¥120,000 (MSc up to 24 months, PhD up to 36 months) | May be waived | Not covered |
| **UNU Rector's Scholarship** (MSc) | ¥130,000, up to 24 months | Fully covered | Up to US$2,500, plus a research allowance |
| **Resona Bank Scholarship** (MSc) | ¥130,000, up to 24 months | Fully covered | Up to ¥260,000 |

¥120,000 is about US$800 a month. Visa fees and health or accident insurance are **not** covered.

There's **no separate scholarship form**. You give the information in your admission application, and scholarships are awarded to accepted students on a competitive basis.

## Who can apply

- A **citizen of a developing country** on the **OECD DAC list** of aid recipients, including Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Ethiopia, Pakistan and most of Africa and Asia
- Able to show **financial need**
- **Not** living in Japan on a working visa
- **MSc applicants** must not already hold a master's degree or be enrolled in another postgraduate programme
- Recipients can't hold another scholarship at the same time
- The Rector's and Resona scholarships also look for an interest in the work of the United Nations

## How to apply

1. Choose the MSc or PhD and one of the four thematic areas on the UNU-IAS degree programmes page: https://unu.edu/ias/admissions/degree-programmes
2. When applications open in **November 2026**, complete the online application, including the scholarship and financial-need information.
3. Submit before the deadline, usually late January.

## Prepare now

- A clear **research proposal** in one of the thematic areas. This matters most.
- Transcripts and degree certificates
- English test results (check the FAQ for requirements)
- Reference letters
- Proof of financial need

## Tips

- Read UNU-IAS's research themes and link your proposal to them directly.
- Show how your study will help your home country's sustainability challenges.
- Apply early. Scholarships are competitive and limited.

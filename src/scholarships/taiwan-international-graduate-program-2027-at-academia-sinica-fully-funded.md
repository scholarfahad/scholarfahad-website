The **Taiwan International Graduate Program (TIGP)** is a PhD programme run by **Academia Sinica**, Taiwan's top research institution, together with leading Taiwanese universities. All courses and research are in **English**, and students come from all over the world.

TIGP offers **14 interdisciplinary PhD programmes** in the sciences and humanities. Students do their research in Academia Sinica's labs and receive their PhD from a partner university.

## What you get

- A tax-free stipend of **NTD 40,000 per month** (about USD 1,200) in your first year
- The stipend is **extended** for students with good academic performance. In later years, funding usually comes from your thesis adviser.
- An extra **NTD 6,000 per month** for outstanding students through the TIGP Research Performance Fellowship
- **No application fee**

Tuition and fees vary by partner university, so check with your chosen programme.

## Fields of study

TIGP programmes cover areas such as:
- Chemical biology, molecular biology, molecular medicine and neuroscience
- Agricultural and biological sciences, biodiversity and bioinformatics
- Chemistry, nanoscience and sustainable chemical technology
- Physics and molecular science
- Earth system science
- Data science and computing
- Linguistics

See the official TIGP website for the full list of 14 programmes and their partner universities.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- You have a **bachelor's or master's degree**. Most programmes accept applicants with only a bachelor's.
- **Final-year undergraduates** can apply and submit their diploma later.
- English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS or similar). You may be exempt if your degree was taught in English and your university issues an official certificate.
- Some programmes may ask for GRE subject scores.

You can apply to **only one** TIGP programme.

## How to apply

1. **Choose one TIGP programme** that matches your interests and read its requirements.
2. **Create an account** on the TIGP online application portal.
3. **Upload your documents.** These usually include transcripts, a CV, a statement of purpose and English test results.
4. **Add your referees.** They will submit recommendation letters online.
5. **Submit** before the deadline.

## Deadline

**1 February 2027, 11:59 p.m. Taiwan time (GMT+8)**, for study starting in **Fall 2027**

## Tips

- Look at the research of TIGP faculty in your programme and mention the labs you would like to join.
- Your statement of purpose should explain your research interests clearly and show what you have already done.
- Contact your referees early so their letters arrive on time.

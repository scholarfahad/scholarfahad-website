The **University of Lausanne (UNIL)** in Switzerland offers **Master's Grants for international students** who completed their previous degree outside Switzerland. About **10 grants** are awarded each year.

> **Deadline:** 1 November 2026, for master's programmes starting in **September 2027**. Your recommendation letters must also arrive by this date.

## What the grant covers
- **CHF 1,600 a month** for the minimum official length of your master's (usually 3 or 4 semesters)
- **Exemption from normal tuition fees.** You only pay a CHF 80 fee per semester.

**Note:** The grant does not cover all living costs in Lausanne, one of Switzerland's more expensive cities. Plan for extra funds.

## Who can apply
- International students who hold, or are completing, a **bachelor's degree from a university outside Switzerland** that UNIL considers equivalent to its own bachelor's
- **Excellent academic results**
- **C1 level** in the programme's teaching language (French or English, depending on the master's)
- Current UNIL students generally cannot use this grant to fund their existing degree

## Programmes not eligible
- Master's programmes at the **School of Medicine**
- **Most Master of Law** programmes (some law specialisations are exceptions, for example International and Comparative Law, Legal Theory, and International Tax Law and Policy)
- **MAS** and other continuing-education programmes

## Documents
- Degree certificate, or proof you will graduate before the master's starts
- Academic transcripts
- Passport or ID
- CV
- Motivation letter
- **Two recommendation letters**, sent directly to UNIL by your referees

## How to apply
1. Choose an **eligible master's** at UNIL.
2. Complete the **online grant application** and pay the **CHF 200** administrative fee (non-refundable).
3. Ask your two referees to send their letters **directly to UNIL** before the deadline.
4. Submit everything by **1 November 2026**.

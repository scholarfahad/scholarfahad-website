The **United Nations University Institute for the Advanced Study of Sustainability (UNU-IAS)** in **Tokyo, Japan**, offers a two-year **MSc in Sustainability**, taught in English. Students from developing countries can be considered for the **Japan Foundation for UNU (JFUNU) Scholarship**.

Applications for **September 2027** entry will open in **November 2026**.

## What the JFUNU Scholarship covers

- A **monthly living allowance of ¥120,000** for up to **24 months**
- **Tuition fees may be waived** for recipients (tuition is USD 12,000 a year)

**Not covered:** travel to and from Japan, visa fees, and health or accident insurance.

## About the programme

- **MSc in Sustainability**, two years, full-time, in Tokyo
- Taught in **English**
- Students choose one of four thematic areas:
  - Governance for Climate Change and Sustainable Development
  - Biodiversity and Society
  - Water and Resource Management
  - Innovation and Education
- A double degree option with the University of Tokyo is available

## Who can apply

- **Citizens of developing countries** on the OECD DAC list who can show financial need
- A **bachelor's degree** (or equivalent) in a field related to sustainability; final-year students can apply
- A **GPA of 2.8 or higher** on a 4.0 scale (about 70%)
- **English:** TOEFL iBT 100, TOEFL PBT 600 or IELTS Academic 7.0, or proof of two years of English-taught study

**Not eligible:** people living in Japan on a work visa, those already enrolled in another postgraduate programme or holding a master's degree, and those receiving another scholarship.

## How to apply

1. **Choose your thematic area** and prepare a short research proposal on a sustainability issue.
2. **Apply online** to UNU-IAS when applications open in November 2026.
3. **Indicate your scholarship interest** in the application form. There is no separate scholarship application.
4. **Upload your supporting documents** (transcripts, degree certificate, English test results and recommendation letters) by the deadline.

## Tips

- **Book your English test now.** UNU-IAS asks for a high score (IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100).
- **Write a clear research proposal** linked to one of the four thematic areas.
- **Show your financial need** and how the degree will help your country.
- Applying for the scholarship is free. Never pay anyone who promises a UNU scholarship.

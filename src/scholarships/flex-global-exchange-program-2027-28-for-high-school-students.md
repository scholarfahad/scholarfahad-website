The **FLEX Global (Future Leaders Exchange) Program** is a **fully funded U.S. government exchange** that gives secondary school students the chance to spend **one academic year in the United States**, living with an **American host family** and attending an **American high school**.

## What you get

- **10 months** (one school year) in the USA
- Live with a **volunteer host family**
- Attend a **US high school**
- **No programme fees.** The programme is fully funded.
- Leadership, community service and cultural activities

## Eligible countries (2027–28)

- **Africa:** **Kenya, Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia**
- **Asia and Middle East:** Bahrain, India, Indonesia, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia, Pakistan, Tajikistan, Thailand, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan
- **Europe and Caucasus:** Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Greece, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Türkiye, Ukraine
- **Americas:** Argentina, Brazil

**Nigeria, Ghana and other countries not listed are not eligible.**

## Who can apply

- **Secondary school students**, generally aged **15 to 18**
- Citizens of an eligible country
- Exact birth-date ranges and requirements are in the official 2027–28 brochure

It's **very competitive**. About 1 in 50 applicants is selected.

## How to apply

- **Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Greece, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Ukraine and Uzbekistan:** apply online at https://discoverflexglobal.org/apply-to-flex
- **Argentina, Bahrain, Brazil, Egypt, India, Indonesia, Jordan, Kenya, Morocco, Pakistan, Thailand, Tunisia and Türkiye:** contact your **FLEX Global country office** (details on the same page)

Selection includes several **testing rounds**, which vary by country.

Questions: **discoverflex@americancouncils.org**

## Deadline

Applications for **2027–28** are open now. **Deadlines differ by country**, so check with your country office and apply early.

## Tips

- Read the official 2027–28 brochure for your exact age range.
- Practise your English. Testing rounds usually include English tests.
- Never pay agents. The FLEX programme is free.

The **University of Manchester**, one of the UK's top research universities and a member of the Russell Group, offers the **Global Futures Scholarship** to talented international students starting a full-time course in **September 2027**. More than **350 scholarships**, worth over **£6 million**, are available across undergraduate and master's courses.

## Scholarship value (Africa)

- **Master's:** **£19,000** off international tuition fees
- **Bachelor's:** up to **£36,000**, paid as **£12,000 a year for three years** off international tuition fees (you need to score 60% in your exams each year to keep it)

The scholarship is a tuition discount. It does not include a living allowance.

## Eligible African countries

You must normally live in (be "domiciled" in) one of these countries, meaning you have mainly lived there for the past three years:

**Botswana, Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Mauritius, Nigeria, South Africa and Zimbabwe**

## Who can apply

- You hold an **offer** for a full-time, on-campus course at the University of Manchester starting in **September 2027**
- **Master's applicants** must hold an **unconditional** offer
- You are **self-funded** (not sponsored by an employer or government)
- **Bachelor's applicants** need qualifications such as A-levels, the IB Diploma, the South African NSC/IEB or an approved foundation year

### Not eligible

- **Bachelor's:** Medicine (MBChB), Dentistry (BDS), Architecture, courses with an integrated foundation year, and students who have studied in the UK before
- **Master's:** MBA, MPhil, MArch, PGCE, clinical medicine and dentistry, some research master's, and applicants whose bachelor's degree is from outside Africa
- Blended and distance-learning courses

## Deadlines

**Master's:**

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Scholarship form deadline (unconditional offer needed) | **20 April 2027** |
| Decisions | By 24 May 2027 |

**Bachelor's:**

| Round | Who | Deadline | Decisions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Students who sat exams in 2026 | **25 February 2027** | By 2 April 2027 |
| Round 2 | All other students | **8 April 2027** | By 12 May 2027 |

Bachelor's winners must make Manchester their UCAS **Firm** choice by 15 May 2027.

## How to apply

1. **Apply for your course** at the University of Manchester (through UCAS for bachelor's degrees, or the university's online application for master's).
2. **Receive an offer.** Master's applicants need an unconditional offer.
3. **Watch your email.** The university emails the scholarship application form to eligible offer-holders from December 2026.
4. **Complete and submit the scholarship form** before your deadline.

## Tips

- **Apply for your course early.** You can't apply for the scholarship until you have an offer.
- **Master's applicants:** send all your documents quickly so your offer becomes unconditional before 20 April.
- **Plan your living costs**, since the scholarship covers tuition only.
- **Combine it with other funding** if you can, such as external scholarships.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed scholarship.

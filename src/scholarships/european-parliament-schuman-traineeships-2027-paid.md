The **European Parliament** offers paid **Robert Schuman Traineeships** to university graduates who want hands-on experience in European politics and law-making. Trainees work in the Parliament's offices, mainly in **Brussels and Luxembourg**, and see how the EU works from the inside.

The next traineeship runs from **1 March to 31 July 2027**.

## What trainees receive

- A **monthly grant** for the five months of the traineeship (the amount is set by the European Parliament and published on its traineeship website)
- Real work experience in a European Parliament directorate or office
- Training, networking and a certificate at the end

## Who can apply

- **At least 18 years old** on the start date
- A **completed university degree** of at least three years (bachelor's or equivalent) by the application deadline. Unfinished studies do not count, even if you graduate soon after.
- **Languages:** thorough knowledge of one official EU language and very good knowledge of a second. Non-EU citizens need very good knowledge of **English, French or German**.
- You must **not** have done a traineeship or paid work of more than two months at an EU institution, body or agency in the six months before the start date.
- A clean criminal record

### Nationality

- Places are mainly for **nationals of EU member states and of candidate or accession countries**.
- About **10% of places** may go to nationals of **other countries**, so applicants from Africa, Asia and the Americas can apply, but competition is very strong.
- Selected non-EU trainees must hold a **valid Schengen visa** for the whole traineeship. Visa costs are not reimbursed.

## How to apply

1. Go to the **[European Parliament traineeship portal](https://ep-stages.gestmax.eu/website/application-informations)** and read the application guidance.
2. **Browse the traineeship offers** and choose the ones that match your studies and skills.
3. **Create an account** and complete the online application, including your motivation.
4. **Submit before 31 October 2026, 23:59 Brussels time.**

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |

## Tips

- **Apply early.** Don't wait for the last day, because the portal can slow down near the deadline.
- **Choose offers that match your degree**, such as law, political science, economics, communication or translation.
- **Write a focused motivation** explaining why you want to work at the European Parliament and what you can bring.
- **Missed this window?** Prepare now for the spring 2027 call.
- Applying is free. The European Parliament never charges fees.

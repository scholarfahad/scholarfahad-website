The **African Leadership College of Higher Education (ALCHE)** in **Mauritius**, part of the African Leadership group, offers **full-ride scholarships** through the **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program** to talented students from **across Africa** who can't afford university.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** for your undergraduate degree
- **Additional financial support** throughout your time at ALCHE

ALCHE says **very limited** scholarships are available in each intake.

## Degrees at ALCHE

- BSc (Hons) Computer Science
- BSc Software Engineering
- BSc (Hons) Entrepreneurial Leadership
- BSc (Hons) International Business and Trade

All students first complete a **Leadership Core** year, then choose their degree.

## Who can apply

- **Financial need:** you can't afford ALCHE tuition even with other aid, and can show this with official documents
- **Academics:** you meet ALCHE's admission criteria, with national exam results of **at least a B average**
- **Leadership:** clear evidence of leadership potential and community involvement
- **Able to study in Mauritius:** a Mauritian citizen, or able to get a student residence permit

**Especially encouraged to apply:** young women, people with physical or learning disabilities, and applicants **aged 26 or under**.

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission to ALCHE.** Only applicants eligible for admission can apply for the scholarship.
2. **Eligibility check:** submit information to confirm you qualify.
3. **Scholarship application form:** household income, personal and guardian details, how your education has been paid for, **essays and video answers**, referral letters and supporting documents.
4. **ALCHE Fit Challenge:** a short online test, about 25 minutes.
5. **Review and selection** by the scholarship committee.

Official page: https://alcheducation.com/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/

## Deadline

**14 November 2026** for the **February 2027 intake**, as reported. ALCHE says applications are ongoing and places are very limited, so apply as early as you can.

## Tips

- Start your ALCHE admission application now. You can't apply for the scholarship without it.
- Gather proof of financial need early: income letters, school fee records and similar documents.
- Use your essays and video to show real leadership: a project you started, a group you led, or a problem you solved in your community.
- Don't pay anyone. Applying is done only through ALCHE's official website.

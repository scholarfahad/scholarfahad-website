The **E5 Doctoral Training Partnership (E5 DTP)** is a PhD programme led by the University of Edinburgh's School of Geosciences and funded by the **Natural Environment Research Council (NERC)**. It offers **fully funded PhD studentships** in earth, ecology and environmental sciences, and is open to students of **all nationalities**.

> **Deadline:** 2 December 2026, **12 noon UK time** (1:00 PM in Nigeria). Applications for 2027 are open now.

## What the studentship covers
- **Stipend:** **£21,805 a year** (2026/27 rate), for up to **4 years**
- **Tuition fees:** covered. For international students, the University covers the difference between home and international fees.
- **Research costs** for your project
- Training and development throughout the PhD

**Not covered:** visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, flights and relocation costs.

## Who can apply
- Students of **any nationality**. UKRI limits international students to **30%** of each intake.
- At least a **2:1 honours degree** or equivalent. **A master's is not required.**
- Relevant professional experience and postgraduate qualifications are also considered
- You must meet the University of Edinburgh's English requirements. Test results are only needed if you receive an offer.
- You must live within commuting distance of Edinburgh during the PhD

## Research areas
Projects cover the environment in its broadest sense: earth sciences, geography, ecology, climate, oceans, biodiversity and more. Browse the current supervisor-led projects on the E5 DTP website.

## How to apply
1. **Choose one project** from the E5 project list. You may apply for **only one**; multiple applications are rejected automatically.
2. **Contact the supervisor** to discuss the project before you apply.
3. **Prepare your documents** as PDFs:
   - E5 Studentship form (with your EDI Response ID)
   - Academic CV (**maximum 2 pages**)
   - EDI monitoring form (completed online, then saved as PDF)
   - Transcripts and certificates (interim transcripts if still studying)
4. **Ask two referees** early. They must use the **official E5 reference form** and an institutional email address. Your proposed supervisor cannot be a referee, and reference letters are not accepted.
5. **Submit** in the E5 portal before **12 noon UK time on 2 December 2026**. Uploading is not enough; you must click submit.

## Interviews
- In person in Edinburgh: **22–23 February 2027**
- **Online for applicants outside the UK: 24 February 2027**

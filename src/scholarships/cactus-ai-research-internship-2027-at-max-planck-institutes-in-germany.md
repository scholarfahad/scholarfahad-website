The **CaCTüS Internship Programme** gives talented students from around the world a chance to do real research in **artificial intelligence, machine learning and brain research** at **Max Planck Institutes** in **Tübingen and Stuttgart, Germany**. The programme is designed especially for students who have faced **barriers to higher education**.

## Programme details

- **Dates:** 1 July – 30 September 2027 (90 days, in person)
- **Location:** Tübingen and Stuttgart, Germany
- **Research areas:** AI, machine learning, computational neuroscience and brain research

## What's covered

- **Monthly stipend** of about **€1,350**, to cover living costs, housing and health insurance
- **Accommodation** arranged by the Internship Coordination Office
- **Travel costs** reimbursed (check the official conditions)
- Mentoring from leading researchers and hands-on research experience

**Not covered:** visa fees and trips to the embassy.

## Who can apply

- **Bachelor's or master's students** in computer science, mathematics, physics, engineering, psychology, cognitive science, neuroscience or a related field
- **Bachelor's students** must have completed at least **four semesters** before the internship starts
- **Not yet started a PhD**
- A strong academic record, good English and clear motivation for research
- Available for the **whole 90 days** (visa rules don't allow extensions)
- Have faced significant **personal, financial, regional or societal barriers** to higher education, which is central to selection
- **Open to all nationalities**

## How to apply

1. Go to the official **[CaCTüS programme website](https://www.projects.tuebingen.mpg.de/program)** and read the guidelines.
2. Prepare your CV, academic records and motivation statement.
3. **Explain your background**, including the barriers you have faced and how you overcame them.
4. Choose research projects that match your interests.
5. Submit your application on the online portal by **16 November 2026**.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications open | 21 October 2026 |
| Application deadline | **16 November 2026** |
| Internship | 1 July – 30 September 2027 |

## Tips

- **Be honest and specific** about the challenges you've faced. It's a key part of selection.
- **Show your research curiosity**, through class projects, coding work or a short research idea.
- **Match your skills to the projects**, such as programming in Python, maths or neuroscience.
- **Start your visa process early** once selected.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a CaCTüS place.

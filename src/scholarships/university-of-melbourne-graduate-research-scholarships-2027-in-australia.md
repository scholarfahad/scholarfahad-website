The **University of Melbourne**, Australia's top-ranked university, offers **Graduate Research Scholarships** to domestic and **international students** starting a **PhD** or **master's by research** in 2027.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fee offset:** up to **4 years** for a PhD, or up to **2 years** for a master's by research
- **Living allowance: A$41,100 per year** (2027 full-time rate, pro rata), about US$27,000. It may be increased each year.
- **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)**, single membership, for international students who need a student visa
- **Relocation grant:** A$3,000 if you're moving from outside Australia (A$2,000 from another Australian state)

## Who can apply

- Anyone who **applies for a University of Melbourne graduate research degree** (PhD or master's by research) and meets its entry requirements
- **Open to international students** of any nationality
- Entry usually needs a strong bachelor's (often with honours) or master's degree that includes a **research component**, plus English proficiency. Check your chosen course's requirements.

## How to apply

There's **no separate scholarship application**. You're **automatically considered** when you submit a complete application for a graduate research course by a round closing date.

1. **Find a research topic and a supervisor.** Many courses expect you to contact a potential supervisor first. Use the University's Find an Expert tool: https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/
2. **Prepare your documents:** research proposal, CV, transcripts, English test and referee details.
3. **Apply online** for your graduate research course: https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/graduate-research
4. Make sure your application is **complete** by the round deadline. Incomplete applications are not assessed.

## Scholarship rounds for 2027 start

| Round | Applications complete by | Results by |
|---|---|---|
| **Round 1** | **31 October 2026** | 18 December 2026 |
| Round 2 | 31 January 2027 | 26 March 2027 |
| Round 3 | 15 May 2027 | 16 July 2027 |

Successful applicants must start by 31 October of the year of their offer.

## Tips

- **Apply in Round 1** if you can. More scholarships are available early.
- Contact supervisors **now**. A supportive supervisor greatly improves your chances.
- Write a focused research proposal that matches your supervisor's work.
- Research experience matters: a thesis, publications or a research project strengthen your application.
- Never pay agents. Applying is done directly on the University's website.

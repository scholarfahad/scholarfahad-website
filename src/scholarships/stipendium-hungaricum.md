Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian government's scholarship for international students. It is based on agreements between Hungary and partner countries, so availability depends on where you're from.

## What it covers

The scholarship generally includes:

- Free tuition
- A monthly stipend (higher for PhD students)
- Free dormitory place or a contribution towards accommodation costs
- Medical insurance

## Who can apply

You need to be a citizen of one of the scholarship's partner countries and meet the entry requirements of your chosen programme. Each partner country also sets its own rules and quotas.

## How the application works

There are two steps, and both are required:

1. **Apply online** on the Stipendium Hungaricum application system, choosing up to two programmes.
2. **Get nominated by your Sending Partner,** the authority in your home country (often the ministry of education or a scholarship board). Check what your Sending Partner requires, because some have their own separate application.

## Documents you'll need

- Passport
- Motivation letter
- Transcripts and diplomas
- Language certificate
- Medical certificate
- Recommendation letters and a research plan for PhD applicants

## Tips

Hungarian universities often hold entrance exams or interviews, sometimes online. Prepare for them as carefully as for the written application.

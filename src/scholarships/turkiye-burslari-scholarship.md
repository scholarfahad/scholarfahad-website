Türkiye Bursları is the Turkish government's scholarship programme for international students. It is one of the most popular fully funded scholarships, with applications from almost every country.

## What it covers

The scholarship is fully funded. It generally includes:

- University tuition fees
- A monthly stipend
- Accommodation
- Health insurance
- A one-year Turkish language course
- A return flight ticket

## Who can apply

Each level has its own age and grade requirements. As a general guide:

- **Undergraduate:** usually under 21, with strong secondary school grades
- **Master's:** usually under 30, with a strong bachelor's degree
- **PhD:** usually under 35, with a strong master's degree

Check the official website for the exact grade thresholds and any changes for the current year.

## How to apply

Applications are made online on the Türkiye Bursları website. You choose up to several universities and programmes on the same application. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview.

## Documents you'll need

- Passport or national ID
- Recent photo
- Diploma or expected graduation letter
- Transcripts
- Language test score, if you have one
- Statement of purpose (written into the online form)
- Research proposal for PhD applicants

## Tips

Your statement of purpose should explain why you chose Türkiye and each programme, not just why you want a scholarship. Applicants who show a genuine interest in the country and a clear plan stand out.

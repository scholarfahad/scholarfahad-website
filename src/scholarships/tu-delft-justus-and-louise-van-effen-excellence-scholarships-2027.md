**Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)**, one of the world's leading technical universities, offers the **Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships** to outstanding **international students** starting a **two-year MSc** in **September 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees** for each year of a regular two-year MSc programme
- A **contribution toward living expenses**

TU Delft gives only a small number of these scholarships, **about two per faculty**, so competition is very strong.

## Who can apply

- **International students** applying for a **regular two-year MSc** at TU Delft
- Excellent academic record. Successful candidates are usually in the **top 10%** of their previous degree programme.
- You must have applied for, or already received (conditional) admission to, an eligible MSc
- **Not eligible:** students with a bachelor's from another Dutch university, current TU Delft bachelor's students, and Joint Education Programmes

## Fields

All of TU Delft's regular MSc programmes, including engineering (civil, mechanical, electrical, aerospace, chemical), computer science, architecture, applied sciences, and technology and policy.

## How to apply

You must submit **all three** of the following by the deadline:

1. A **complete MSc admission application** for an eligible TU Delft programme, with all required documents, including your English test where required
2. The **scholarship application form**, uploaded in your application
3. **Two academic reference letters**

**Incomplete applications are not assessed.**

Official page: https://www.tudelft.nl/en/education/study-programme-orientation/practical-matters/scholarships/justus-louise-van-effen-excellence-scholarships

## Deadline

**1 December 2026, 23:59 CET** for September 2027 entry.

## Tips

- Start your MSc application now. Transcripts, an English test and references take time.
- Ask your two referees early, and give them at least 3–4 weeks.
- Use your motivation letter to link your past projects to the specific MSc and to TU Delft's research.
- Also look at other TU Delft and Dutch scholarships, since places are very limited.

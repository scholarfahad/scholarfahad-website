The **Berlin School of Economics (BSoE)** runs a joint **PhD programme** in **Economics, Finance, Accounting and Management**. It brings together universities and research institutes in the Berlin-Brandenburg region, so you get world-class training and a wide choice of supervisors. It is open to students of **all nationalities**, and the programme is taught in **English**.

## Partner institutions

- Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin
- Freie Universität Berlin
- Technische Universität Berlin
- University of Potsdam
- European University Viadrina Frankfurt (Oder)
- ESMT Berlin
- Hertie School
- ROCKWOOL Foundation Berlin Institute for the Economy and the Future of Work (RFBerlin)
- WZB Berlin Social Science Center

Your PhD degree is awarded by the institution where your supervisor works.

## Doctoral tracks

- **Economics**
- **Finance**
- **Accounting**
- **Management**

## How the programme works

- **Qualification phase** (about 3 semesters): advanced courses (at least 90 ECTS)
- **Dissertation phase** (about 3–4 years in total for the whole PhD): your own research with a supervisor
- **Total length:** about 4 years, full-time, starting **1 October 2027**

## Funding

- **Stipend of about EUR 1,650 per month** during the qualification phase
- A **family allowance** for eligible children
- During the dissertation phase, students are **usually employed** (paid) at their supervisor's institution, if they make good progress
- **No tuition fees**. You pay only a semester contribution of about EUR 300–350.

## Who can apply

- Applicants of **any nationality**
- A **master's degree** in **economics or business**. Related fields such as statistics, industrial engineering or quantitative social sciences are accepted if you have enough economics or business background.
- **Bachelor's holders** may apply if they also apply to a master's programme with a fast-track option.
- A strong academic record and good **quantitative skills**
- **English:** proof is needed if English is not your first language. TOEFL or IELTS is recommended but **not required**.
- **GRE:** highly recommended but **not required**
- **German is not required**
- You do **not** need a supervisor before you apply

## Documents (in English)

- **Motivation letter** (maximum 2 pages)
- **CV**
- Transcripts and degree certificates
- GRE or GMAT scores (optional, but recommended)
- English test result (optional)
- **Two referees**, who submit letters directly

## How to apply

1. Choose your **track**: Economics, Finance, Accounting or Management.
2. Prepare your documents and describe your research interests.
3. Apply on the **official Berlin School of Economics application portal**.
4. Enter your **two referees**. They must upload their letters by **19 January 2027**.

## Key dates

- **15 January 2027, 23:59 CET:** application deadline
- **19 January 2027:** reference letter deadline
- **3–19 March 2027:** interviews
- **End of March 2027:** decisions
- **1 October 2027:** programme starts

For admission questions, contact **bsoe.admission@hu-berlin.de**.

## Tips

- Show strong maths, statistics and econometrics in your transcripts and CV.
- In your motivation letter, describe a research question that interests you and why Berlin is the right place to study it.
- Take the GRE if you can. It is not required, but a good score can strengthen your application.

Japan is one of the best places in Asia to do a PhD, with world-class universities, strong research funding and close ties to Southeast Asia. Here are **three funded PhD routes for students from ASEAN countries** for 2027.

## 1. AUN/SEED-Net PhD Scholarship in Japan

The **ASEAN University Network / Southeast Asia Engineering Education Development Network (AUN/SEED-Net)**, supported by Japan's JICA, funds PhD study at top Japanese universities in engineering, science, technology and innovation.

**What it covers:**
- All tuition and examination fees
- A monthly living allowance of **¥117,000–148,000**
- Round-trip airfare to Japan
- Outfit and moving allowances
- Up to ¥360,000 a year for research

**Who can apply:**
- Citizens of Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand or Vietnam
- Faculty, staff or outstanding students of an **AUN/SEED-Net member university**
- A master's degree (or finishing one before departure)
- **35 or younger** on arrival in Japan
- Strong English, and no other scholarship

**How to apply:** contact your university's international office or AUN/SEED-Net coordinator to get nominated, then submit your documents (transcripts, English test results and a commitment letter). Recent calls closed in **late November to mid-December**, for study starting the following autumn.

## 2. MEXT (Japanese Government) Scholarship

The **MEXT Scholarship** is Japan's main government scholarship and is open to students from **every ASEAN country**.

**What it covers:**
- Full tuition
- A monthly allowance
- Round-trip airfare

**Two ways to apply:**
- **Embassy recommendation:** apply through the Japanese embassy in your country. Applications usually open around **April**.
- **University recommendation:** apply directly to a Japanese university that offers MEXT places. Deadlines vary by university.

**Tip:** contact a Japanese professor in your field early. A supervisor's support makes a big difference.

## 3. JAIF PhD Scholarship at Vietnam Japan University

The **Japan–ASEAN Integration Fund (JAIF)** is funding new scholarships at **Vietnam Japan University (VJU)** in Hanoi, part of Vietnam National University, including PhD study for the first time.

**What it covers:**
- **Full scholarships** (tuition, airfare to Vietnam and living costs, for three years of PhD study) for students from **Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar and Timor-Leste**
- **Tuition support** for Vietnamese students
- **Internships in Japan** (about 1.5 months) for selected students

**Note:** this PhD is studied in **Vietnam**, with an internship in Japan. There are four intakes between 2026 and 2029, so check VJU's website for the next deadline.

## Tips for all applicants

- **Start now.** The AUN/SEED-Net deadline is usually in November or December.
- **Find a supervisor in Japan** whose research matches yours, and email them a short research proposal.
- **Prepare your English test** (IELTS or TOEFL) early.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a Japanese scholarship.

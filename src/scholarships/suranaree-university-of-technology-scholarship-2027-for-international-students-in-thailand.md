Suranaree University of Technology (SUT) in Nakhon Ratchasima, Thailand, offers the **Scholarship for SUT Graduate International Students** to non-Thai students who want to study for a Master's or PhD. Up to **20 scholarships** are awarded each academic year across all graduate programmes.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition waiver**, plus waivers of the university support fee and student activity fee
- **Monthly stipend of 10,000 Thai baht**, paid from your official registration date
- Funding period:
  - Master's: up to 6 trimesters (about 2 years)
  - PhD after a Master's: up to 9 trimesters (about 3 years)
  - Direct PhD after a Bachelor's: up to 15 trimesters

**Not covered:** airfare, visa costs, accommodation, health insurance and research or publication costs. On-campus housing costs roughly 4,300 baht a month.

## Eligibility

- Open to **international (non-Thai) applicants**
- A **Bachelor's degree with honours** for Master's and combined Master's/PhD programmes
- PhD applicants who hold a Master's from another university need a **GPA above 3.5** and **at least one Q1 or Q2 Scopus journal paper**
- An English test score that meets SUT's minimum requirement
- You must not hold another scholarship

## Obligations

- At least **72 hours of teaching or lab assistance** per trimester
- Keep a GPA of at least 3.25
- Before graduating:
  - Master's students must publish at least one journal paper (TCI or Scopus)
  - PhD students must publish one Scopus Q1/Q2 paper as first author

## How to apply

1. Choose a Master's or PhD programme at SUT that matches your background.
2. Contact a potential supervisor, especially for research-based programmes.
3. Prepare your transcripts, degree certificate, English test score, CV and a research proposal (for PhD applicants).
4. Apply through the SUT international graduate admission portal and pay the application fee (about USD 60).
5. Confirm with SUT's Center for International Affairs that your application is also considered for the scholarship.
6. Shortlisted candidates may have an interview or proposal presentation.

## Deadline

SUT admits students every trimester. The current round is reported to run from **2 November 2026 to 4 January 2027**. Check the official admission page for the latest dates before applying.

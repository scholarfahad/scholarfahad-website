The **European Youth Assembly (EYA) 2027** is an international youth conference organised by the **Center for International Policy and Sustainability (CIPS)**. It takes place in **Amsterdam, Netherlands, from 17 to 20 March 2027**.

The assembly brings together students, young professionals, researchers and civil society leaders for policy dialogues, workshops, networking and cultural activities.

**Please note:** this is a private conference, not an official European Union event. All applicants must **pay an application fee**, and funded places are limited.

## Participation categories

**Fully funded (30 seats)**
- Support towards a round-trip international flight
- Hotel accommodation in Amsterdam
- Breakfast during your stay
- Visa invitation letter and support
- Meals and coffee breaks during sessions
- Full access to the assembly
- Certificate of participation

**Partially funded (20 seats)**
- Hotel accommodation in Amsterdam
- Breakfast during your stay
- Visa invitation letter and support
- Meals and coffee breaks during sessions
- Full access to the assembly, a certificate and official merchandise
- **Flights are not covered**

**Free Assembly Pass**
- Access to sessions only; you cover your own travel and accommodation

## Eligibility

- Open to applicants of **any nationality**
- Generally aged **17 to 47**
- Students, graduates, young professionals, researchers, entrepreneurs, NGO and community leaders, educators and media professionals

## How to apply

1. Complete the online application form on the CIPS website.
2. Pay the application fee (the amount is shown on the application page).
3. Choose the funding category you want to be considered for.
4. Wait for the selection result by email.

## Deadline

**20 December 2026**

**Tip:** the airfare is described as "support", so it may not cover the full ticket. Check exactly what is covered before you pay any fee. Schengen visas also take time, so apply early.

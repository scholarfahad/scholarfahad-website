**Ghent University** in Belgium is accepting applications for its **Special Research Fund (BOF) Doctoral Scholarships for Candidates from the Global South**, call 2027. **15 PhD scholarships** are available in **any field of research**.

## How the scholarship works ("sandwich" PhD)

- The PhD lasts **48 months** in total
- **Ghent University funds 24 months**, and a **partner institution in your home country funds the other 24 months** (this co-funding is required)
- You start and finish with a research stay of **3 to 12 months at Ghent**, with at least **24 months of research at home** in between
- The first stay in Ghent must begin between **1 October 2027 and 28 February 2028**
- You receive a **joint or Ghent PhD** at the end

## What Ghent provides

- A scholarship during your research stays in Ghent
- A **bench fee of €310** for each month of research in Ghent
- An **€8,000 travel and operating budget**, managed by your Ghent supervisor

Your **home institution** must give you a full-time local scholarship or salary for 24 months and largely release you from teaching (no more than 10% of your time on other duties).

## Eligibility

- **Citizenship** of, and coming from, a country on Ghent's **Global South country list for 2027** (check the official list)
- At the **start of your PhD**, with at least **36 months of doctoral research left** on 1 October 2027
- Applying as a degree student for a PhD or joint PhD at Ghent (exchange students cannot apply)
- A **Ghent University professor** who agrees to supervise you
- A **supervisor at your home or partner institution** who will confirm funding in writing

Each Ghent supervisor can support at most 2 candidates per call, and they must be of different genders.

## Required documents

- Application form (in English), completed by the candidate
- Written statement from your partner institution confirming 24 months of funding and teaching release
- Advice on the candidate from your Ghent supervisor
- Ethics and biosafety advice from your Ghent supervisor
- Exception request form (only if needed)

## How to apply

1. Download the call documents and check that your country is on the 2027 list.
2. Contact Ghent University professors in your field and get one to agree to supervise you.
3. Arrange the 24-month funding and teaching release with your home institution.
4. If you need an exception to the funding or mobility rules, email BOF@ugent.be by **15 January 2027**.
5. Email your completed application to **BOFapplication@ugent.be** by **9 February 2027**. Your Ghent supervisor submits their advice documents by the same date.

## Key dates

- Exception requests: **15 January 2027**
- Application deadline: **9 February 2027**
- Results: from **12 May 2027**
- Start: **1 October 2027**

The **Nusuk Challenge 2026** is a free competition run by Saudi Arabia's **Ministry of Hajj and Umrah** through **Nusuk**, the official app for planning Umrah and visiting the Two Holy Mosques. Every day, users answer a short Islamic knowledge question for a chance to win an **Umrah trip** or **Zamzam water**.

## Prizes

- **100 Umrah trips**, each for the **winner and one companion**
- **10,000 cartons of Zamzam water**

In last year's edition, the Umrah prize included flights, accommodation and the visa. The full 2026 prize details have not been confirmed, so read the terms inside the app.

## Who can take part

- Anyone who downloads the **official Nusuk app**
- Saudi citizens, residents **and people living abroad**
- If you don't have a Saudi visa, you can sign in using the **"As a Guest"** option
- **Free.** There is no fee to take part

## How to enter

1. Download the **official Nusuk app** from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
2. Create an account or sign in (you can use "As a Guest").
3. Open the **Nusuk Competition** in the app.
4. Use your **daily key** to open a "door" and choose a topic:
   - Holy Quran
   - The Prophet's biography (Seerah)
   - Makkah and the Grand Mosque
   - Madinah and the Prophet's Mosque
5. **Answer the question correctly** to enter that day's draw.
6. Come back **every day** for a new key. You can also earn extra keys by inviting others with your own link in the app.

Winners are announced regularly in the app and on Nusuk's official X account (**@MyNusuk**).

## Dates

The challenge **started on 1 November 2026**. The end date has not been officially announced, so take part every day while it is running.

## Stay safe

- Use **only the official Nusuk app**. Do not download copies from links on social media.
- **Never pay** anyone for "keys", "answers" or a "guaranteed win".
- Do not send your passport, bank details or one-time codes to anyone on social media.
- Check winners and announcements only through the app and Nusuk's official accounts.

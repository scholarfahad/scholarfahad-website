**Anthony Udokwu's Foundation–Yahweh**, a registered charity in England and Wales (Charity No. 1214603), helps Nigerian students who face serious barriers to finishing their education. Its **2028 University Scholarship** is now open.

This scholarship is for **Nigerian university students facing real hardship**. Being a student alone does not make you eligible.

## Who can apply

You must be a **university student in Nigeria** and fit at least **one** of these groups:

1. **Students living with a disability**
2. **Orphans:** usually students who have lost both parents. A student who has lost one parent may qualify if the surviving parent has abandoned them or gives no real support.
3. **Students in critical circumstances:** abuse, exploitation, serious neglect, abandonment, or unsafe living conditions affecting their education or safety

## What you'll need

- Your **university, course and matriculation number**
- Your **annual school fees** (in naira)
- Details of **who currently pays your fees**
- Contact details of someone who can **verify** your situation, such as a lecturer, pastor or community leader

**Do not upload** medical, orphanhood or abuse-related documents at the first stage.

## How to apply

1. Go to the official **[2028 Scholarship application page](https://www.anthonyudokwufoundationyahweh.co.uk/2028-Scholarship/)**.
2. Complete the **eligibility check**.
3. Enter your personal and university details, using an email address you can access.
4. Explain your **financial situation** and who pays your fees.
5. Answer the **ten ethical scenario questions** (a score of 80 or more is recorded as passed, but a lower score doesn't automatically disqualify you).
6. Complete the **verification and declaration** section.
7. Click **"Submit 2028 Scholarship Application"**, then keep your reference and track progress in the Foundation Records Portal using the same email.

## Deadline

**28 February 2027**

## Important

- Applying is **free**
- Only **one application per email address**
- **Never share your bank details** during the application
- Apply only through the foundation's official website

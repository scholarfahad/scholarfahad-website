The **Ruth Titilayo Educational Foundation (RTEF)** supports Nigerian students who need help paying for their education. Its **2027 scholarships** are open across five categories, from secondary school to postgraduate study.

This scholarship is open to **Nigerian students only**.

## What the scholarship covers

- A **tuition allowance** of up to **₦100,000**
- A **maintenance allowance** of up to **₦20,000**
- **Travel or fieldwork support** for some awards, at the committee's discretion

The award may cover all or part of your school costs.

## Scholarship categories

1. **Secondary School Scholarship:** for students currently in secondary school
2. **Undergraduate Scholarship:** for students at federal or state universities in Nigeria
3. **Polytechnic Scholarship:** for students at polytechnics
4. **Postgraduate Scholarship:** for master's or PGD students at Nigerian public universities
5. **Technical and Vocational Scholarship:** for students on skill-based courses at technical or vocational institutions

## Eligibility highlights

**Undergraduates:**
- Living in Nigeria
- **Under 25** years old
- A current student, including final-year students
- A minimum **GPA of 4.0 on a 5.0 scale** (or equivalent)
- Studying at a federal or state university

**Postgraduates:**
- Nigerian citizens
- A **First Class or Second Class Upper** degree from a Nigerian public university
- An admission letter for a master's or PGD at a Nigerian public university
- Genuine financial need

Check the official page for your category for its full requirements.

## How to apply

1. Go to the official **[Ruth Titilayo Foundation scholarships page](https://ruthtitilayofoundation.org/scholarships/)**.
2. Choose your **category** and read its requirements.
3. Complete the **online application form**.
4. Submit before **31 October 2026**.

## Selection

Shortlisted applicants are contacted by the foundation and invited for an **interview**. Awards are made after the interviews.

## Key date

**Application deadline: 31 October 2026**

## Tips

- **Apply today.** The deadline is very close.
- **Check your GPA** and make sure you meet the minimum for your category.
- **Explain your financial need clearly** and honestly.
- **Apply only on the official website**, and never pay anyone to apply for you.

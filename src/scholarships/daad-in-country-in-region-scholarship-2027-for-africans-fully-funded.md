The **DAAD In-Country/In-Region Scholarship Programme** is funded by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) and the German government. It helps talented graduates from **Sub-Saharan Africa** study a master's degree at strong universities **in Africa**, either in their home country or in another African country.

- **In-Country:** you study in your home country (or the country where you have residence)
- **In-Region:** you study in another Sub-Saharan African country

The programme aims to train the next generation of African lecturers and researchers.

## What the scholarship covers

**All scholars:**
- **Tuition fees**
- **Monthly stipend**
- **Study and research allowance**
- **Thesis printing and publication costs**

**In-Region scholars also get:**
- **Travel allowance**
- **Health insurance**

**Duration:** usually up to 2 years

## Host universities and programmes (this call)

- **Burkina Faso:** 2iE – MSc Water and Environmental Engineering
- **Ghana:** WACCI, WACCBIP and University for Development Studies (UDS) – MPhil programmes
- **Ethiopia:** Jimma University – MSc Environmental Health
- **Kenya:** Chuka University – MSc Plant Breeding; Moi University – MEd in Research
- **Rwanda:** University of Rwanda – MSc Electrical Power Systems
- **Tanzania:** Ardhi University – MSc Geomatics
- **Uganda:** Busitema University – MSc Climate Change and Disaster Management
- **Malawi:** MUBAS – MSc Environmental Health; LUANAR – MSc Aquaculture and Fisheries Science, MSc Irrigation Engineering

Each programme has its own call document with specific requirements.

## Who can apply

- You are a national (or recognised refugee) of a **Sub-Saharan African country**
- You hold a **first university degree** (bachelor's) with good results
- You plan a career in **teaching and/or research** at a university in Sub-Saharan Africa
- **Women**, **recognised refugees** and people from **marginalised regions or groups** are especially encouraged to apply

## How to apply

1. **Choose a host programme** from the list above that matches your background.
2. **Download and read its call document** from the official DAAD listing. It explains the documents and application method.
3. **Prepare your documents.** These usually include certificates, transcripts, a CV, a motivation letter and recommendation letters.
4. **Apply** exactly as the call document says, before the deadline.

## Deadline

**12 November 2026.** Some host programmes may have their own dates, so always check the specific call.

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, explain how the degree will help you become a lecturer or researcher in Africa.
- Choose a programme closely linked to your first degree.
- Start now. The deadline is very close.

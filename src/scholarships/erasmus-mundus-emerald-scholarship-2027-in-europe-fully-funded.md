**EMerald** is an **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in Georesources Engineering**, funded by the European Union. It trains engineers to find, extract, process and recycle the **minerals and raw materials** the world needs for clean energy, electronics and industry.

## Partner universities and route

| Semester | University |
|---|---|
| 1 | **University of Liège (ULiège)**, Belgium |
| 2 | **University of Lorraine**, Nancy, France |
| 3 | Specialisation: **Luleå University of Technology**, Sweden (primary raw materials) **or** **TU Bergakademie Freiberg**, Germany (secondary raw materials and recycling) |
| 4 | Master's thesis at any partner university, often with industry |

**Duration:** 2 years (120 ECTS), starting in **September 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers

The **Erasmus Mundus scholarship** includes:
- **EUR 1,400 per month** for 24 months
- **Full tuition** (participation costs)
- **Insurance**

Students without a scholarship pay about **EUR 8,000 per year** (non-EU).

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- One of these:
  - An **engineering bachelor's** (at least 3 years / 180 ECTS) with basic geology knowledge
  - A bachelor's in **Minerals, Mining, Chemical, Geological or Metallurgical Engineering**, or **Materials Engineering** focused on metals
  - A **master's in Geology**
- At least **22.5 ECTS in university-level mathematics**
- **English at B2–C1 level**: **TOEFL 87+, IELTS 6.5+ or Duolingo 120+**

## Documents you need (in English)

- Passport or ID copy
- One-page **CV**
- **Motivation letter**
- **Recommendation letter**
- High school and university diplomas and transcripts, with sworn English translations
- **English test** result
- A short **motivation video**
- If you were not at university in the last 5 years: a record of what you did

Incomplete applications are not considered.

## How to apply

1. Check that your degree and maths credits meet the requirements.
2. Prepare all documents and record your motivation video.
3. Apply online through the **EMerald website** before the deadline.

## Key dates

- **3 November 2026:** scholarship applications open
- **28 February 2027:** scholarship deadline
- **16 April 2027:** results
- **September 2027:** programme starts

## Tips

- In your motivation letter and video, explain why raw materials matter for the energy transition and for your country.
- Highlight any mining, geology, lab or field experience.
- Make sure your transcript clearly shows your maths courses.

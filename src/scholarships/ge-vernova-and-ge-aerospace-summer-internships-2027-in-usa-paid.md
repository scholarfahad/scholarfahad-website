**General Electric (GE)** split into three companies in 2024: **GE Aerospace** (jet engines), **GE Vernova** (power, wind and grid technology) and **GE HealthCare**. Each company offers **paid summer internships** for university students. Many summer 2027 roles are already posted.

## Example roles (Summer 2027)

- **GE Vernova Gas Power** engineering, manufacturing and supply chain internships (Greenville, South Carolina, and Schenectady, New York)
- **GE Vernova Advanced Research** internship for master's students (Niskayuna, New York)
- **GE Aerospace** engineering, software and project management internships
- **Edison Engineering Development Program (EEDP)**, a graduate programme for engineers starting in 2027

## Pay and benefits

- **USD 24–35 per hour** for many engineering and supply chain internships
- **USD 1,000–2,000 per week** for some master's-level research internships
- **Relocation or housing allowance** for some interns who live far from the job location
- Real projects, mentoring and a possible route to a full-time job

Most internships run from **May/June to August 2027**, full-time.

## Who can apply

- **Full-time students** in engineering, computer science, science or a related field (bachelor's or master's, depending on the role)
- A minimum **GPA of 3.0** (or 4.0 out of 5.0)
- You must **already be allowed to work in the United States** without sponsorship. **GE does not offer visa sponsorship** for these roles.

This means these internships are mainly for students studying in the USA. International students at US universities should check whether their work permission (such as CPT) is accepted for each role.

## How to apply

1. Go to the **GE Vernova careers** site (careers.gevernova.com) or **GE Aerospace careers** site (careers.geaerospace.com).
2. Search for **"Intern"** or **"Summer 2027"**.
3. Read each role's requirements and location carefully.
4. Apply online with your **CV** (and transcript if asked).

## Deadlines

Each internship has its **own deadline**, and many roles stay open only until they are filled. **Apply as early as possible.**

## Tips

- Tailor your CV to each role and highlight relevant projects, labs and software skills.
- Keep your GPA strong. Many roles ask for 3.0 or higher.
- Look out for GE internships in your own country on the same careers sites. Some are posted outside the USA.

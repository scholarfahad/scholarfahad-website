The **German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)** offers **RISE Germany** (Research Internships in Science and Engineering) to undergraduate students who want hands-on research experience in Germany. Interns work with PhD students at top German universities and research institutes during the summer.

**Important:** you must be studying at a university in the **United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or Ireland**. Students at universities in other countries are not eligible.

## What the internship offers

- **€992 monthly stipend** (US, UK and Irish students)
- **International travel subsidy**
- **Health, accident and personal liability insurance**
- **Help finding housing** from the host institution
- An invitation to the RISE meeting in Heidelberg in July 2027, with a €160 stipend

**Canadian students** are funded through Mitacs with **CAD 7,000** to cover travel and living costs.

The internship lasts **up to 3 months**, starting from 15 May 2027 at the earliest.

## Eligibility

- Currently enrolled in a **bachelor's degree** at a university in the US, Canada, the UK or Ireland
- Studying **biology, chemistry, computer science, physics, earth sciences, engineering** or a closely related field
- **International students** must have studied at that university for **more than 12 months** by the deadline
- At least **2 years of your degree completed** by the start of the internship, and still an undergraduate afterwards
- German citizens are eligible only if they have lived outside Germany for 6 years or more
- No German is needed; the working language is English

## Required documents

- A motivation letter for **each project** you apply to
- CV
- A one-page list of courses you'll have completed by the start date
- Your academic transcript
- An enrolment certificate showing your expected graduation date (stamped and signed)
- A reference letter from a professor with a PhD, submitted online by the professor

## How to apply

1. Create a DAAD ID and search the **RISE Germany internship database** for projects that match your field.
2. Apply online to the projects you like, with a separate motivation letter for each.
3. Upload your documents by **30 November 2026**.
4. Ask your professor to submit the reference letter by **15 December 2026**.
5. Placements are announced in **February or March 2027**.

## Deadline

**30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Central European Time (CET)**

**Sabancı University** is one of Türkiye's leading private research universities, located in **Istanbul**. It offers generous scholarships to graduate students, including **international students**, for its academic **master's and PhD programmes**. According to the university, **almost all students admitted to academic graduate programmes receive full tuition exemption**.

> **Status:** applications for **Fall 2027** haven't opened yet. For Fall 2026, the deadlines were **27 March 2026** (early round for Economics) and **8 to 15 June 2026** for most programmes. Expect a similar timeline, and start preparing now.

## What the Full Scholarship covers

- **100% tuition exemption.** International tuition is **US$24,500 a year** (2026–27), so this is a big saving.
- A **monthly stipend** (2026–27 rates, paid in Turkish lira):
  - **Master's:** TL 43,500 a month (10 months a year)
  - **PhD before the qualifying exam:** TL 67,500 a month (12 months)
  - **PhD after the qualifying exam:** TL 75,000 a month (12 months)
- **Free dormitory accommodation** (a shared room for master's students, usually a single room for PhD students), or **transport support** if you live off campus
- **Private health insurance**
- A **laptop**
- Limited **conference support**

Other scholarship types include **project-funded** scholarships (with a higher stipend), a **Distinguished Science and Technology Scholarship** for top PhD students, and **tuition waiver** scholarships. Stipend amounts are usually updated each year.

## Who can apply

- **Turkish and international applicants** to Sabancı's **academic** (not professional) master's and PhD programmes
- A **bachelor's degree** for master's programmes, or a **master's degree** for PhD programmes
- Strong grades and English proficiency, as each programme requires
- Scholarships are awarded on **academic merit** during the admission evaluation

## Programmes include

**Engineering and Natural Sciences:** Computer Science and Engineering, Cyber Security, Data Science, Electronics Engineering, Energy Technologies and Management, Industrial Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Materials Science and Nano Engineering, Mathematics, Mechatronics Engineering, Molecular Biology, Genetics and Bioengineering, and Physics

**Arts and Social Sciences:** Cultural Studies, Economics, History, Political Science, Psychology, and Visual Arts and Visual Communication Design

## How to apply

1. **Choose your programme** and check its requirements on Sabancı's website.
2. **Apply online** at admission.sabanciuniv.edu when the Fall 2027 call opens.
3. Upload your documents. Shortlisted candidates may be invited to an **interview** (online, in person or hybrid).
4. The **admissions jury recommends scholarships** while it evaluates applications, so there's no separate scholarship form for most awards.

## Documents you'll usually need

- **Transcripts** and **degree certificates**
- **Statement of purpose**
- **Reference letters**
- **English test results** (such as TOEFL or IELTS), if required
- **CV**
- **GRE**, for some programmes

## Tips

- **Prepare now.** Request reference letters and book your English test before the call opens.

- **Watch for the early deadline.** Some programmes, like Economics, have an earlier round.

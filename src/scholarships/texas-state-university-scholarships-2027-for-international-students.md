**Texas State University**, in San Marcos between Austin and San Antonio, offers generous merit scholarships to new first-year students, and **international students are eligible**.

## Assured Scholarships (for students with SAT or ACT scores)

| Scholarship | Per year | Total (4 years) | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| President's Honor | $12,000 | $48,000 | SAT 1420+ / ACT 32+ and top 25% of class (or IB/AP Capstone) |
| Achievement | $8,000 | $32,000 | SAT 1260–1410 / ACT 27–31 and top 25% (or IB/APCD) |
| Achievement | $8,000 | $32,000 | SAT 1160–1250 / ACT 24–26 and top 5% (or IB/APCD) |
| Achievement | $7,000 | $28,000 | Top 6–15% of class |
| Achievement | $6,000 | $24,000 | Top 16–25% of class |
| Distinguished | $5,000 | $20,000 | SAT 1160+ / ACT 24+ and top 26–30% |
| Distinguished | $4,000 | $16,000 | Top 31–35% of class |
| Distinguished | $4,000 | $16,000 | SAT 1060–1150 / ACT 21–23 and top 25% (or IB/APCD) |

## Merit Scholarship (no SAT/ACT needed)

- For students **without** SAT or ACT scores who are in the **top 35%** of their graduating class (or IB/AP Capstone)
- Amount varies, based on an academic review and available funding
- Students are usually told within two weeks of acceptance

## Pay Texas resident tuition

If you receive **at least US$1,000** in Texas State competitive or merit scholarships, you may qualify for a **non-resident tuition waiver**, which means paying the much lower Texas resident tuition rate.

## Keeping your scholarship

- A **3.25 cumulative GPA** at Texas State
- Complete **28 credit hours** a year
- Remain an undergraduate without a bachelor's degree

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to Texas State University for fall 2027.
2. **Send your SAT or ACT scores** and your school's class rank, if available.
3. **Apply through BOSS** (Bobcat Online Scholarship System) for extra competitive scholarships.
4. Scholarships are awarded after admission, **subject to funding**, so apply early.

## Tips

- **Take the SAT or ACT** if you can. The best awards depend on test scores.
- **Ask your school** for a letter confirming your class rank or position.
- **Apply early.** Merit awards are limited by funding.
- Applying for these scholarships is free. Never pay anyone who promises an award.

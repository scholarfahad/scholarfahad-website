The **University of Manitoba Graduate Fellowship (UMGF)** is the university's main merit award for graduate students. It supports outstanding students in **research-based master's and PhD programmes** at the **University of Manitoba** in **Winnipeg, Canada**, and it is open to students of **any nationality**.

## How much it pays

| Level | Amount | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **PhD** | **CAD 25,000 per year** | 12 to 48 months (up to **CAD 100,000**) |
| **Master's** | **CAD 20,000 per year** | 12 or 24 months (up to **CAD 40,000**) |

Your department decides the final amount and duration. The fellowship is paid to you, and you use it for tuition and living costs. Many departments add **research or teaching assistantship** pay on top, so ask your future supervisor about the full funding package.

Students recommended for the UMGF are also **automatically considered** for extra named awards, such as the Sir Gordon Wu Scholarship and the UM Alumni Association Fellowship.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality** (Canadian and international)
- Admitted to a **full-time, thesis-based master's or PhD** programme (a research route with a thesis, dissertation or major research project)
- A minimum admission **GPA of 3.0** (some departments ask for more)
- You must keep a GPA of 3.0, with no grade below C+, to keep the fellowship

Pre-master's, course-based and occasional students are **not eligible**.

## How to apply

There is **no separate application form** for the UMGF.

1. **Choose a research-based master's or PhD programme** at the University of Manitoba.
2. **Contact a potential supervisor** whose research matches your interests. Many departments expect this before you apply.
3. **Apply for admission** through the Faculty of Graduate Studies online portal before your department's deadline.
4. Your **department reviews applicants and nominates** the strongest ones for the UMGF.
5. If you are selected, you get an offer by email. Accept it within one month.

## Deadlines

There is no single UMGF deadline. **Each department sets its own admission deadline**, and many fall between **December and February** for a Fall 2027 start. Check your programme's page and apply early.

## Tips

- Strong grades matter most. The fellowship is merit-based.
- A supportive supervisor can help you get nominated, so reach out early with a clear research idea.
- Ask about assistantships and other awards, so you know the full amount you will receive each year.

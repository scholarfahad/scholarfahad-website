Google is giving eligible university and college students in **27 African countries** a free **12-month Google AI Plus** subscription through its Gemini student offer. The offer was announced on 20 August 2026, and students who claimed last year's offer can renew for another year.

## What you get with Google AI Plus

- **Gemini Omni**, Google's latest AI model, in the Gemini app
- **Twice the usage limits** in Gemini
- **Deep Research**, for detailed reports on any topic, now also in Gemini Live
- **Gemini Live** for real-time voice conversations
- **Study tools:** study notebooks, flashcards and custom practice quizzes
- **Interactive 3D visualisations and simulations** to understand hard topics
- **400 GB of cloud storage** shared across Google Drive, Gmail and Google Photos
- A discounted bundle with YouTube Premium (up to 70% off)

## Eligible countries

Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cabo Verde, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Kenya, Mali, Mauritius, Mozambique, Namibia, Niger, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

## Who can claim

- Students **18 years or older**
- Currently enrolled at an accredited **university, polytechnic, college of education or other higher institution** in an eligible country
- A personal Google account
- Students who claimed last year's offer can **renew for another 12 months** after re-verifying their student status

## How to claim

1. **Go to Google's student offer page** ([one.google.com/ai-student](https://one.google.com/ai-student)) and sign in with your personal Google account.
2. **Click "Claim your offer".**
3. **Verify your student status** through SheerID. You may need to enter your school name and upload proof of enrolment, such as a student ID card, an admission letter or a fee receipt.
4. **Activate Google AI Plus** once you're verified, then open the Gemini app to start using it.

## Tips

- Use a document that clearly shows **your name, your school and the current academic year**, so verification goes smoothly.
- If verification fails, check that your photo is clear and try again with a different document.
- **Set a reminder** for when your free year ends, in case Google asks you to choose a paid plan or cancel.
- The offer is from Google directly. **Never pay anyone** to "activate" it for you or share your Google password with anyone.

## How to use it for your studies and scholarship applications

- Use **Deep Research** to explore universities, scholarships and research topics.
- Create **practice quizzes and flashcards** from your lecture notes.
- Ask Gemini to give **feedback** on your CV or motivation letter, but always write in your own voice.
- Store your scholarship documents safely in your **400 GB of Google Drive**.

## What the scholarship covers

The scholarship package varies slightly by university but usually includes:

- **Full tuition fees**
- **Accommodation**
- **Return flights** and travel costs
- **A living stipend**
- **Books and study materials**
- **Health insurance**
- **Mentoring, leadership training and career support**
- Membership of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars network across Africa

## Who can apply

- **Citizens of African countries** (some universities also accept refugees and displaced people living in Africa)
- Young people with **strong academic results** who face **financial barriers** to education
- A clear commitment to **giving back** and creating positive change in their communities
- Meet the admission requirements of the university and programme you choose

Each university sets its own eligibility rules, such as age limits and required qualifications, so check each one carefully.

## How to apply

1. **Choose a university and programme** from the list above that fits your goals and qualifications.
2. **Visit the university's Mastercard Foundation Scholars page** (links are available from the [Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program website](https://mastercardfdn.org/scholars)).
3. **Apply for admission and the scholarship** following that university's instructions. Some universities use one combined application, while others need a separate scholarship form.
4. **Submit before the deadline.** Deadlines differ for each school, starting with UCT on 31 October 2026.

## Documents you usually need

- Academic transcripts and certificates (WAEC, NECO, KCSE, WASSCE, A-Level, or degree transcripts for graduate programmes)
- Personal statement or essays
- Recommendation letters
- Proof of financial need
- Passport or national ID
- English test results, where required

## Tips for a strong application

- **Tell your story.** Explain the challenges you've faced and how you overcame them.
- **Show leadership and service.** Give real examples of how you've helped your school, family or community.
- **Connect your studies to Africa's future.** Explain how your degree will help you make a difference at home.
- **Apply to more than one university** if you're eligible, to improve your chances.
- **Start early.** Gathering transcripts and recommendation letters takes time.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a Mastercard Foundation scholarship.

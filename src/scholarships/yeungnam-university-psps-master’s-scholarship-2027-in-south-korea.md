**Yeungnam University** in South Korea is offering scholarships for international students to study a **master's degree** at its **Park Chung Hee School of Policy and Saemaul (PSPS)**, starting in **March 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **100% tuition** for all four semesters
- **Admission fee** paid
- **Living allowance of ₩1,000,000 per month** (about US$700) for the first 12 months, March 2027 to February 2028

**Not covered:** living costs after the first year, and health insurance. Insurance is mandatory: about ₩80,000 a month for National Health Insurance, plus ₩100,000 a year for private insurance.

## Programmes

- Saemaul and International Development
- Public Policy and Leadership
- Forest and Environmental Policy. This one gives priority to applicants with backgrounds in natural sciences, agriculture, forestry or environmental engineering.

## Who can apply

- International applicants with a **bachelor's degree** in any field, or finishing one before the programme starts
- Able to attend daytime classes in Korea
- Government officials, professionals and recent graduates are all welcome

## English requirement

- **IELTS 5.5**, or TOEFL iBT 71, TOEIC 750, or CEFR B2
- **Exempt** if your country's official language is English, or your bachelor's degree was taught in English and your university provides an official statement

## Key dates

- **Online application and fee:** 23 October 2026, 2:00 PM Korea time
- **Documents by email:** 30 October 2026, 2:00 PM Korea time
- **Classes begin:** 1 March 2027

## Application fee

**₩100,000 or US$80**, non-refundable. You also pay any bank charges.

## Documents

- Application form, personal statement and statement of academic purpose (official forms)
- Degree certificate (or expected graduation letter) and transcripts. These usually need an apostille or embassy authentication.
- English test result or exemption proof
- At least one recommendation letter
- Passport copy
- Parents' nationality documents and a birth certificate or family relationship document
- Payment receipt

## How to apply

1. Choose your programme on the PSPS website: https://psps.yu.ac.kr/psps/index.do
2. Download the official forms and checklist from the admissions notice.
3. Fill in the online application and pay the fee by **23 October 2026**.
4. Email your scanned documents, in checklist order, by **30 October 2026**. Confirm the email address on the PSPS website.

Questions: psps@yu.ac.kr · +82-53-810-1318

## Keeping the scholarship

You must get a **B average or higher** each semester, with **no F grades**. If not, you pay 30% of the next semester's tuition.

## Tips

- Start getting your documents authenticated now. Apostilles take time.
- In your statement of purpose, link the programme to development or public policy work in your home country.
- Submit a few days before the deadline in case of payment or internet problems.

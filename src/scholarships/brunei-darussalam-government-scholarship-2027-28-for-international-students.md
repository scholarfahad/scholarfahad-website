Every year, the **Government of Brunei Darussalam** offers **fully funded scholarships** to international students to study at universities in Brunei, including **Universiti Brunei Darussalam (UBD)**, the country's top university.

**Status:** the 2026/27 call closed on **15 February 2026**. The 2027/28 call is expected to open around December 2026 or January 2027, with a deadline around **mid-February 2027**. Prepare now.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Free tuition**, including acceptance, registration, examination and orientation fees
- **Return economy flights** to and from Brunei
- **Free accommodation** in the university's residential college
- **Personal allowance:** BND 500 per month
- **Meals allowance:** BND 150 per month
- **Book allowance:** BND 600 per year
- **Medical insurance**
- **Baggage allowance** when you finish: BND 500 if you live outside ASEAN

These amounts are from the last call and may change.

## Universities

- **Universiti Brunei Darussalam (UBD)**
- Universiti Teknologi Brunei (UTB)
- Sultan Sharif Ali Islamic University (UNISSA)
- Politeknik Brunei

## Who can apply

- Citizens of **ASEAN, Commonwealth and OIC member countries**, among others. **Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Pakistan, Cameroon and many other countries qualify.**
- **Bachelor's and diploma:** aged 18 to 25
- **Master's:** aged 35 or younger
- Able to study in **English, Malay or Arabic**
- Brunei permanent residents are not eligible

## Documents

- Certified copies of your passport and birth certificate or national ID
- Academic certificates and transcripts, with certified English translations if needed
- English proficiency proof
- Reference letters
- Proof of employment (if working)
- A research proposal (for research master's)

Files are uploaded online: Word, PDF or JPG, 10 MB maximum.

## How to apply

1. When the call opens, fill in the **Brunei Darussalam Government Scholarship** online application, linked from the Brunei Ministry of Foreign Affairs scholarship page.
2. **Also apply for admission** to your chosen university (UBD, UTB, UNISSA or Politeknik Brunei) through its own website.
3. Shortlisted applicants may be interviewed or tested.

UBD scholarship information: https://ubd.edu.bn/admission/scholarship/

## For PhD applicants: UBD Graduate Research Scholarship (UGRS)

The government scholarship covers bachelor's and master's only. PhD applicants can apply for **UBD's own UGRS**:

- Tuition waived
- **BND 1,200 per month** for 36 months
- Free on-campus accommodation
- One return flight, plus up to BND 3,000 for research and conferences
- Requires a master's with at least an Upper Second, age 30 or under, and a strong research proposal

Details: https://ubd.edu.bn/admission/graduate/graduate-fees-funding/ugrs

## Tips

- Prepare your transcripts, certified copies and references **before** the call opens. The window is short.
- Choose your course on UBD's website early and check its entry requirements.
- Never pay an agent. Applying is free.

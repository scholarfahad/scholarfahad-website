The **One Future Conference 2027** is an international gathering of young leaders in **Toronto, Canada**, organised by One Future Conference Inc. Participants from around the world come together for four days of talks, workshops and networking on leadership, innovation and global challenges. This is the conference's **third edition**, with about **300 participants** expected.

## Dates and location

**12–15 May 2027**, Toronto, Canada

## Funded seats

There are **50 funded seats** in total, and one application covers both categories.

### Fully funded (25 seats)
- **Round-trip international airfare support**
- **3 nights' accommodation**
- Full conference participation
- Daily meals and coffee breaks
- Certificate of participation
- Official conference merchandise
- **Visa invitation letter** and support

### Partially funded (25 seats)
- Everything above **except airfare**

The remaining participants attend on a self-funded basis.

## Application fee

Applying for a funded seat costs **CAD 24.99** (non-refundable). Paying the fee does not guarantee a funded place. Decide whether it is worth it for you before you apply.

## Who can apply

- People from **any country**
- Aged **17 to 47**
- From any academic or professional background
- Interested in leadership, innovation and global issues

## How to apply

1. Visit the official One Future Conference website.
2. Fill in the **funded application form**. Write strong, honest answers about your leadership experience and goals.
3. Pay the **CAD 24.99** application fee.
4. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**15 November 2026**

## Important to know

- **A visa is not guaranteed.** The organisers can give you an invitation letter, but the Government of Canada makes the final visa decision. Canadian visitor visas can be hard to get, so check the requirements for your country first.
- Only 25 seats are fully funded, so competition is strong.
- If you are selected for a partially funded seat, you will need to pay for your own flight.

## Tips

- In your answers, give real examples of leadership: a project, club, campaign or business you started or led.
- Show what you will bring back to your community after the conference.

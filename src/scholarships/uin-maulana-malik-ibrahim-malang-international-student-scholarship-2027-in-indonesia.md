Universitas Islam Negeri (UIN) Maulana Malik Ibrahim Malang, a state Islamic university in East Java, Indonesia, is accepting applications for the **International Student Scholarship (ISS) 2027**. The scholarship is for foreign students who want to study a bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree.

## What the scholarship covers

- Registration fee
- Full tuition
- Examination fees
- Student dormitory (free accommodation)
- One semester of Indonesian language preparation (BIPA)

**Not covered:** living costs, flights and visa costs. Plan for your own monthly expenses.

## Duration

- Bachelor's: 8 semesters
- Master's: 4 semesters
- Doctoral: 6 semesters

The scholarship is reviewed every year based on your grades and conduct. You must keep a GPA of at least 3.00.

## Eligibility

- Open to foreign citizens
- Bachelor's applicants need a secondary school certificate, master's applicants a bachelor's degree, and doctoral applicants a master's degree
- Good academic results at your previous level
- No legal issues
- **Maximum age:** 22 for bachelor's, 35 for master's, 40 for doctoral
- An Arabic or English proficiency certificate (an Indonesian certificate is optional)
- Doctoral applicants must submit a research proposal

## Study programmes

- **Bachelor's:** 30 programmes, including Islamic Education, Arabic, Psychology, Management, Informatics Engineering and Civil Engineering
- **Master's:** 12 programmes, including Islamic Education, Psychology, Mathematics Education and Biology
- **Doctoral:** 6 programmes, including Islamic Family Law, Sharia Economics and Islamic Studies

## Required documents

All documents must be in English or Indonesian; translations must be done by an official or certified translator.

1. Application form
2. Passport copy
3. Last certificate and transcript
4. Recent formal photo (4x6)
5. CV
6. Motivation letter
7. Reference letter from the Indonesian embassy or consulate in your country
8. Police clearance certificate from your home country
9. Arabic or English proficiency certificate
10. Indonesian proficiency certificate (optional)
11. Certificates of academic or non-academic achievements
12. Medical certificate

## How to apply

1. Register on the UIN Malang international scholarship portal.
2. Complete the application form and upload all documents.
3. Save your proof of registration.
4. Shortlisted applicants go through a portfolio review and an online interview.
5. Check the results on the International Office website.

## Timeline

- **Application deadline:** 30 November 2026 (23:59 Western Indonesia Time, WIB)
- Desk review: 1 to 3 December 2026
- Interviews: 7 to 11 December 2026
- Results: 14 December 2026
- Visa process: January 2027
- Arrival in Indonesia: 1 to 12 February 2027

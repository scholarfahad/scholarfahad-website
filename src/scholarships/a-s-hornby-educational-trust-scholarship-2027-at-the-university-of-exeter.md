The A.S. Hornby Educational Trust, in partnership with the University of Exeter, offers **fully funded scholarships** for experienced English language teachers from developing countries to study the one-year **MEd TESOL** (Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages) in the UK.

> **Deadline:** 22 November 2026, for September 2027 entry.

## What the scholarship covers
- Full tuition fees
- A monthly living allowance for accommodation and daily costs
- Return international flights
- Visa costs
- English language test costs
- A warm clothing allowance
- A dissertation allowance

**Number of awards:** up to 10.

## Who can apply
- You **live and work** in a country on the OECD DAC list of developing countries (least developed, low income, lower middle income or upper middle income). **Nigeria is eligible.**
- At least **three years of full-time experience** teaching English (TESOL) or in related ELT work, after your first degree
- A first degree equal to a UK **upper second-class (2:1)** or higher, or a relevant postgraduate degree
- You must meet the University of Exeter's entry requirements for the MEd TESOL

**Not eligible:** people who already hold a doctorate in TESOL, applied linguistics or English, or a UK master's in these areas.

The scholarship is only for the **MEd TESOL**, not the PGCert or PGDip.

## How to apply
1. Complete the **Hornby Scholarship application form** on the University of Exeter's award page.
2. You **do not need to apply to the MEd TESOL first.** If you are shortlisted, Exeter will tell you how to apply for the programme.
3. Shortlisted candidates are interviewed online.

## Timeline
- **22 November 2026:** application deadline
- **By 5 January 2027:** shortlisting results
- **15–24 February 2027:** interviews
- **By 4 March 2027:** final results
- **September 2027:** programme starts

## Tips
- Show the impact of your teaching: who you teach, the challenges, and what you have changed.
- Explain how the MEd will help you improve English teaching in your country when you return.
- The Hornby Trust looks for teachers who will contribute to ELT in their home country, not just advance their own careers.

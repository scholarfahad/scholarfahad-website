The University of Warwick in the United Kingdom is offering **up to 80 fully funded PhD scholarships** for students starting in October 2027. Its flagship stream, the **Chancellor's Scholarship**, is open to applicants of **any nationality** in **any subject**.

You submit **one application**, and Warwick considers you for every stream you are eligible for.

## Scholarship streams

- **Faculty Chancellors' Scholarships:** up to 30 awards, open to all nationalities
- **Interdisciplinary Chancellors' Scholarships:** up to 12 awards, for interdisciplinary projects linked to a Warwick research centre
- **AHRC Doctoral Scholarships:** up to 3 awards, for arts and humanities projects
- **Doctoral Access, Pathway:** up to 3 awards, for applicants who identify as Black or of Black heritage
- **Doctoral Access, Sanctuary:** up to 2 awards, for people with UK asylum-related status
- **China Scholarship Council (CSC):** up to 30 awards, for Chinese (PRC) citizens only

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition fees** at the UK or international rate
- **Tax-free stipend** at the UKRI rate for 3.5 years (4 years for the Sanctuary stream)
- **£5,000 Research Training and Support Grant**
- Support towards UK visa costs for eligible international students
- Paid family and medical leave, plus up to 8 weeks of annual leave a year

## Eligibility

- Open to **any nationality or fee status**
- You must apply for an eligible PhD, MPhil/PhD or EngD at Warwick starting in the **2027/28 academic year** (the scholarship cannot be deferred)
- Current Warwick PhD students are not eligible
- No department nomination is needed

## How to apply

1. Choose a PhD programme at Warwick and (recommended) contact a potential supervisor.
2. **Submit your PhD course application by 7 December 2026.**
3. Wait for an email with your 7-digit applicant ID (this can take 48 hours or more).
4. Register an ITS applicant account.
5. **Complete the scholarship application by 10 December 2026** (it takes at least 36 hours, so start early).
6. Make sure your referees submit their references by 21 December 2026.

## Key dates

All deadlines are at 16:59 UK time, and late applications are not accepted.

- PhD course application deadline: 7 December 2026
- Scholarship application deadline: 10 December 2026
- References deadline: 21 December 2026
- Mid-stage notification: 25 January 2027
- Results: early March 2027

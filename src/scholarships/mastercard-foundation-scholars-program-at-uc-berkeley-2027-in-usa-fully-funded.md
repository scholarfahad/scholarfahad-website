The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of California, Berkeley** supports talented young Africans to earn a **master's degree** at one of the world's top public universities. Scholars also join a community of African leaders and take part in leadership and entrepreneurship programmes.

Berkeley plans to support **over 200 master's students** through the programme up to 2030.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Tuition** and **Berkeley student fees**
- **Housing**, **food** and other **living expenses**
- **Travel** to and from the USA
- **Visa and SEVIS fees**
- **Leadership and entrepreneurship** programmes with the scholar cohort

## Who can apply

- You are a **citizen of an African country** (including Africans who are forcibly displaced or have refugee status within Africa)
- You show **academic talent**, **leadership potential** and **community engagement**
- You have faced **significant barriers** to higher education
- You intend to **return to Africa** to contribute after your studies

**Priority goes to applicants who:**
- Have an **undergraduate degree from an African university**
- Do **not** already hold a postgraduate degree
- Have **not** benefited from the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program before

**Not eligible:**
- Dual citizens
- People with asylee, refugee or permanent resident status in the **USA, Canada, UK or EU**
- PhD and undergraduate applicants (at Berkeley, this programme is for **master's degrees only**)

## How to apply

1. Look at the **list of eligible master's programmes** on the Berkeley Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program website.
2. **Apply for admission** to one of those programmes through Berkeley's Graduate Division.
3. Apply in the programme's **first round**. Later rounds are not considered.
4. Eligible applicants are **nominated by their programme** for the scholarship. Follow any extra instructions on the scholars programme website.

## Deadline

Each master's programme has its **own deadline**. Some close as early as **15 November 2026**, so check your programme's date now.

## Tips

- In your statement of purpose, show your leadership, your community work and your plan to make an impact back home.
- Choose a programme that clearly fits your goals and background.
- Apply early. Graduate applications need references, transcripts and test results, which take time to prepare.

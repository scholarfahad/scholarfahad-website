The **Asian Cultural Council (ACC)**, a New York-based foundation, supports cultural exchange between Asia and the United States, and within Asia. Its **2027 fellowships and grants** give artists, scholars and arts professionals funding and time for open-ended research, learning and exchange in another country.

## Grant types

- **Individual Fellowship:** one to six months of self-directed research and exchange in a new country or region, for individuals or small groups. Grants of **up to US$35,000**.
- **New York Fellowship:** a six-month programme based in New York City.
- **Graduate Fellowship:** a living stipend for students from Asia taking graduate degrees in the United States.
- **Organization Grant:** support for cultural exchange projects run by organisations.

## What fellows receive

- **Grant funding** for travel and research
- Advice on logistics and research sites
- **Introductions** to ACC alumni and arts professionals
- **J-1 visa sponsorship** for those travelling to the United States
- Access to ACC's global alumni community

ACC fellowships support **research and exchange**, not production work such as touring, performing, exhibiting, publishing or making new work.

## Who can apply

- **Residents of these Asian countries and regions:** Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam
- **Or** citizens or permanent residents of the **United States**
- **Artists, scholars and arts professionals** in fields such as architecture, art history, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, museum studies, music, photography, theatre and visual art

You cannot propose travel to the place where you currently live.

## How to apply

1. Read the **2027 grant guidelines** on the [ACC website](https://www.asianculturalcouncil.org/our-work/programs/fellowships-and-grants).
2. Choose the fellowship or grant that fits you.
3. Plan your **research or exchange project**: where you'll go, who you'll meet and what you want to learn.
4. **Apply online** between 1 October and **10 November 2026**.
5. For questions, email application@accny.org.

## Tips

- **Focus on learning, not producing.** Explain what you want to research and why it matters for your work.
- **Choose one clear destination.** Proposals covering several countries or very short trips are usually less competitive.
- **Show how the exchange will shape your future practice** and benefit your community.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises an ACC grant.

The **Airtel Africa Tech Fellowship** is run by the **Airtel Africa Foundation** with **Airtel Nigeria**. It supports bright Nigerian undergraduates studying technology courses, so they can finish their degrees and grow into Africa's next tech leaders.

**100 students** will be selected for the 2026/2027 fellowship.

## What the fellowship covers

- **Tuition support**
- **Stipends** for living costs
- **Mentorship** from technology professionals
- Support for **essential study needs**

Support can continue **from your current level until your final year**, as long as you meet the fellowship's conditions (for example, keeping good grades).

## Eligible courses

- Computer Science
- Information Technology
- Cybersecurity
- Systems Engineering
- Software Engineering
- Other technology-related courses

## Eligible universities (12)

- University of Lagos (UNILAG)
- University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN)
- Ahmadu Bello University (ABU)
- University of Benin (UNIBEN)
- Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU)
- University of Ilorin (UNILORIN)
- Tai Solarin University of Education (TASUED)
- University of Abuja (UNIABUJA)
- University of Uyo (UNIUYO)
- Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO)
- University of Maiduguri (UNIMAID)
- University of Ibadan (UI)

## Who can apply

- You are an **undergraduate** at one of the **12 universities above**
- You are in any year, from **100 level to final year**
- You study a **technology-related course**
- You have a **strong academic record**

Selection is done by **independent consultants**.

## How to apply

1. Check that your university and course are on the list.
2. Prepare your academic results and supporting documents.
3. Apply through the **Afara Initiative** application portal, linked from the official Airtel Africa Foundation page.
4. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**7 December 2026**

## Tips

- Keep your CGPA strong. Continued support depends on meeting the fellowship's conditions.
- In your application, show your passion for tech with real examples, such as projects you built, hackathons or coding communities.
- Never pay anyone to "process" your application.

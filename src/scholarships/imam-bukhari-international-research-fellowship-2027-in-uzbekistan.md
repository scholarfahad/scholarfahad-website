The **Imam Bukhari International Scientific Research Center** in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, is accepting applications for its **Imam Bukhari International Scholarship 2027**, a visiting research fellowship for foreign researchers.

The fellowship is for researchers who want to study the history and culture of Central Asia and the Islamic scholarly tradition. Preference goes to research on scholars such as **Imam al-Bukhari** and **Imam at-Tirmidhi**.

**Important:** this is a research fellowship, **not a degree scholarship**. It does **not** pay a stipend, tuition or travel costs.

## What the fellowship provides

- A two-room apartment in Khoja Ismoil Town, Samarkand
- Shared office space at the Center
- A visa support letter and help with the visa process
- Access to the Center's academic community and events

**Not covered:** international flights, living costs and medical insurance. You can hold this fellowship alongside another grant, stipend or sabbatical salary.

## Fellowship period

April to October 2027. The minimum stay is two months.

## Eligibility

- Foreign researchers of any nationality
- Normally a doctorate (PhD or equivalent) or postdoctoral status; PhD candidates may also apply
- Research on Central Asian history and culture, Islamic sciences, or related humanities and social sciences
- A working knowledge of English, Arabic, Russian or Uzbek (a certificate is recommended)

## Obligations

- Live in Samarkand during the fellowship
- Take part in Center events and give at least one fellows' seminar
- Acknowledge the Center in publications that come from your research

## Required documents

1. Completed application form
2. Research proposal (up to 1,000 words)
3. Up to two samples of your publications
4. Copies of your degree certificates
5. CV
6. Two recommendation letters from scientists or professors
7. A language proficiency certificate (recommended)

Scan documents at 300 dpi or higher.

## How to apply

1. Download the application form from the Center's website.
2. Prepare your research proposal and supporting documents.
3. Email your full application to the Center, or post it to the Center's Samarkand address.
4. Results are usually announced by early February 2027.

## Deadline

**20 December 2026**

The **Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society (HIIG)** in Berlin invites applications for its **Impact AI Fellowship 2027**. Fellows investigate a real AI application and assess whether it really delivers the benefits it claims for people, society or the environment.

## What you'll do

- Join a **method workshop** to learn HIIG's Impact AI assessment approach
- **Investigate a real AI case**, gathering evidence on its impact
- **Co-author a case report** that assesses the AI application against 25 criteria
- Work with researchers and fellows from around the world

## Fellowship details

- **Dates:** 16 August – 22 October 2027 (10 weeks)
- **In-person weeks in Berlin (compulsory):** 16–20 August and 4–8 October 2027
- **Other weeks:** can be remote or part-time, by agreement
- **Preparatory session:** 11 May 2027 (compulsory, online)

## Stipend and costs

- **€5,000 stipend** in total for the whole fellowship (including any tax or social security costs in your home country)
- **Travel and accommodation support** is considered case by case
- **Not covered:** health insurance, visa costs and meals

## Who can apply

- A **master's degree** (completed)
- **No academic publication record needed**
- An interest in AI, technology and its real impact on society
- Able to travel to **Berlin** for the two in-person weeks
- Open to **all nationalities**

## Documents you need

Combine everything into **one file**:

1. **CV** (up to four pages), required
2. **Motivation letter** (up to two pages), required
3. **AI project description** (up to two pages), optional: an AI application you would like to investigate

## How to apply

1. Read the call and FAQ on the [official HIIG page](https://www.hiig.de/en/impact-ai-call-for-fellows/).
2. Join an **online information session** (no registration needed): **10 November 2026 (10 am)** or **17 November 2026 (3 pm)**, Berlin time.
3. Prepare your CV and motivation letter, plus the optional project description.
4. Submit your single file through the online form on the HIIG page by **15 January 2027**.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Online info sessions | 10 and 17 November 2026 |
| Application deadline | **15 January 2027** |

## Tips

- **Suggest a real AI case** you know well, such as a tool used in health, agriculture, education or finance in your country.
- **Show your research and analytical skills** in your CV and letter, even without publications.
- **Plan your visa early** if you need a Schengen visa for Germany.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone to apply for you.

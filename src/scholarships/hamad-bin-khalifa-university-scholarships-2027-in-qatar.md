**Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU)** in Doha, Qatar, is offering scholarships to master's and PhD students for the **Fall 2027** intake. International students can receive a **monthly stipend**, a **tuition waiver** and a **return air ticket**.

There is **no separate scholarship form**. You are considered for funding automatically when you apply for admission.

## What the scholarship covers

| | International PhD | International Master's |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly stipend | QAR 9,000 | QAR 7,000 |
| Duration | up to 45 months | up to 21 months |
| Tuition waiver | 100% | 75% (STEM) or 60% (humanities, social sciences, policy, Islamic studies) |

- One round-trip air ticket for international students (arrival and return after graduation)
- The stipend covers living costs, including housing and meals. HBKU does not pay housing separately.

**Important:**

- Stipends are given to full-time students who work as **research or teaching assistants** (about 10 hours a week).
- Funding depends on merit and available funds. It is **not guaranteed**.
- Master's students pay the remaining 25–40% of tuition. LL.M., MS Economics and certificate programmes get **no tuition waiver**.

## Eligibility

- A bachelor's degree for master's programmes, or a master's degree for PhD programmes
- Minimum **GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0**, or equivalent
- **IELTS 6.5** or **TOEFL 79**
- GRE: not required for most master's programmes. Required for some PhD programmes (for example, Sustainable Energy and Sustainable Environment).

Requirements vary by college, so check your programme's page.

## Colleges

- Science and Engineering
- Health and Life Sciences
- Islamic Studies
- Humanities and Social Sciences
- Law
- Public Policy

## Documents

- Online application form (fee: **QAR 250**, non-refundable)
- Transcripts and degree certificates, with an explanation of the grading system
- IELTS or TOEFL score, and GRE if required
- Two recommendation letters (at least one academic)
- Personal statement (300–500 words)
- CV
- Passport copy

All documents must be in PDF.

## Key dates

- **Applications open:** 1 October 2026
- **Deadline for international applicants:** 1 February 2027
- **Qatari and resident applicants seeking funding:** 15 March 2027

## How to apply

1. Choose your programme on the HBKU website and read its admission requirements.
2. Apply online through the HBKU application portal: https://hbkuqa.elluciancrmrecruit.com/Apply
3. Upload your documents and pay the QAR 250 fee.
4. You are considered for scholarships automatically. Funding decisions are sent by email.

## Tips

- Apply early. Funding is limited and competitive.
- Make your personal statement specific: which faculty member's research matches yours, and why HBKU.
- PhD applicants: contact a potential supervisor before applying.
- If you're applying for a master's, budget for the tuition the waiver doesn't cover.

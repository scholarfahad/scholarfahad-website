DAAD scholarships don't always mean studying in Germany. Through its **In-Country/In-Region Scholarship Programme**, the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) funds talented Sub-Saharan Africans to study for a **master's degree at selected universities in Africa**, either in their **home country** (in-country) or in **another Sub-Saharan African country** (in-region).

Right now, **13 master's programmes** are accepting applications for the **2027 intake**.

> **Deadline: 12 November 2026.** Each programme has its own call document with its exact requirements and steps. Read the call for your programme carefully before applying.

## What the scholarship covers

According to DAAD, scholarship holders receive:

- A **monthly scholarship payment** (stipend)
- **Tuition fees**
- An **annual research allowance**
- A possible **research stay abroad**, subject to funding and a successful application

## Who can apply

- **Citizens of Sub-Saharan African countries**
- Holders of a **first academic degree** (bachelor's or equivalent) in a relevant field
- **Women** and candidates from **less privileged regions or groups** are especially encouraged
- Each programme sets extra requirements (grades, subject background, work experience and so on). Check its call.

## How to apply

1. **Choose a programme** that matches your first degree and career goals.
2. **Open its call PDF** and read the eligibility rules, required documents and application steps.
3. **Apply as the call instructs.** DAAD applications are made through the **DAAD portal**, and some programmes also require a separate application to the university.
4. Submit **before 12 November 2026**.

## Documents you'll usually need

Each call has its own list, but expect:

- **DAAD application form** (online)
- **CV**, often in Europass format
- **Motivation letter**
- **Reference letters**
- **Certified degree certificates and transcripts**
- **Proof of admission or application** to the university, if required

## Tips for a strong application

- **Pick a programme connected to your work or community.** DAAD funds development-related fields, so show how your study will help your country.
- **Write a focused motivation letter** for each programme. Read our [motivation letter guide](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/).
- **Start early.** Certified documents and reference letters take time.

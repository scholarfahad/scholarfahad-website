The Hanns Seidel Foundation (Hanns-Seidel-Stiftung) is a German political foundation based in Munich. Through its scholarship programme, it supports talented, socially engaged students and doctoral candidates, **including international students at German universities**. The money does not have to be repaid.

> **Important:** This is not for students who are still applying from home without a university place. You need to be **admitted to or studying at a German university**, and you need **good German (B2)**.

## What the scholarship gives you
- **Students:** up to **€885 a month**, plus a **€300 study allowance**
- **Doctoral candidates:** up to **€1,650 a month**, plus a **€100 research allowance**
- Seminars on leadership, key skills and intercultural competence
- A lifelong network of scholars and alumni

Amounts are set for each scholar. These are the maximum rates on the foundation's website. Ask the foundation what applies to international students.

## Who can apply

**International students (bachelor's and master's)**
- Under **45** years old
- Studying **full time** at a state or state-recognised university in **Germany**
- German at **B2 level** or higher
- Strong academic results and **social, community or political engagement**

**International doctoral candidates**
- Admitted to a state or state-recognised university or research institute in Germany
- Adequate German
- Your PhD must be carried out and submitted at a German university

## Documents
- CV and a statement of your study and career goals, **written in German**
- Certificates and transcripts (English is accepted for international applicants)
- Academic references (English accepted)
- Proof of German language level
- Proof of volunteering or community involvement

## How to apply
1. **Get your place** at a German university (or admission as a doctoral candidate).
2. **Check for a Hanns Seidel office in your country.** If there is one, contact it first. If not, apply online.
3. **Apply online** at [stipendienbewerbung.hss.de](https://stipendienbewerbung.hss.de) during the application window.
4. Make sure your references arrive before the deadline.

## Deadlines
- **15 December 2026 – 15 January 2027** (12:00 noon, German time)
- **15 June – 15 July 2027** (12:00 noon, German time)

## Free online information sessions
The foundation runs virtual information days (in German) from 18:30 German time on **19 October**, **18 November** and **10 December**. Details are on the official page.

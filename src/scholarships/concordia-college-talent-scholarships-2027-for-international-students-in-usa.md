Concordia College, a private liberal arts college in Moorhead, Minnesota, USA, offers **talent scholarships** to first-year students with strong ability in the arts or debate. International applicants are also considered for **merit scholarships** when they are admitted.

These are **partial scholarships**, so you need a plan to cover the remaining costs.

## Talent scholarship areas

- Music
- Theatre
- Visual arts
- Speech and debate (forensics)

Each area has its own application. Some require an audition, a portfolio, a performance recording or an interview with faculty. Recipients are expected to take part in the department's ensembles, productions or events.

## Other scholarships for international students

- **International Excellence Scholarship:** $13,000 to $19,000 per year for up to 4 years, based on your academic record and awarded automatically at admission
- **Concordia Scholarship Interview:** a virtual interview that can earn extra funding
- **#YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship:** two awards each year worth at least 50% of tuition, for first-year international students of any major
- **Davis UWC Scholars:** full demonstrated need met for eligible graduates of United World College (UWC) schools
- **On-campus work:** up to 20 hours a week, about $3,600 a year

## Cost of attendance (2026/27)

- Tuition: $32,900
- Fees, housing, meals and books: about $16,260
- **Total: about $49,160 per year** (about $52,660 including personal costs and travel)

Even with scholarships, Concordia expects international families to pay **about $24,000 to $33,000 per year**.

## Eligibility

- First-year undergraduate applicants, including international students
- Talent and experience in music, theatre, visual arts or speech and debate
- Admitted to Concordia College by the talent scholarship deadline
- English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo or equivalent) for international applicants

## How to apply

1. Apply for admission to Concordia College as early as possible.
2. Complete the separate talent scholarship application for your area.
3. Prepare any audition, portfolio or performance materials.
4. Finalists are invited to an interview or audition (visual arts interviews are on 15 February 2027).
5. Check your Concordia status portal for scholarship interview invitations.

## Deadline

**1 February 2027** for talent scholarship applications. You must be admitted to Concordia by this date.

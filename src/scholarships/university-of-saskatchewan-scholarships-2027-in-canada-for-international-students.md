The **University of Saskatchewan (USask)** in **Saskatoon, Canada** is one of Canada's leading research universities. It offers several scholarships to **international students** from any country, at both undergraduate and graduate level.

## 1. University Graduate Scholarship (UGS): master's and PhD

| Level | Amount | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Master's** | **CAD 25,000 per year + tuition** | Up to 2.5 years |
| **PhD** | **CAD 30,000 per year + tuition** + CAD 5,000 travel allowance | Up to 4 years |

**Who can apply:**
- Students of **any nationality**
- **Full-time** graduate students who are admitted or being admitted to a USask master's or PhD
- Master's students within their first 36 months and PhD students within their first 48 months
- A strong academic average (about **80%** or higher)
- English proficiency

Only **some departments** offer the UGS. Check with your department.

**How to apply:**
1. Find a USask graduate programme and apply for admission.
2. Apply for the UGS on the **USask online awards portal**.
3. Upload a cover letter, an up-to-date CV, a **research outline** (maximum 500 words, including your planned start date) and transcripts from every university you attended.
4. Ask **three referees** to email confidential letters to the College of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies. Each letter should name the scholarship and the student in the first paragraph.

**Deadline:** set by each department. Check the College of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies funding page.

## 2. International Excellence Award: undergraduate

- **Value:** **CAD 10,000** (one-time), used for tuition
- **Who:** new international undergraduate students with strong grades
- **How:** you are **considered automatically** when you apply for admission

**Deadlines:**
- **15 February 2027:** apply for undergraduate admission
- **1 March 2027:** submit all documents, pay the application fee (about CAD 90) and meet the admission and English requirements

## Important to know

- The **UGS is a full funding package** for graduate students. The undergraduate award is **CAD 10,000** and does not cover the full cost of studying in Canada.
- You will need to show proof of funds for a Canadian study permit.

## Tips

- **Graduate students:** contact a potential supervisor early. A supportive supervisor helps your department nominate you.
- **Undergraduates:** apply before 15 February and send all documents by 1 March, so you are considered for every award.
- Keep your grades high. Most awards are based on academic merit.

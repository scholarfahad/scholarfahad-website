The **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in Theoretical Chemistry and Computational Modelling (TCCM)** is a 2-year master's degree funded by the European Commission. It trains students to use **quantum chemistry, molecular simulation and computer modelling** to understand and design molecules and materials. This is useful in drug design, energy, materials science and more.

## Partner universities

- **Universidad Autónoma de Madrid**, Spain (coordinator)
- **University of Barcelona**, Spain
- **University of Valencia**, Spain
- **KU Leuven**, Belgium
- **University of Groningen**, Netherlands
- **Université de Toulouse III – Paul Sabatier**, France
- **University of Perugia**, Italy
- **University of Trieste**, Italy

**Structure:** you spend your first year at one university, and the second year across partner universities, including a research thesis. The programme starts in **September 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **EUR 1,400 per month** for living costs
- **Tuition and participation costs**
- **Health insurance**

About **15 scholarships** are expected each year.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- A **bachelor's degree** in **chemistry, physics, materials science** or a closely related field, completed before the programme starts
- **English at B2 level or higher**, for example **IELTS 6.5** or **TOEFL iBT around 90**. Documented study in English may be accepted.
- A real interest in **theory, simulation and computer modelling**. Computational project work helps.
- You have not received an Erasmus Mundus master's scholarship before

## Documents you need (in English)

- **Europass CV**
- Passport identity page
- Degree certificate and full transcripts
- **Motivation letter**
- **Two reference letters** from lecturers or supervisors
- English proficiency proof
- Proof of residence

## How to apply

1. Register on the **official TCCM application portal** (emtccm.org).
2. Fill in your personal and academic details.
3. Upload all your documents.
4. Give your **referees' contact details** so they can upload their letters.
5. Review and submit before the deadline.
6. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an **interview**.

## Deadline

The 2027 deadline has **not been published yet**. Last year's deadline was **2 February 2026**, so expect a date in **early February 2027**. Results usually come in spring, and the programme starts in September 2027.

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, mention any computational projects, coding (for example Python) or modelling software you have used.
- Explain clearly why theoretical and computational chemistry matters for your career goals.
- Ask your referees early so their letters arrive before the deadline.

The **Pro. Dr. Gabriel Olubunmi Fakeye (GOF) Scholarship and Bursary Award** supports undergraduate students in financial need. It is run by the **Cherubim & Seraphim Movement Church, Surulere District Headquarters, Lagos**, and has given awards for more than 10 years.

**Important:** applicants **must be members of the C&S Movement Church, Surulere District** or one of its branches.

## Awards

| Award | Amount | Number | Minimum CGPA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scholarship** | ₦300,000 each | 2 | 3.5 |
| **Bursary** | ₦100,000 each | 40 | 3.0 |

The amounts and number of awards are as reported for the 2027 session. Confirm on the official website.

## Who can apply

- A **member of the Cherubim & Seraphim Movement Church, Surulere District** or its branches
- An **undergraduate at a Nigerian public tertiary institution**
- In **financial need**
- A **CGPA of at least 3.5** for the scholarship, or **3.0** for the bursary

## Documents (upload online, max 5MB each)

1. **Admission letter**, plus your **latest result or current CGPA**, signed by an authorised officer of your institution (PDF, DOC or DOCX)
2. **Recommendation letter from your Branch Leader** (PDF, DOC or DOCX). If you can't get one, explain why.
3. **Passport photograph** (JPG, PNG or JPEG)

## Selection process

1. **Apply and submit** online before the deadline.
2. **Verification:** documents are checked with your institution and your church band captain.
3. **Exam and interview:** shortlisted applicants are invited to an examination and interview.
4. **Payment:** winners are paid directly into their bank accounts.

## Deadline

**4 December 2026**

## How to apply

1. Go to the official application website: https://gofscholarship.candsyf.org/
2. Create a profile and fill in the form.
3. Upload your documents and submit.

Questions: **gofscholarship@candsyf.org**

## Tips

- Ask your Branch Leader for the recommendation letter early.
- Get your result or CGPA signed by your faculty or department officer before you start.
- Make sure the CGPA you enter matches your signed result exactly. It will be verified.

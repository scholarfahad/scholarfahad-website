The **University of the West of England (UWE Bristol)** is recruiting a **Doctoral Researcher** to do a **fully funded PhD** on **Islamic finance**. The position is part of **MINARET**, a **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network** funded by the European Union's **Horizon Europe** programme.

As an MSCA doctoral researcher, you are **employed on a salary**, not just given a scholarship.

## The project

**Doctoral project DC9: Islamic Finance, Governance and Constitutional Legitimacy**

The research looks at how Islamic finance institutions relate to **public trust**, **economic governance** and **constitutional legitimacy**, with case studies in **Uzbekistan and Indonesia**.

You will be based in the **Bristol Research in Economics (BRE)** group at **Bristol Business School**, Frenchay campus, Bristol, UK.

## What you get

- **Monthly salary:** EUR 4,008 before tax (about GBP 3,447)
- **Mobility allowance:** EUR 489 per month (about GBP 421)
- **Family allowance:** EUR 455 per month (about GBP 391), if you have a family
- A **36-month, full-time** contract
- Training and research visits within the MINARET European network

## Who can apply

- Applicants of **any nationality**
- You must **not already hold a PhD**
- A **master's degree** in economics, economic development, development studies, behavioural science or a related field
- **IELTS 7.0** overall or equivalent
- **Mobility rule:** you must **not have lived or worked in the UK for more than 12 months in the 3 years** before your start date

**Skills needed:**
- Strong **mixed-methods** research skills (quantitative and qualitative)
- Excellent written and spoken English
- Able to work independently and in a team

**Desirable:** experience combining several disciplines.

## How to apply

1. Read the full job advert (reference **R09651**) on jobs.ac.uk or the UWE jobs website.
2. Prepare an up-to-date **CV** and a strong **cover letter** linking your skills and research interests to the project.
3. Gather your master's transcript, degree certificate and English test result.
4. Apply online through the **UWE recruitment portal** linked from the advert.

## Deadline

**4 January 2027**

## Tips

- Read about Islamic finance in Uzbekistan and Indonesia, and show in your cover letter that you understand the research questions.
- Show clear experience with both quantitative and qualitative methods, for example surveys, interviews or statistical analysis.
- Check the mobility rule carefully before you apply.

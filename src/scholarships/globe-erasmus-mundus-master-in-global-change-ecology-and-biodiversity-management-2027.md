**GLOBE**, the **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Global Change Ecology and Biodiversity Management**, trains the next generation of scientists and managers working on climate change, biodiversity loss and ecosystem protection. Students study in **Spain, Wales, Portugal and Mexico**, and the best applicants receive a **full EU scholarship**.

> **Deadline: 4 January 2027.** Applications for the **3rd edition (2027–2029)** opened on 28 September 2026. There's **no application fee**.

## Where you study

| Period | University | Country |
|---|---|---|
| Semester 1 | Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, Madrid | Spain |
| Semester 2 | Bangor University | Wales, UK |
| Summer school (3 weeks) | Universidad Autónoma de Tlaxcala | Mexico |
| Semester 3 | Universidade de Lisboa | Portugal |
| Semester 4 | Master's thesis at any partner or associated partner | — |

The programme is worth **120 ECTS** over two years.

## What the scholarship covers

Erasmus Mundus scholarships go to a selection of the top-ranked admitted applicants, and **all nationalities can apply**. Scholarship holders receive, for the **full 24 months**:

- **All participation (tuition) fees** covered
- A **monthly stipend of €1,400** for living costs, accommodation and travel

Students who have already held an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master scholarship can't receive another.

**Self-funded places** are also available. According to the programme's cost information (listed for the previous edition), fees are €4,500 a year for students from Erasmus+ programme countries and €9,000 a year for others.

## Who can apply

- A **bachelor's degree of at least 180 ECTS** (or equivalent) in **biology, agriculture, environmental sciences, forestry, earth and environmental geography, natural resources** or a related field
- **English at B2 level or higher** by the Stage 1 deadline, such as **IELTS 5.5**, **TOEFL iBT 72** or **Cambridge B2 (160)**
- Nationals of, or graduates from universities in, majority English-speaking countries (as defined by UK Visas and Immigration) are exempt from the English test

## How to apply

**Stage 1 (by 4 January 2027):** complete the online form with:

- Your personal and academic details
- Your English test details
- A **1–2 minute video** introducing yourself and your motivation
- Contact details for **two referees**

**Stage 2 (January–February 2027, shortlisted applicants only):** upload:

- **Passport** valid for the full two years
- **Authenticated or apostilled degree certificates**
- **Transcripts** in English
- **English test certificate**
- **Europass CV**
- **Declaration of veracity**
- **Postgraduate eligibility certificate**

All documents must be in English.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Make your video count.** Be natural, explain why ecology and biodiversity matter to you, and connect them to a real problem in your country.
- **Ask your referees early** and tell them about the programme.
- **Start authenticating your degree certificate now.** Apostille and authentication take time if you're shortlisted.
- **Apply to up to three Erasmus Mundus programmes** in the same year to increase your chances. See our [list of Erasmus Mundus scholarships open now](/scholarships/21-erasmus-mundus-scholarships-open-or-opening-soon-for-2027/).

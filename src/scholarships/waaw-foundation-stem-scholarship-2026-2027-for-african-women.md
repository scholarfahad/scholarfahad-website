The **WAAW Foundation (Working to Advance African Women)** supports young African women studying **science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM)**. Every year it awards scholarships to promising female undergraduates at universities across Africa, and asks scholars to help inspire more girls into STEM through community outreach.

The **2026/2027** applications are expected to open in **November 2026**.

## Award

- **US$500** per scholar, paid in the local currency
- Paid in **two parts**, linked to the scholar's STEM outreach activities in her community

## Who can apply

- A **female student of African origin**, living and studying in Africa
- Enrolled in a **bachelor's degree** in a STEM field
- In your **first or second year** (third year for five-year programmes)
- **Under 32** years old
- Strong academic results and **financial need**
- A commitment to **leadership and community service**

## Eligible fields

- Engineering
- Computer science and IT
- Mathematics, statistics and physics
- Environmental and agricultural sciences
- Biochemistry, pharmacy and biomedical sciences

**Not eligible:** medicine and surgery, nursing, optometry, business, social sciences and humanities, as well as master's, MBA, PhD or second degrees.

## How to apply

1. **Complete the online application form** on the WAAW Foundation website when applications open.
2. If shortlisted, **write essays** about your career goals and how the scholarship will help you.
3. **Attend an interview** and submit two reference letters (one from a lecturer and one from an academic adviser or mentor) plus your transcript and school ID.
4. Final awardees are announced.

| Stage | Date |

## Tips

- **Line up your referees now**: a lecturer and an academic adviser or mentor.
- **Show your community impact.** WAAW values girls who are already helping others into STEM.
- **Keep your transcript and school ID ready** to scan.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a WAAW scholarship.

The German Academic Exchange Service (**DAAD**) funds master's degrees at top African universities through its **In-Country/In-Region Scholarship Programme**. For the **2027 intake**, two programmes at the **University of Ghana** are open to graduates from across sub-Saharan Africa, including Nigeria.

> **Deadline:** 12 November 2026. The University of Ghana's own admission deadline may be earlier, so check the programme's call.

## Programmes at the University of Ghana

### 1. MPhil in Seed Science and Technology: WACCI
Offered by the **West Africa Centre for Crop Improvement (WACCI)**. Ideal for graduates in agriculture, crop science, plant breeding or related fields.

### 2. MPhil in Molecular Cell Biology of Infectious Diseases: WACCBIP
Offered by the **West African Centre for Cell Biology of Infectious Pathogens (WACCBIP)**. Ideal for graduates in biology, biochemistry, microbiology, medical laboratory science or related fields.

### Bonus: another Ghana option
**MPhil in Agricultural Economics** at the **University for Development Studies (UDS)**, also funded by DAAD this year.

## What the scholarship covers
- **Tuition fees**
- **Monthly scholarship** (stipend)
- **Study and research allowance**
- **Thesis publishing costs**
- **For In-Region students** (studying outside their home country, for example **Nigerians in Ghana**): **travel allowance** and **health insurance**

Funding lasts for the normal length of the master's, usually up to **two years**. The exact amounts are in each programme's official call.

## Who can apply
- Graduates from a **sub-Saharan African** country (check that your country is on the DAAD portal's list)
- A strong, relevant **bachelor's degree**
- You must meet the University of Ghana's admission and language requirements
- Preference for applicants planning a career in **teaching or research** at an African university
- **Women, refugees with recognised status, and candidates from marginalised regions or groups** are especially encouraged

## How to apply
1. Choose your programme (WACCI or WACCBIP) and read its **official DAAD call for applications**.
2. Apply on the **DAAD portal** before **12 November 2026**. If you apply for more than one DAAD programme, rank them in order of preference.
3. **Separately apply for admission** to the programme at the University of Ghana, following its instructions.
4. Use a **computer**. The DAAD portal works only partly on mobile phones.

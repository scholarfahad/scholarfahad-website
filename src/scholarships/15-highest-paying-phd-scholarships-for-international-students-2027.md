Not all fully funded PhDs pay the same. Some give you a few hundred dollars a month; others pay more than many full-time jobs. We ranked the PhD scholarships open to international students with the **highest stipends**, and checked every amount on the official page.

> **Note:** Amounts are the latest official rates and change every year. A high stipend in an expensive city (London, Singapore, Stanford) may go less far than a smaller one elsewhere. Rent matters as much as the headline figure.

## Closing soon
- **Commonwealth PhD Scholarships:** 20 October 2026 (Nigerians eligible)
- **Imperial President's PhD Scholarships:** 2 November 2026 (first round)
- **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme:** 1 December 2026

## The list

### 1. AI Singapore PhD Scholarship (NUS), Singapore
- **Stipend:** S$6,300 a month for international students
- **Also:** 4 years of subsidised tuition
- **Field:** artificial intelligence research
- **Catch:** international recipients must work for 2 years in a Singapore-based company afterwards
- **Official:** https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/ai-singapore-scholarship/

### 2. Stanford Biosciences PhD Fellowships, USA
- **Stipend:** US$58,460 a year (2026–27)
- **Also:** tuition, health and dental insurance
- **Catch:** only a limited number of fellowships for the most competitive international applicants
- **Official:** https://biosciences.stanford.edu/faqs/do-you-offer-fellowships-to-international-applicants/

### 3. Princeton University PhD Funding, USA
- **Stipend:** US$52,680 for a 12-month University Fellowship (2026–27)
- **Also:** PhD students are funded through fellowships and assistantships
- **Official:** https://gradschool.princeton.edu/financial-support/financial-support-model/funding-calendar-university-rates-costs

### 4. Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme, Hong Kong
- **Stipend:** HK$344,400 a year (about HK$28,700 a month, 2026–27)
- **Also:** HK$14,400 a year for conference and research travel; many universities add a tuition waiver or top-ups
- **Where:** 8 Hong Kong universities, including HKU, CUHK and HKUST
- **Deadline:** 1 December 2026, 12:00 noon Hong Kong time
- **Official:** https://www.ugc.edu.hk/eng/rgc/funding_opport/hkpfs/

### 5. A*STAR Computing and Information Science (ACIS) Scholarship, Singapore
- **Stipend:** S$4,550 a month for international students (NUS rate)
- **Also:** full tuition, plus book, computer, conference and thesis allowances
- **Catch:** non-Singaporeans must work in Singapore for 3 years afterwards. A*STAR's page also says non-Singaporeans need a degree from a Singapore university, so check this before applying.
- **Official:** https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/For-Graduate-Studies/A-STAR-CIS-Scholarship

### 6. Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship (NTU), Singapore
- **Stipend:** S$4,300 a month for international students
- **Also:** tuition fees covered, up to 4 years, **no bond**
- **Requirement:** a first-class honours degree or equivalent; open to all nationalities
- **Official:** https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/scholarships/npgs

### 7. Imperial College Business School PhD Scholarship, UK
- **Stipend:** £29,580 a year (2026–27), tax-free
- **Also:** full tuition waiver (including overseas fees), for up to 5 years
- **Official:** https://www.imperial.ac.uk/business-school/phd/funding-scholarships/

### 8. NUS President's Graduate Fellowship, Singapore
- **Stipend:** S$3,900 a month for international students
- **Also:** full tuition waiver, up to 4 years
- **Catch:** international students complete 312 hours of teaching duties
- **Official:** https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/presidents-graduate-fellowship

### 9. Imperial President's PhD Scholarships, UK
- **Stipend:** £27,036 a year (2027–28 rate)
- **Also:** full tuition fees for a 3.5-year PhD; open to all nationalities
- **Requirement:** first-class degree or distinction-level master's, and an agreed Imperial supervisor
- **Deadlines:** 2 November 2026, 11 January 2027 or 1 March 2027
- **Official:** https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/postgraduate-doctoral/grants-scholarships/presidents-phd/

### 10. NUS Research Scholarship, Singapore
- **Stipend:** S$3,000 a month, rising by S$500 after you pass the PhD qualifying exam
- **Also:** full tuition waiver, up to 4 years
- **Official:** https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/nus-research-scholarship

### 11. University of Sydney RTP and International Stipend, Australia
- **Stipend:** A$44,293 a year (2027 rate)
- **Also:** 100% tuition and Overseas Student Health Cover
- **Next deadline:** 18 December 2026 (for research periods 3 and 4, 2027)
- **Official:** https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/australian-government-research-training-program/rtp-international.html

### 12. Gates Cambridge Scholarship, UK
- **Stipend:** £23,152 a year (2026–27)
- **Also:** full university fees, flights at the start and end, visa costs and the Immigration Health Surcharge
- **Who:** outstanding applicants from any country outside the UK
- **Official:** https://www.gatescambridge.org/programme/the-scholarship/

### 13. Commonwealth PhD Scholarships, UK
- **Stipend:** £2,000 a month in London, or £1,712 a month elsewhere
- **Also:** full tuition, return flights, study travel grant, fieldwork flights and family allowances
- **Who:** citizens of eligible Commonwealth countries, **including Nigeria**. You apply through your national nominating agency **and** the CSC portal.
- **Deadline:** 20 October 2026, 16:00 UK time (your nominator may close earlier)
- **Official:** https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-phd-scholarships-for-least-developed-countries-and-vulnerable-states/

### 14. KAUST Fellowship, Saudi Arabia
- **Stipend:** US$25,000 to US$30,000 a year, depending on qualifications and progress
- **Also:** free campus housing, full tuition and bench fees (worth up to US$35,000 a year), health insurance and relocation support
- **Who:** every admitted KAUST PhD student receives it
- **Official:** https://admissions.kaust.edu.sa/fees-funding

### 15. Clarendon Scholarship (Oxford), UK
- **Stipend:** at least £20,780 a year (2025–26)
- **Also:** full course fees; no restrictions on nationality or subject
- **How:** no separate application; apply to your Oxford course by the December or January deadline
- **Official:** https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon

## Tips
- **KAUST** may leave you with the most money: housing is free, so most of the stipend is savings.
- **Singapore scholarships** pay well, but several come with a work bond. Read the terms.
- **US PhDs** usually fund you through your department; you apply for admission, not for a separate scholarship.

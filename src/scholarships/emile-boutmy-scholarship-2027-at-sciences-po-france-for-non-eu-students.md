**Sciences Po** in Paris, one of the world's leading universities for the social sciences, offers the **Emile Boutmy Scholarship** to outstanding international students from outside the European Union. About **300 scholarships** are awarded each year.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Bachelor's:** full tuition, or €9,500 off tuition each year. It is renewed automatically if you continue to a Master's at Sciences Po.
- **Master's (two-year programmes):** €18,500 off tuition each year, up to €37,000 in total.

**Not covered:** living costs, accommodation, flights and visa fees. You will need your own funds for living in Paris.

## Eligibility

- A citizen of a country **outside the European Union**, with a household whose tax residence is outside the EU
- A **first-time applicant** to Sciences Po
- Applying for a Bachelor's degree or an eligible two-year Master's programme
- Meets the programme's academic and language requirements

**Not eligible:**
- Dual citizens with an EU nationality
- Swiss and Norwegian applicants
- Exchange students
- One-year Master's programmes
- Master's in Journalism and most Master's dual degrees

## How to apply

1. Apply for admission to Sciences Po online.
2. In the **"Financial information"** section of your application, tick the box to apply for the Emile Boutmy Scholarship.
3. You may be asked for proof of your family income and situation.
4. Scholarship decisions are made based on your academic merit and profile, and are sent with your admission decision.

There is no separate scholarship application.

## Deadlines (2027 intake)

- **Master's (second and final round): 29 November 2026**
- **Bachelor's (students from schools outside France): 13 January 2027**
- Bachelor's (students from French secondary schools): 1 April 2027

All deadlines are in Paris time.

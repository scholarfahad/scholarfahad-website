The **Université d'Angers** in western France invites outstanding international students to apply for the **Eiffel Excellence Scholarship 2027**, the French government's flagship scholarship for international master's and PhD students. The university selects its candidates and submits them to Campus France.

## What the Eiffel Scholarship covers

- **Master's:** a monthly allowance of **€1,200**, for up to 12 months (M2), 24 months (M1) or 36 months (engineering degree)
- **PhD:** a monthly allowance of **€2,100**, for 12 to 36 months (36-month awards are limited and reserved for first-year PhD students)
- **Return international flight**
- **Health insurance**
- **Cultural activities**
- Help finding accommodation

## Fields of study at Angers

- **Science and technology:** biology and health, ecological transition, mathematics and digital science, engineering
- **Humanities and social sciences:** history, French language and civilisation, law and political science, economics and management

## Who can apply

**Master's:**
- Born **after 31 March 1997**
- A current or former student of a **University of Angers partner institution**
- Excellent academic results
- Not studying in France in 2026–2027

**PhD:**
- Born **after 31 March 1991**
- Currently studying, with excellent results
- Students already in France can apply but are not a priority

**Both levels:**
- Not a French citizen (dual nationals holding French nationality are not eligible)

## How to apply

You **cannot apply directly to Campus France**. Applications sent directly by students, or by a foreign institution, are not accepted.

**Master's:**
1. Choose a master's programme in the University of Angers catalogue, and check that your home university is an Angers partner.
2. Complete the online Eiffel application form for the relevant faculty.
3. Upload your documents: ID, CV, diplomas and rankings, language certificate, transcripts from the last three years, professional project and recommendation letters.
4. Submit by **15 November 2026**.

**PhD:**
1. Find a thesis supervisor at the University of Angers through its doctoral schools.
2. Agree on your research project with the supervisor.
3. Your **supervisor submits the application**, following the university's internal procedure.

The university then selects its best candidates and submits them online to Campus France.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |

| PhD | Contact your supervisor as early as possible |

## Tips

- **Check the partnership first.** Master's applicants must come from an Angers partner university.
- **Contact a PhD supervisor now.** They need time to prepare and submit your file.
- **Show excellence.** Include your class rank and best results in your file.
- **Talk to Campus France in your country** for guidance on studying in France.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises an Eiffel scholarship.

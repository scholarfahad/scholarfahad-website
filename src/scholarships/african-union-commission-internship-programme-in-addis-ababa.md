The **African Union Commission (AUC)** runs an internship programme that gives young Africans hands-on experience inside the institution working on the continent's development and integration. Interns join departments at the AU headquarters in **Addis Ababa, Ethiopia**.

> **Deadline: 31 December 2026**, according to the AU careers portal.
>
> **Please note: this internship is unpaid.** The African Union doesn't pay interns a salary or stipend. Interns cover their own travel, accommodation, living costs and medical insurance.

## About the internship

- **Duration:** 3 months, which can be renewed once depending on your performance and the department's needs
- **Location:** AU Commission headquarters, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
- **Work:** full-time, in one of the Commission's departments
- **No previous work experience required**

## Who can apply

- **Citizens of an African Union member state**
- **Age 32 or under** at the time of selection
- **Final-year bachelor's students**, or **bachelor's or master's graduates** in a relevant field
- **Fluent in at least one AU working language:** English, French, Arabic or Portuguese
- Good conduct and no serious criminal record

## Documents you'll need

- **CV in the official AU format.** The AU CV template is **mandatory**, and you can download it from the job listing.
- **Motivation letter** explaining what you hope to gain from the internship
- **Passport or national ID** copy
- **Academic certificates**
- **Recommendation letter** from your university

## How to apply

1. Go to the **AU careers portal** and open the Internship Program listing (official link above).
2. **Download the AU CV template** and fill it in carefully.
3. Create an account, complete the online application and upload your documents.
4. Submit **before 31 December 2026**.

## Is it worth it if it's unpaid?

For many young Africans, yes. An AU internship gives you experience at a major international organisation, contacts across the continent and a strong line on your CV for future jobs in diplomacy, policy and development. But plan your budget honestly. Living in Addis Ababa for three months costs money, so think about savings, family support or sponsorship from your university or employer before you apply.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Use the AU CV template exactly.** Applications in other formats may be rejected.
- **Make your motivation letter specific.** Name the department you're interested in and connect it to your studies and career goals.
- **Show your commitment to Africa's development** through volunteering, student leadership or community projects.

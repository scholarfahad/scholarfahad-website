**Wageningen University & Research (WUR)** in the Netherlands is one of the world's leading universities for agriculture, food, environment and life sciences. Through its **Africa Scholarship Programme (ASP)**, WUR offers **fully funded scholarships** to talented African students for a **two-year MSc** starting in **September 2027**.

> **Deadline: 1 December 2026.** Your **complete MSc application**, with all documents including your English test result, must be submitted by this date to be considered for the scholarship.

## What the scholarship covers

For **24 months**:

- **Tuition fees**
- A **living allowance**
- **Travel** to and from the Netherlands
- **Visa costs**
- **Health insurance**

## Who can apply

- **Citizens of an African country**
- **Unconditional admission** to a WUR MSc programme starting September 2027
- A **First Class Honours** bachelor's degree, or a **GPA of 80% or higher**
- Applicants **without a master's degree** are preferred
- You must meet WUR's **English language requirements** before the deadline

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Complete MSc application deadline | **1 December 2026** |
| Selected candidates notified | March–April 2027 |
| Scholarship offers | **7 April 2027** |
| Programme starts | September 2027 |

WUR selects final recipients **by lottery** from eligible, shortlisted candidates. Others are placed on a waiting list.

## How to apply

1. **Choose an MSc programme** at WUR, such as plant sciences, food technology, environmental sciences, nutrition, animal sciences, development studies or climate studies.
2. **Apply online** through the WUR application portal.
3. **Upload all required documents** before 1 December 2026, including your **bachelor's diploma**, **transcripts** and **English test result**.
4. Follow the **scholarship instructions** on WUR's official page and watch your email for updates.

## Other WUR scholarships

- **Anne van den Ban Fund:** for nationals of about 70 developing countries, including **Nigeria**. It offers a similar full package (tuition, living allowance, travel, visa and insurance), but it's **by invitation only**. Your complete MSc application must also be in by **1 December 2026**, with results expected in May 2027.
- **WU Excellence Scholarship:** a full tuition waiver for students from a specific list of countries (such as the US, UK, Japan and Brazil).
- **NL Scholarship:** **€5,000** (up to €15,000 maximum) for students from selected non-European countries, including **South Africa**. Applications by invitation, **before 1 May 2027**. This is partial funding only.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Apply early.** WUR admissions take time, and your file must be complete by 1 December.
- **Book your English test now** if you don't have a valid result.
- **Write a clear motivation letter** connecting your studies, your experience and how you'll use what you learn in Africa. Read our [motivation letter guide](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/).
- **Check your GPA.** The scholarship needs First Class Honours or 80%+, so apply only if you meet it.

King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in Thuwal, Saudi Arabia, is a leading graduate research university on the Red Sea coast. Every student admitted to its master's (MS), combined MS/PhD and PhD programmes receives the **KAUST Fellowship**, a full scholarship with a generous stipend.

The fellowship is open to students of **all nationalities**, and there is **no application fee**.

## What the KAUST Fellowship covers

- **Full tuition** and bench fees
- **Tax-free stipend:** US$20,000 a year for MS students, and US$25,000–30,000 a year for PhD students, depending on qualifications and progress
- **Free on-campus housing:** shared accommodation for single students and two-bedroom units for married students
- **Health insurance** for students and eligible dependents living on campus
- **Visa costs and relocation support**
- **Return travel** after graduation

## Programmes

KAUST offers graduate programmes in three divisions:

- **Biological and Environmental Science and Engineering (BESE):** Bioscience, Environmental Science and Engineering, Marine Science, Plant Science
- **Computer, Electrical and Mathematical Science and Engineering (CEMSE):** Applied Mathematics and Computational Science, Computer Science, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Statistics
- **Physical Science and Engineering (PSE):** Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Earth Science and Engineering, Energy Resources and Petroleum Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, Mechanical Engineering

## Who can apply

- **MS:** a relevant bachelor's degree
- **PhD:** a relevant master's degree (strong bachelor's graduates can apply for the combined MS/PhD)
- A competitive academic record (minimum GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale; admitted students are usually much stronger)
- **English proficiency:** IELTS 7.0 overall (at least 6.5 in each section), TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic 71, or Cambridge C1/C2 185. Duolingo is not accepted.
- **GRE:** optional
- English tests are waived for students who completed an in-person degree in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand

## Documents you need

- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV
- Statement of purpose
- Contact details for three referees
- English test score
- GRE score (optional)
- Copy of your passport or national ID

## How to apply

1. **Choose your programme** and research areas, and look at KAUST faculty whose work interests you.
2. **Create an account** on the official KAUST admissions portal.
3. **Complete the online application** and upload your documents.
4. **Enter your referees' details.** KAUST will contact them for recommendation letters.
5. **Submit by 3 January 2027.** Admitted students automatically receive the KAUST Fellowship.

## Tips for a strong application

- **Write a focused statement of purpose.** Name the research areas and KAUST faculty you want to work with, and explain how your experience prepares you.
- **Highlight your research.** Projects, publications, internships and technical skills all strengthen your application.
- **Choose referees who know your work** and can speak about your research potential.
- **Book your English test early**, so your score arrives before the deadline.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises KAUST admission.

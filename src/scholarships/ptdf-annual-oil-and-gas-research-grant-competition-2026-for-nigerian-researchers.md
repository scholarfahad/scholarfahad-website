The **Petroleum Technology Development Fund (PTDF)** has opened the **2026 cycle** of its **Annual Oil and Gas Research Grant Competition**. The programme funds Nigerian researchers to solve real problems in the **oil, gas and energy industry**, and to build local research and technology capacity.

## What PTDF offers

- A **research grant** for successful proposals, released in **instalments** as you reach agreed project milestones
- Projects can last up to **24 months**
- The chance to work with industry and present your work to PTDF and sector experts

PTDF has not published a fixed grant amount for this cycle.

## Research themes (2026 cycle)

1. Energy security, basin development and subsurface studies
2. Sustainable production, enhanced oil recovery and asset integrity
3. Gas commercialisation and flare reduction
4. Integrated technologies, renewables and low-carbon solutions
5. Environmental sustainability, circular economy and ESG
6. Digital oilfield, automation and emerging technologies
7. Facilities engineering, local content and industrial systems
8. Energy economics, fiscal systems, policy analytics and human capital

## Who can apply

- **Nigerian citizens living in Nigeria**
- Researchers at **universities and other academic institutions**, **companies**, **research organisations** or **NGOs**
- The **principal researcher must be a senior academic** based in Nigeria
- **Industry collaboration** is strongly encouraged
- **Nigerians abroad** can join as collaborators
- **Multidisciplinary teams** have an advantage

## Proposal format

- Follow PTDF's **14-section** proposal template
- **Maximum 5,000 words**
- Times New Roman, 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing, 2.54 cm margins
- **APA 7th edition** referencing
- **No names, logos or institutional identifiers** in the proposal (blind review)

## How to apply

1. Choose **one theme** that fits your research.
2. Build your team, ideally including an industry partner.
3. Write your proposal following the official format.
4. Submit through the **PTDF Oil and Gas Research portal**: oilandgasresearch.ptdf.gov.ng
5. Shortlisted teams are invited to an **oral presentation**.

## Deadline

**10 February 2027**

## Tips

- Focus on a real industry problem in Nigeria, and show clearly how your results could be used.
- Include an industry partner's support letter if you can.
- Remove all names and logos from your proposal. Identifying details can disqualify you.

The **Oxford–Pershing Square Graduate Scholarship** is one of the most generous scholarships at the **University of Oxford**. It supports outstanding students who want to tackle the world's biggest problems through Oxford's **1+1 MBA**: a one-year master's degree followed by the one-year MBA at the Saïd Business School.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full course fees** for both the **one-year master's** and the **MBA**
- A **living-costs grant of at least £21,805** for each year of study
- **Five scholarships** are awarded each year

## How the 1+1 MBA works

1. **Year 1:** a one-year master's degree at Oxford, in a subject such as public policy, global health, energy systems, development studies or computer science
2. **Year 2:** the one-year MBA at Oxford's Saïd Business School

## Who is considered (in order of priority)

1. **New applicants** admitted to both a 1+1 MBA partner master's and the MBA (waitlisted applicants are not considered)
2. Applicants admitted to a full-time one-year master's **not** on the partner list, who have also applied separately to the MBA
3. Current Oxford master's students admitted to the MBA who weren't considered the year before

## How to apply

1. **Choose your one-year master's** from Oxford's 1+1 MBA partner programmes.
2. **Apply for both courses**: the master's and the 1+1 MBA.
3. **Write a scholarship essay** of no more than **500 words** answering:
   > *"How do you intend to change the world and what does this tell us about you as a person?"*
4. **Upload the essay** in the **Funding** section of your 1+1 MBA application.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| 1+1 MBA application deadline | **6 January 2027** |
| Master's application deadline | December or January (check your course page) |
| Shortlisting | Early April 2027 |
| Interviews | Late April – early May 2027 |

## Tips

- **Apply for your master's by its own deadline**, which may come before 6 January.
- **Make your essay personal and specific.** Show a clear problem you want to solve and why you're the person to do it.
- **Show leadership and impact** in your CV and statements.
- Applying is free, though Oxford charges standard application fees for its courses.

The **Alexander von Humboldt Foundation** is inviting applications for the **Humboldt Residency Programme 2027**. The six-week residency in Germany brings together researchers, creatives and civil society practitioners from around the world to work on one big global challenge.

## 2027 theme: Oceanic Horizons

**"Oceanic Horizons: Navigating the Future of Our Blue Planet"** covers ocean health, human activity at sea, the blue economy, adapting to change and ocean governance. Applicants from **all disciplines** are welcome, including those with creative or unusual approaches.

## Places (12 in total)

- **3 early career researchers:** currently doing a PhD or completed it less than four years before August 2027, with experience in science communication or linking research to society and policy
- **4 civil society practitioners:**
  - creatives (journalists, artists, filmmakers, writers);
  - practitioners (policy, NGO, entrepreneurship, consulting) without a current academic position
- **5 Humboldt alumni:** past Humboldt Foundation fellows or award winners

All applicants must be **based outside Germany**.

## What the residency covers

- A **monthly allowance** during the six-week residency
- A **travel allowance** to and from Germany
- **Accommodation**: two weeks at Schloss Wiepersdorf (a cultural foundation in Brandenburg) and four weeks in a Berlin aparthotel
- A budget for public events and end-of-programme outputs
- Family accommodation and **childcare support** for participants with children

## Programme dates

- 29 June 2027: virtual kick-off
- 2 to 13 August 2027: residency at Schloss Wiepersdorf
- 13 August to 10 September 2027: residency in Berlin, with trips to Northern Germany and the North and Baltic Sea coasts
- September to October 2027: finish outputs remotely
- 26 October 2027: virtual wrap-up

**In-person attendance from 2 August to 10 September 2027 is mandatory.**

## How to apply

1. Prepare a **CV of up to 3 pages** (PDF).
2. Record a **60-second introduction video** (landscape 16:9; mp4, mov, avi or mpeg).
3. Upload the video to Smash (fromsmash.com) and copy the link.
4. Complete the **online application form** on the Humboldt Foundation website and add your CV and video link.

## Deadline

**14 December 2026.** Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and decisions are expected at the end of February 2027.

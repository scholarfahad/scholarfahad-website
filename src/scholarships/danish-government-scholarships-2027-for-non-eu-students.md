The Danish Government Scholarships (also called Danish State Scholarships) are funded by Denmark's Ministry of Higher Education and Science and help talented students from outside Europe take a full degree in Denmark. Each university chooses its own recipients.

> **Important:** You do not apply to the government. You apply for a **master's programme** at a Danish university, and the university decides who receives the scholarship. Most deadlines for September 2027 are **15 January 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers
The scholarship has two parts. Universities can give one or both:
- A **full or partial tuition fee waiver**
- A **grant for living costs**

What you get depends on the university and faculty. For example:
- **University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Science:** tuition and living expenses for 22 months
- **University of Copenhagen, Faculty of Theology:** partial tuition waiver only
- **Aarhus University, Arts and Aarhus BSS:** tuition waiver plus a monthly living grant
- **Aarhus University, Natural and Technical Sciences:** tuition waiver only

Living grants are taxed in Denmark (some nationalities may be exempt).

## Who can apply
- Citizens of countries **outside the EU, EEA and Switzerland**, including Nigerians and other Africans
- Admitted to a **full degree programme** (usually a master's) at a Danish university
- You will hold a time-limited Danish residence permit for study
- Not eligible if you have the rights of a Danish citizen or receive the Danish State Education Grant (SU)

## How you are selected
- **Merit only.** Awards are based on academic results, not financial need.
- It is very competitive. At Copenhagen's Faculty of Science, applicants need a bachelor's average above 90%, and 2025 recipients had at least 98%. You must also be admitted to your **first-priority** programme.

## How to apply
1. Choose an English-taught master's programme at a Danish university (for example, Copenhagen, Aarhus, DTU, SDU or Aalborg).
2. Apply for **admission** before the deadline. At Copenhagen, the portal opens on **15 November** and closes on **15 January** at 23:59 (Danish time).
3. There is **no separate scholarship application**. Eligible applicants are considered automatically.
4. If you are selected, you will be told through the application portal. Accept the offer before its deadline or it goes to someone else.

## Key dates (most master's programmes, September 2027 start)
- **Mid-November 2026:** applications open
- **15 January 2027:** application deadline
- **March 2027:** admission decisions (Copenhagen replies by 13 March)

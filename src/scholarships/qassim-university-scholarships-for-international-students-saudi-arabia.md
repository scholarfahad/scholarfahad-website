**Qassim University** is a public university in **Buraydah, in Saudi Arabia's Qassim Region**. International students can study there on **Saudi government scholarships**, which are offered as **full or partial grants**, for postgraduate programmes including master's and PhD degrees.

> **Application dates:** Saudi universities open applications in **short windows**, usually once per semester. Qassim University's graduate admission page lists an electronic application period from **27 January to 11 February** for its next first-semester intake, but confirm the exact year and dates on the official pages before you plan. Join our WhatsApp channel so you don't miss the window.

## Two ways to apply

**1. If you live outside Saudi Arabia (most international students)**

Apply through the official **Study in Saudi Arabia platform** (studyinsaudi.sa). This route is for:

- International applicants **without valid Saudi residency**
- **GCC nationals**
- Residents **sponsored by Qassim University or another Saudi university**

The platform offers **fully funded grants** and **partial grants** for international students at Saudi universities.

**2. If you already live in Saudi Arabia**

Apply through **Qassim University's own admission portal**. This route requires:

- **Valid residency**, with at least one year remaining from the application date
- **No sponsorship** from a Saudi university
- **Age:** under **30** for master's and under **35** for PhD
- Degree documents **certified by the Saudi Embassy or Cultural Attaché** in the country where you earned them

Health programmes aren't open on this track, and final admission depends on approval from Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education.

## Academic requirements

According to Qassim University's graduate admission page:

- **Master's:** a recognised **bachelor's degree**
- **PhD:** a recognised **master's degree**
- **GPA:** at least **4.25 out of 5** for applied programmes, or **4.50 out of 5** for theoretical programmes (or the equivalent)
- **Qiyas General Abilities Test** from Saudi Arabia's National Center for Assessment (valid for 5 years)
- **STEP English test** for programmes that require it (valid for 3 years)

Requirements can differ by programme and by application route, so check the programme you choose.

## Documents you'll usually need

- **Passport** copy
- **Degree certificates**
- **Academic transcripts**
- **Qiyas** and **STEP** results, if required
- Certified or translated documents, if your degree is from outside Saudi Arabia

## How to apply from abroad

1. Go to **studyinsaudi.sa** and create an account.
2. Search for **Qassim University** and choose your programme.
3. Fill in the application and **upload your documents** during the open window.
4. Track your application on the platform. The university decides on admission.

## Tips

- **Prepare your documents now** so you're ready when the short window opens.
- **Check whether your programme is taught in Arabic or English.** Many Saudi programmes teach in Arabic.
- **Apply only through official platforms.** Never pay agents who promise a guaranteed Saudi scholarship.

Questions? Qassim University's Deanship of Postgraduate Studies can be reached at **gsd@qu.edu.sa**.

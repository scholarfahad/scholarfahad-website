**Sultan Qaboos University (SQU)** in **Muscat** is Oman's leading public university and one of the top universities in the Arab region. It regularly hires **academic staff from around the world** to teach and do research in its colleges.

## Types of jobs

- **Professors**
- **Associate Professors**
- **Assistant Professors**
- Lecturers and instructors (in some colleges)

SQU's colleges include Science, Engineering, Economics and Political Science, Medicine and Health Sciences, Nursing, Education, Arts and Social Sciences, Law, Agricultural and Marine Sciences, and the Centre for Preparatory Studies.

Some roles, such as **demonstrator** and **clinical instructor** posts, are often **for Omani citizens only**. Check this in each job advert.

## What SQU jobs usually offer

- A **tax-free salary**, depending on rank and experience
- **Housing** (or a housing allowance)
- **Annual air tickets**
- **Health care**
- **Paid annual leave**
- **End-of-service benefits**
- Research support and professional development

The exact package is given in each job advert and in your offer letter.

## Who can apply

For most academic positions:
- A **PhD** in the relevant field (some instructor roles accept a master's)
- **University teaching experience**
- A record of **research and publications**
- Ability to **teach in English**. Some subjects, such as Arabic language and literature, are taught in Arabic.

Senior roles (Associate Professor and Professor) usually need several years of experience and a strong publication record.

## How to apply

1. Go to the official **SQU careers portal**.
2. Open **Academic Positions** and find a vacancy that matches your field.
3. Read the requirements and deadline carefully.
4. Prepare your **CV** (2–3 pages) and a **cover letter** (1 page).
5. Create an account, upload your documents and submit.
6. Shortlisted candidates are contacted for interviews. Referees may be asked for letters later.

## Deadlines

Each vacancy has its own deadline, and many are open **until filled**. New jobs are posted throughout the year, so check the portal often.

## Tips

- Tailor your CV to the job: highlight teaching, research and publications in that exact field.
- Name the courses you can teach and the research you plan to do at SQU.
- SQU never charges a fee to apply. Ignore any "agent" who asks for money.

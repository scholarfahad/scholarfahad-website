The **Japan Youth Summit 2027** is an international youth conference in **Osaka, Japan**, from **10 to 13 May 2027**, organised by **Youth Break the Boundaries (YBB)**, a private youth-programme organiser. The 2027 theme is **"Youth Innovation for a World Without Limits"**, focusing on five UN Sustainable Development Goals: quality education (SDG 4), industry and innovation (SDG 9), sustainable cities (SDG 11), responsible consumption (SDG 12) and climate action (SDG 13).

## Funding: what you need to know

The summit offers **two ways to take part**:

### Fully funded places (limited)

A **small number of competitive places** cover:

- Accommodation
- Meals during the event
- Local transportation and airport assistance at set times
- A city tour or university visit
- Certificate and seminar kit
- Mentoring and pre-orientation sessions

**Not covered, even for funded participants:** international flights, visa costs and personal expenses.

### Self-funded places

Most participants pay a programme fee and cover their own costs. Check the official website for the current fee and refund policy before paying anything.

### Registration fee

All applicants pay a **US$10 registration fee** when applying.

## Who can apply

- **Aged 15 to 40**
- **Any nationality** and educational background
- No IELTS or TOEFL required
- No criminal record

## How to apply

1. Visit the official website, **[japanyouthsummit.com](https://japanyouthsummit.com)**, and read the application guidelines carefully, including fees and refunds.
2. Complete the online application and pay the US$10 registration fee.
3. Choose whether to apply for a **fully funded** or **self-funded** place.
4. Submit before **20 December 2026**.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Application deadline | **20 December 2026** |
| Summit | 10–13 May 2027, Osaka |

## Before you apply

- **Plan your flight budget.** Even with a funded place, you'll need to pay for return flights to Japan and your visa.
- **Read the fee and refund rules** before paying any programme fee.
- **Check visa requirements early.** Japanese visas can take several weeks.
- **Think about value.** If you can't cover the travel costs, consider fully funded alternatives such as exchange programmes and government scholarships.

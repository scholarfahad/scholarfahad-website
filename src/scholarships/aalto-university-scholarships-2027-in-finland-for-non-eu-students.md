**Aalto University**, Finland's leading university for technology, business, art and design, offers **tuition fee scholarships** to talented students from **outside the EU/EEA** starting a bachelor's or master's degree in **autumn 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers

- A **tuition fee waiver of up to 100%** (some awards are 50%), for the **normal length of your degree**: 2 years for a master's, 3 years for a bachelor's
- Tuition at Aalto is about **€12,000 to €20,000 per year**, depending on the programme, so a full waiver saves you a lot

**Not covered:** living costs. To get a Finnish residence permit, you must show you can support yourself.

## Keeping the scholarship

Continuing scholarships require **full-time study and steady progress** according to your study plan. Progress is checked each spring.

## Who can apply

- Citizens of countries **outside the EU/EEA** who must pay tuition fees
- Applying to an **English-taught** bachelor's or master's programme at Aalto
- A strong academic record. Scholarships are merit-based.

## Fields

Engineering, technology, computer science, business and economics, art, design and architecture.

## Dates (autumn 2027 intake)

- **Master's programmes:** applications open **7 December 2026** and close **5 January 2027** (15:00 Finnish time). Documents are due by **12 January 2027**.
- **Bachelor's programmes:** applications open **7 January 2027** and close **22 January 2027**.

## How to apply

1. Choose an English-taught programme at Aalto.
2. Apply for admission during the application period.
3. **Tick the scholarship option on the admission form.** There's no separate scholarship application.
4. **You can't apply for the scholarship after the application period closes**, so don't forget this step.

Official page: https://www.aalto.fi/en/study-at-aalto/scholarships-and-tuition-fees

## Tips

- Prepare your documents early (transcripts, English test, CV, motivation letter), since the master's window is during the holidays.
- Budget for living costs in Finland and plan how you'll show proof of funds.
- Write a motivation letter that links your goals to the specific Aalto programme.

The **Niger Delta Development Commission (NDDC)** has opened applications for its **2026/2027 Local Post-Graduate (Master's Degree) Scholarship Programme**. It funds indigenes of the Niger Delta to study a master's degree at **recognised Nigerian universities**.

**Applying is completely free.**

## Who can apply

- An **indigene of one of the 9 Niger Delta states**: Abia, Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Edo, Imo, Ondo or Rivers
- **Not older than 40**
- **Bachelor's degree holders:** at least a **Second Class Lower**, and **NYSC completed** (discharge or exemption certificate)
- **HND holders:** at least an **Upper Credit**, plus a relevant Postgraduate Diploma where applicable
- **PGD holders:** at least an **Upper Credit**
- A **master's admission letter** from a recognised Nigerian university
- Candidates from **oil-producing host communities** are reported to get priority if they meet the cut-off mark

## Eligible fields

- Agricultural Sciences
- Architecture
- Engineering
- Environmental Sciences
- Geosciences
- Humanities
- Information Technology
- Law
- Management and Social Sciences
- Medical Sciences
- Education
- Biotechnology / Biomedical Science

## What it covers

The NDDC's notice doesn't state the amount or what the scholarship pays for. Earlier rounds have been described as covering tuition and providing support for living costs. Check the official portal for the 2026/2027 details.

## Documents

- Recent passport photograph
- **Local Government Identification Letter**
- **Master's admission letter**
- Degree certificates and transcripts
- **NYSC discharge or exemption certificate**
- Birth certificate or age declaration
- Your O'Level results and NIN may also be checked

## Selection process

1. Online application
2. Screening of your documents and qualifications
3. **Computer-based test (CBT)** at centres in **Akwa Ibom, Edo and Rivers**. You choose your preferred centre when applying. You need **at least 70%** to move on.
4. Possible oral interview
5. Final verification of your admission, certificates and indigene status

## How to apply

1. Go to the NDDC scholarship page: https://www.nddc.gov.ng/WhatWeDo/Scholarship
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Fill in the form and upload your documents.
4. Submit before **8 November 2026**.

Problems with the portal: **scholarship@nddc.gov.ng**

## Tips

- Get your **Local Government Identification Letter** early. It often takes time.
- Make sure your **admission letter** is for a master's programme at a recognised Nigerian university.
- Prepare for the CBT like an exam: practise general aptitude and your subject area.
- The NDDC never charges for this application. **Don't pay anyone** who promises to "secure" a slot.

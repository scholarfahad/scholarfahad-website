**Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** is one of China's top universities, known worldwide for engineering, medicine, business and science. For 2027 it welcomes international **master's and PhD** students on several scholarships, including the fully funded **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)**.

## Scholarships available

| Scholarship | Level | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)** | Master's, PhD | Tuition, housing, insurance and monthly stipend |
| **Shanghai Government Scholarship** | Master's, PhD | Full or partial support |
| **SJTU Scholarship** | Master's, PhD | All or part of tuition, plus accommodation support depending on level |
| **SJTU Tuition Waiver / School Scholarships** | Master's | All or part of tuition |

**CSC monthly stipend** (standard CSC rates): **RMB 3,000** for master's and **RMB 3,500** for PhD.

You can apply for scholarships **either** through SJTU **or** through a Chinese embassy, **not both**.

## Who can apply

- **Non-Chinese citizens** with an ordinary passport
- **Master's:** a bachelor's degree, aged **35 or under**
- **PhD:** a master's degree, aged **40 or under** (some schools accept bachelor's holders for direct PhD entry)
- **Final-year students** can apply with a certificate of expected graduation
- You meet the **language requirements** of your programme (English-taught or Chinese-taught)
- You do not hold another scholarship
- Your passport is valid for at least **12 months** from your expected start date

## Documents you usually need

- Passport
- Degree certificate (or expected graduation certificate) and transcripts, in Chinese or English
- Study plan or research proposal
- Recommendation letters
- Language certificate
- **PhD:** a summary of your research experience; some schools also ask for a **Letter of Intent for Supervision** from an SJTU professor

Upload **colour scans of original documents**. Phone photos and photocopies are not accepted.

## How to apply (CSC through SJTU)

1. Choose your programme and check its requirements.
2. Apply online at **apply.sjtu.edu.cn** and pay the **RMB 800** application fee (non-refundable).
3. Register on the **CSC portal** (studyinchina.csc.edu.cn) and apply for **Type B**, using SJTU's agency number **10248**.
4. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadlines (Beijing time)

- **5 November 2026:** applications open
- **15 December 2026:** first-round CSC deadline
- **15 February 2027:** final CSC deadline
- **31 March 2027:** Shanghai Government and SJTU scholarships
- **31 May 2027:** self-funded applicants

## Tips

- Apply in the **first round** (by 15 December) if you can.
- For PhD, contact an SJTU professor early. A supervision letter can greatly improve your chances.
- Make sure all your scans are clear, in colour and complete.

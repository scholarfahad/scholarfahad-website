The **German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)** funds master's degrees at leading African universities through its **In-Country/In-Region Scholarship Programme**. You study in your **own country** (In-Country) or in **another sub-Saharan African country** (In-Region), with full funding from Germany.

> **Deadline:** 12 November 2026, for the **2027 intake**. Universities may set earlier admission deadlines, so check each programme's call.

## What the scholarship covers
- **Tuition fees**
- **Monthly scholarship** (stipend)
- **Study and research allowance**
- **Thesis publishing costs**
- **In-Region students only** (studying outside their home country): **travel allowance** and **health insurance**

Funding lasts for the normal length of the master's, usually **up to two years**. Exact amounts are in each programme's call.

## Who can apply
- Graduates from a **sub-Saharan African** country (your country must be on the DAAD portal's list)
- A strong, relevant **bachelor's degree**
- You must meet the host university's admission and language requirements
- Preference for applicants planning a career in **teaching or research** at an African university
- **Women, recognised refugees, and candidates from marginalised regions or groups** are especially encouraged

## How to apply
1. Choose a programme and read its **official DAAD call for applications**.
2. Apply on the **DAAD portal** before **12 November 2026**. If you apply to more than one programme, rank them by preference.
3. **Apply separately for admission** to the programme at the host university.
4. Use a **computer**. The DAAD portal works only partly on mobile phones.

The **ICP Connect Scholarship** at **Hasselt University (UHasselt)** in Belgium is a fully funded award for students from developing countries. It is funded by **VLIR-UOS**, the Belgian agency for university development cooperation, and supports students on UHasselt's international master's programme in **road safety**.

## Programme

**Master of Transportation Sciences (Road Safety)**, 2 years, on campus in Hasselt, Belgium

The programme trains experts to make roads and transport systems safer, with a focus on developing countries. You will study traffic safety, transport policy, data analysis and road safety management.

**10 scholarships** are available each year.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition**
- **Monthly allowance** of about **EUR 1,400** for living costs
- **Health insurance**
- **Return economy flight** to Belgium
- Reimbursement of some application costs, such as medical certificates, document legalisation and visa fees

## Who can apply

- You are a **national and a current resident** of one of the **eligible VLIR-UOS countries** in Africa, Asia or Latin America. Check the official country list before you apply.
- You are **aged 35 or under** on 1 January 2027 (and at least 19)
- You have a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, such as engineering, transport, urban planning, economics or statistics
- You meet the programme's English requirements
- Preference goes to people working (or planning to work) in **higher education, government or civil society**
- You have not received a Belgian government study scholarship before
- You can apply for only **one** ICP Connect scholarship per year

## How to apply

1. **Check that your country is eligible** on the VLIR-UOS website.
2. **Apply for admission** to the Master of Transportation Sciences through UHasselt's online application portal.
3. In your application, **say that you are applying for the ICP Connect scholarship**.
4. Upload your diploma, transcripts, CV, motivation letter, reference letters and English test result (if needed).
5. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**1 February 2027** (expected; last year's UHasselt deadline). The general VLIR-UOS deadline is 28 February 2027, but UHasselt usually closes earlier, so confirm the date on the official programme page.

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, describe a real road safety or transport problem in your country and how this degree will help you solve it.
- If you work in government, a university or an NGO, explain how you will use your new skills when you return.
- Genuine admission letters come only from an email address ending in **@uhasselt.be**. Be careful of fake offers.

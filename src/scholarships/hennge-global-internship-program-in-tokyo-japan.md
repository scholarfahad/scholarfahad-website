**HENNGE**, a Tokyo-based cloud security company, runs the **HENNGE Global Internship Program (GIP)**, a hands-on software engineering internship at its **headquarters in Tokyo, Japan**. It's open to students and recent graduates from **anywhere in the world**, and **no Japanese is needed**.

## What it is

- **4–6 weeks** on-site in Tokyo, Monday to Friday, 10:00–19:00
- Two pathways:
  - **Full-Stack:** application development (weeks 1–3), DevOps and infrastructure (week 4), and a possible special assignment (weeks 5–6)
  - **Front-End:** application development training (weeks 1–4) and a DevOps project (week 5)
- Outstanding interns may receive a **full-time job offer** in Japan

## What's covered

The internship is **unpaid**, but HENNGE provides support including:

- **Return flights** to Japan
- A **monthly subsidy**
- **Medical insurance** for interns who don't live in Japan
- A **Japanese mobile phone plan** with data
- Pre-arrival support and **visa guidance**
- Welcome lunches, monthly tech sessions and cultural events

## Who can apply

- **Third-year undergraduates or above**, including master's and PhD students. **Recent graduates** are welcome.
- Studying or holding a degree in **Computer Science** or a related field, or equivalent internship or bootcamp experience
- **Fluent in English.** Japanese is not required.
- **Full-Stack pathway:** Python, Go or TypeScript, plus React or Vue
- **Front-End pathway:** React or Vue in TypeScript
- Comfortable with Linux, macOS or other Unix-like systems
- Interest in open source or tech communities. An interest in building a career in Japan is a big advantage.
- Meet at least three of HENNGE's optional skills, such as AWS, full-stack development or DevOps

## How to apply

1. **Register** on HENNGE's site, choose your pathway (Full-Stack or Front-End) and verify your email.
2. **Solve the coding challenge.** There's no time limit, and solving time isn't scored.
3. Upload your **CV and cover letter** and choose your preferred batch.
4. Pass an **HR interview** and an **online technical interview**.

Apply here: https://recruit.hennge.com/en/gip

To try both pathways, you need a separate email address for each coding challenge. You can't switch pathways after you start.

## Deadline

There's **no fixed deadline**. Each batch has limited places, filled **first come, first served**. Check the batch schedule on HENNGE's page and **apply early**.

## Tips

- Polish your GitHub. Show real projects using the languages they ask for.
- Take your time on the coding challenge. Clean, well-tested code matters more than speed.
- In your cover letter, explain why you're interested in Japan and in HENNGE.
- HENNGE never charges application fees.

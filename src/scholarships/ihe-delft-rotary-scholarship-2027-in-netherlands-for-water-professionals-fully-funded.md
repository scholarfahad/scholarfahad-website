The **Rotary Scholarships for Water and Sanitation Professionals** are a partnership between **The Rotary Foundation** and **IHE Delft Institute for Water Education** in the Netherlands. IHE Delft is the world's largest graduate water education institute, set up with UNESCO. The scholarships train professionals from developing countries to solve water and sanitation problems at home.

## Programme

**MSc in Water and Sustainable Development** at IHE Delft, Delft, the Netherlands

Specialisations include:
- Water and wastewater services engineering
- Water resources and ecosystem health
- Water, food and energy
- Water hazards, risks and climate

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition**
- **Living allowance** and accommodation
- **International travel**
- **Health insurance**
- **Residence permit / visa** costs
- A grant for **thesis fieldwork**
- Mentoring and networking through your **sponsoring Rotary club**

Past listings valued the scholarship at about **EUR 32,500**.

## Who can apply

- You are a **water and sanitation professional** (or have a background in water, sanitation, environment, engineering or development)
- You come from a **developing or emerging country** with water and sanitation challenges
- You **live or work near a Rotary club** that agrees to sponsor you
- You are **admitted (or provisionally admitted)** to the IHE Delft MSc
- You show **leadership** and plan to **return home** to make an impact

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to the IHE Delft MSc programme (last year's deadline was **1 April**).
2. **Find a local Rotary club** near you using the club finder on rotary.org. Ask them to sponsor and nominate you.
3. Complete the **Rotary scholarship application form** with your club.
4. **Your Rotary club submits** the form, your CV and your IHE admission letter to the Rotary/IHE Delft committee.

Only applications **fully supported and submitted by a sponsoring Rotary club** are considered.

## Deadline

The 2027–28 call has not been published yet. In the last two rounds:
- **1 April:** IHE Delft admission
- **15 April:** Rotary scholarship application

Expect similar dates in **April 2027**. **Start contacting Rotary clubs now.** It can take weeks to get a club's support.

## Tips

- Meet the Rotary club in person if you can, and explain your work and how the degree will help your community.
- In your application, describe a real water or sanitation problem you want to solve back home.
- Apply to IHE Delft early, so you have your admission letter before the Rotary deadline.

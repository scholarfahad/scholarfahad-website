Every year, the **Government of the Slovak Republic** offers scholarships to students from selected developing countries for **full bachelor's, master's and PhD degrees** at public universities in Slovakia.

**Status:** the 2026/27 call closed on **25 May 2026**. The 2027/28 call is expected to open around **March 2027**. Use this time to prepare your documents.

## What the scholarship covers

| | Bachelor's & master's | PhD |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly stipend | €700 | €1,100 |
| Extra monthly stipend from the university | €80 | €240 |
| Start-of-study payment | €100 | €100 |
| End-of-study payment | €200 | €200 |

- **Free tuition** at Slovak public universities, where degrees are taught in Slovak
- **A free 10-month Slovak language and preparatory course** before your degree, with a €700 monthly stipend
- A €50 monthly bonus for excellent grades

These amounts are from the 2026/27 call and may change.

## Important: classes are taught in Slovak

Degrees under this scholarship are taught **in Slovak**. If you haven't studied in Slovakia before, you'll first take the 10-month Slovak language course, starting in September.

## Who can apply

**Eligible countries (2026/27 list):**

- **Africa:** Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan, Tanzania, Tunisia
- **Asia & Middle East:** Afghanistan, India, Indonesia, Iraq, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Lebanon, Mongolia, Palestine, Syria, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Vietnam
- **Europe & Caucasus:** Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Ukraine
- **Americas:** Cuba

**Nigeria, Ghana and most other countries are not on the list.** The list can change each year. When the next call opens, check whether your country appears in the application website's eligibility checker.

**Age limits** (on 1 September of the year you start):

- Bachelor's and master's: 18 to 26
- PhD: 23 to 35

PhD places are very few, about 3 in the last call, and only for some countries.

## How to apply

1. Wait for the 2027/28 call on the Ministry of Education page (usually March).
2. Register on the official application website: https://www.vladnestipendia.sk/en
3. Use the eligibility checker to confirm your country can apply.
4. Fill in the online application before the deadline (usually late May).
5. A committee at the Slovak Ministry of Education selects the winners.

## Prepare now

- Get your transcripts, certificates and passport ready.
- Learn some basic Slovak, which will help in the language year.
- Think about which Slovak public university and course you want.

## Tips

- Only public universities are allowed under this scholarship.
- Never pay an agent. Applying through the official website is free.

The **Doha Institute for Graduate Studies** in Qatar offers **full and partial scholarships** for its **master's and PhD programmes** in 2027/28. International students can receive **full funding**.

## Scholarships

- **Tamim Scholarship:** merit-based, for academic excellence
- **Sanad Scholarship:** need-based

Both can be **full or partial**.

## What the full scholarship covers (international master's students)

- **100% tuition waiver**
- **Free student accommodation**
- **Monthly stipend**
- **Health insurance**
- **Return airfare** for students coming from outside Qatar

The Institute doesn't publish the stipend amount.

## Programmes

Master's programmes in **social sciences, humanities, public administration and development economics**, plus doctoral programmes.

**Executive master's, diplomas and certificate programmes are not covered** by the Tamim Scholarship.

## Who can apply

- **Qatari, resident and international students**
- A **bachelor's degree**, completed or expected by **1 August 2027**
- A **minimum GPA of 3.0**
- Able to study in **Arabic and English**. Many programmes are taught in Arabic.
- English proficiency through **TOEFL or IELTS**, or a previous degree taught in English
- You can't combine it with another scholarship, or have received a Doha Institute master's scholarship before

## Dates

- **Applications opened:** 1 September 2026
- **Deadline:** **15 January 2027**

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** through the Doha Institute's online application system.
2. If you're **admitted**, the Admissions Office sends you the **scholarship application form**.
3. Complete it for the merit-based (Tamim) or need-based (Sanad) scholarship.

Official scholarship page: https://www.dohainstitute.edu.qa/en/Admissions-Office/Pages/Scholarships.aspx

## Tips

- Check each programme's **language of instruction** before applying. Strong Arabic skills open more options.
- Apply early. Admission comes first, then the scholarship form.
- A strong statement of purpose and good references improve your chances for the merit-based Tamim Scholarship.

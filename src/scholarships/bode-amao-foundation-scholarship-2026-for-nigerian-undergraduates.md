The **Bode Amao Foundation (BAF) Scholarship** supports bright Nigerian undergraduates who are in financial need. The foundation is based in Ibadan, Oyo State, and has supported students at Nigerian universities for many years.

## What the scholarship gives

- **NGN 100,000 every academic session**
- The award continues **until you graduate**, as long as you keep up good academic performance

## Who can apply

- You are a **Nigerian undergraduate** at a **registered Nigerian university**
- You are in **200 level or above** when you apply
- You have a **strong academic record** (your CGPA is an important part of selection)
- You are in **genuine financial need**, and your university Registrar can confirm this
- Open to students of **any tribe, gender, religion or age**
- No IELTS or other English test is needed

## Documents you need

- Completed BAF application form
- Academic transcript or statement of results
- Recommendation letters
- University ID card
- Letter of identification from your Local Government Area (LGA)
- Medical certificate
- JAMB admission letter
- University admission letter
- Current course registration form

## How to apply

1. **Download the application form** from the Bode Amao Foundation website and print it.
2. **Fill in the form** carefully and honestly.
3. **Take it to your university Registrar** to confirm that you are a current student and in financial need.
4. **Attach all your documents.**
5. **Submit** your application by the method given on the official website.

Submit only **one** application. False information leads to automatic disqualification.

## Deadline

**31 December 2026**

## Tips

- Start early. Getting the Registrar's signature and an LGA letter can take time.
- Make copies of everything before you submit.
- Applying is free. Do not pay anyone who asks for money to process your application.

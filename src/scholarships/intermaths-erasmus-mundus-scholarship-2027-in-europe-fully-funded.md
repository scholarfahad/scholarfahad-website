**InterMaths** is an **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's** in **Interdisciplinary Mathematics**, funded by the European Commission. It trains mathematicians to solve real problems in industry, health, the environment and technology. Students study at **two European universities** and graduate with a degree recognised across Europe.

## Partner universities

- **University of L'Aquila**, Italy (coordinator)
- **Vienna University of Technology (TU Wien)**, Austria
- **Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH)**, Germany
- **Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB)**, Spain
- **Université Côte d'Azur**, Nice, France

## What you can study

You choose a study path with modules such as:
- Computational fluid dynamics in industry (Vienna)
- Computational methods in imaging (Hamburg)
- Decision-making and logistics (Barcelona)
- Mathematical modelling for healthcare (L'Aquila)
- Modelling and simulation for natural disaster mitigation (L'Aquila)
- Stochastics for biological and artificial neural networks (Nice)

**Duration:** 2 years (120 ECTS), starting in **October 2027**

## What the scholarship covers

The **Erasmus Mundus scholarship** includes:
- **EUR 1,400 per month** for 24 months (about EUR 33,600 in total)
- **Full tuition and participation costs**
- **Full insurance**
- An extra contribution for students with special needs, if required

About **20 full scholarships** were awarded in the last round.

Students who don't win a full scholarship may still get a **consortium scholarship** that waives or reduces fees, plus travel and installation support.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- A **bachelor's degree** (at least 180 ECTS or equivalent) in **mathematics, applied mathematics, physics, engineering** or a related field
- A **strong mathematical background**
- **English at B2 level or higher**, for example **IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 80 or PTE Academic 56**. If you studied for at least a year at an English-medium university, you may only need to confirm this in the form.
- You have not received an Erasmus Mundus master's scholarship before

## Documents you need

- Photo
- CV
- Passport or ID card
- English certificate (unless exempt)
- Transcript of records
- Course descriptions
- **Two reference letters**
- **Letter of intent**
- Official English translations of any documents not in English

## How to apply

1. Check that you meet the academic and English requirements.
2. Choose your preferred study path and universities.
3. Prepare all your documents and make sure they are clear and complete.
4. Fill in the **online application form** on the InterMaths website. Your application is automatically considered for the scholarship.
5. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**25 February 2027**, for study starting in **October 2027**

## Tips

- In your letter of intent, explain which real-world problem you want to solve with mathematics and why your chosen path fits.
- Ask referees who know your maths ability well, such as lecturers or project supervisors.
- Add detailed course descriptions so the committee can see the strength of your maths background.

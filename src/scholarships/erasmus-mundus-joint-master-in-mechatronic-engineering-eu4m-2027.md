**EU4M** is an **Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Mechatronic Engineering**, run by four European universities. Students study in **at least two countries** and graduate with a **joint MSc** from the University of Oviedo (Spain) and Hochschule Karlsruhe (Germany). Many also earn a double degree from the University of Skövde.

> **Deadline for scholarships:** 15 January 2027 (2027–2029 intake). Self-funded applicants can apply until May 2027.

## The universities
- **Universidad de Oviedo**, Spain (coordinator), taught in **Spanish**
- **Hochschule Karlsruhe (HKA)**, Germany, taught in **German**
- **SUPMICROTECH-ENSMM**, Besançon, France, taught in **French**
- **University of Skövde (HiS)**, Sweden, taught in **English**

## What the scholarship covers
- **€1,400 a month** for up to **24 months**
- **Tuition fees**
- **Health insurance**
- Language courses and support with visas

Fee-waiver places (tuition only, no monthly allowance) are also available. Without a scholarship, tuition for non-European students is about **€4,500 per semester**.

## Who can apply
- Students of **any nationality**, including Nigerians
- A **bachelor's degree** (180 ECTS or equivalent) in mechatronics or a related field, such as mechanical, electrical, electronic, automation, industrial, production, automotive or aerospace engineering
- A good academic record; professional experience is a plus

## The language requirement (read this!)
Teaching is in the **local language** at each university, except Skövde (English). So **English alone does not meet the requirement**. When you apply you need:
- At least **B1** in the teaching language of your **first-year** country, and
- At least **A2** in the language of your **second-year** country

**Example:** Start in Skövde (English, B1) and move to Germany (German, A2) for the second year.

EU4M offers language courses to help you reach B1 in your study country, but you need the basic level before you apply.

## Programme structure
- **Two years, 120 ECTS**
- **Year 1:** basic courses in automation, mechatronics and mechanical design, plus language study
- **Year 2:** specialisation at another partner university, then a master's thesis at a partner or an associated company
- Scholarship holders spend at least one semester in each of two countries

## How to apply
1. Start or continue a course in **Spanish, German or French** now.
2. Choose your preferred study path on the EU4M website.
3. Register and apply on the EU4M application platform before **15 January 2027**.
4. Upload your degree, transcripts, CV, motivation letter, language certificates and references.

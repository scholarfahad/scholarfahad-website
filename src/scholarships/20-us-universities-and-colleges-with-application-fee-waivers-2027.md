Applying to US universities can be expensive, as application fees often cost **$50 to $150 per school**. The good news is that many universities offer **application fee waivers**. Here are **20 US universities, law schools and colleges** where you may be able to apply for free.

> **Check eligibility first.** Each waiver has its own rules. Some are only for **US citizens or permanent residents**, some are based on **financial need**, and some require you to **attend an information session or open house**. If you're an international applicant and a waiver doesn't cover you, **email the department** and ask; many programmes grant waivers on request.

## Tips for getting a fee waiver

- **Read the conditions carefully** and check whether international applicants qualify.
- **Request the waiver early.** Many schools need the request days or weeks before the application deadline.
- **Attend virtual info sessions.** Many US programmes give waivers to people who attend their online events.
- **Just ask.** A short, polite email to the graduate coordinator explaining your financial situation often works, even when no waiver is advertised.
- **Remember the other costs.** A fee waiver saves the application fee, but you may still need to pay for tests like the GRE, TOEFL or IELTS, and for sending transcripts.

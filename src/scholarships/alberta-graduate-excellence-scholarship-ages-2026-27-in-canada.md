The **Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES)** is a Government of Alberta award for top **master's and PhD students** at public universities in Alberta, Canada. It is **open to international students**.

## Value

**CAD $11,000 to $15,000**, paid from the Alberta Heritage Scholarship Fund. Each university sets its own amount. For example:

- **University of Lethbridge:** $11,000 for master's, $15,000 for PhD
- **University of Alberta:** $12,000, paid as one lump sum

It is not automatically renewed, but you can be selected again in later years if you still qualify.

## Who can get it

- **Enrolled full-time** in a master's or doctoral programme (or a graduate certificate leading to a master's at some universities) at an **Alberta public post-secondary institution**
- **Living in Alberta** while studying
- A **Canadian citizen, permanent resident, protected person, Indigenous student, or international student**. International students need a valid study permit.
- Enrolled in **Fall 2026 and/or Winter 2027** for this round
- Good academic standing. Universities often look at grades: the University of Lethbridge asks for a GPA of 3.5 or higher.

## Important: you can't apply from abroad

AGES is only for students **already studying** in Alberta. If you're outside Canada, the way in is to **get admitted** to a master's or PhD programme at an Alberta university, such as:

- University of Alberta
- University of Calgary
- University of Lethbridge
- Athabasca University

Once you're enrolled, you can be considered for AGES.

## How it works

You usually don't fill in an application. **Your university selects and nominates recipients.**

- **University of Lethbridge:** no application. All eligible students are entered automatically.
- **University of Alberta:** faculties choose nominees and submit them by **13 November 2026**. Nominated students fill in an AGES Student Declaration Form for their faculty. There's also a separate **AGES-Indigenous** award that students apply for directly (deadline 13 October 2026).
- **Other universities:** contact your graduate studies or student awards office.

## Tips

- Keep your grades high from your first semester. Selection is mainly on academic merit.
- Ask your department's graduate advisor about internal deadlines. They are often earlier than the university's.
- When applying to Alberta universities, mention your research plans clearly. Strong applicants are more likely to be chosen for funding.

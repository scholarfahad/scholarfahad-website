The **University of Notre Dame** in Indiana, USA is one of America's top private universities. Since late 2024 it has been one of only a handful of highly selective US universities that are **need-blind for ALL applicants, including international students**, and that **meet 100% of demonstrated financial need** with scholarships.

## What this means for you

- **Need-blind admission:** your ability to pay does **not** affect whether you are admitted.
- **100% of demonstrated need met:** if you are admitted, Notre Dame gives you **scholarships and grants** to cover the gap between the full cost of attendance and what your family can afford.
- **No loans:** loans are not included in financial aid offers for eligible first-year and transfer undergraduates.

The full cost of attendance includes **tuition, housing, food, books and personal costs**. For students from low-income families, the aid can cover almost everything.

Notre Dame also offers a limited number of **merit scholarships** to admitted first-year students.

## Who can apply

- **International students** applying for a **bachelor's degree** (first-year or transfer)
- Excellent grades, strong essays and activities that show leadership and character
- Families who need financial support should apply for aid

Admission is **very competitive**, so put real effort into every part of your application.

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** through the **Common App** (choose Notre Dame).
2. Complete the **2027–28 CSS Profile** (school code **1841**) to apply for need-based aid. It opened on 1 October.
3. After you submit the CSS Profile, upload the documents requested through **IDOC** (for example, parents' tax returns or non-filer statements and earnings statements).

## Deadlines (first-year)

| Round | Application | CSS Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Restrictive Early Action | 1 November 2026 (closed) | 15 November 2026 |
| **Regular Decision** | **1 January 2027** | **15 January 2027** |

**Transfer students (Fall 2027):** application by **15 March 2027**, CSS Profile by **1 May 2027**.

## Tips

- Write essays that show who you are, your values and how you serve your community.
- Be honest and complete in the CSS Profile. Your aid is based on it.
- Apply for financial aid on time. Late forms can reduce your aid.

The **Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia Research Stipend** is offered by the **Stockholm Center for Global Asia** at **Stockholm University** in Sweden. It invites social science researchers from Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia to spend **1 to 3 months** doing research in Stockholm.

The stipend was created through a donation from the former Association Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, which dissolved in 2022.

## What the stipend covers

- **Return airfare** between your home country and Stockholm
- **Accommodation** in Stockholm
- A **contribution towards living expenses**, depending on how long you stay

## Research stay periods (2027)

- **April – June 2027**, or
- **September – November 2027**

## Who can apply

- You are a **social science researcher** based at a **university or research institution in Vietnam, Laos or Cambodia**
- You can be at **any career stage**
- Your research is about **development, democracy and/or human rights** in Vietnam, Laos or Cambodia, or a comparative study that includes at least one of these countries

## Documents you need (in English)

1. **Research proposal** (maximum 2,000 words): objectives, framework, methods and relevance
2. **CV** (maximum 3 pages)
3. **Letter of institutional affiliation** from your university or research institution
4. Your **preferred period** of stay

## How to apply

1. Prepare all four documents in English.
2. Email your application to **asianstudies@su.se**, with the subject line "Application – Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia Research Stipend 2027" (check the exact wording in the official call).
3. Submit before the deadline. Late or incomplete applications are not considered.

Applicants are selected on **academic merit**, **relevance** to the stipend's aims, and whether the research can realistically be done during the stay. Results are usually sent in **January**.

## What is expected of you

- Stay in Stockholm for the **full period**
- Take part in the Center's **seminars and activities**
- **Present your research** at a Center seminar

## Deadline

The 2027 deadline is expected in **mid-December 2026**. The last call closed on **12 December 2025**. Check the Stockholm Center for Global Asia website for the exact date.

## Tips

- Keep your proposal focused: one clear research question you can make real progress on in 1–3 months.
- Explain why being in Stockholm will help your research, for example the libraries, archives or experts you want to work with.

The **Nigerian Bottling Company (NBC)**, the Coca-Cola bottling partner in Nigeria and part of **Coca-Cola HBC**, is recruiting young engineers for its **Supply Chain Academy Technical Trainee Programme 2027**. It is an intensive, paid training programme that builds both **technical** and **leadership** skills for a career in manufacturing.

## What you will learn

- Basic bottling process
- Production process areas
- **Industrial mechatronics**
- **Automation and process control engineering**
- Logistics operations
- Manufacturing excellence tools
- Utility maintenance and operations
- Quality and laboratory processes
- Leadership and team management

## What you get

- A **monthly stipend** for the whole programme (amount not published)
- **Classroom training** at the **Supply Chain Academy, Ikeja plant, Lagos**
- **On-the-job training** at NBC bottling plants across Nigeria
- Strong experience for a career in manufacturing and engineering

Trainees are hired as temporary employees for the training period.

## Who can apply

- **Nigerians** with a minimum of an **OND** in one of these:
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Electrical/Electronics Engineering
  - Production and Materials Engineering
  - Mechatronics Engineering
  - Chemical Engineering
- Strong learning potential and passion for engineering
- Willing to **live in Lagos** during the training
- Willing to **work in any part of Nigeria** where NBC has a plant

## How to apply

1. Go to the **Coca-Cola HBC careers website** (Nigeria).
2. Search for **"Supply Chain Academy – Technical Trainee Program 2027"**.
3. Create an account, upload your **CV**, and complete the application.
4. Submit as soon as possible.

## Deadline

No deadline has been published. The role was posted on **6 November 2026**, and roles like this can close without notice. **Apply now.**

## Tips

- Make your CV clear and show practical skills: SIWES/IT placements, workshop projects, maintenance work or automation experience.
- Mention any experience with PLCs, electrical systems, machines or production lines.
- Coca-Cola HBC never asks for money to apply. Avoid anyone who does.

The University of Sydney, Australia's first university and one of the world's top 20, offers fully funded research scholarships to international students starting a PhD or research master's (MPhil) degree in 2027.

International students can win one of two scholarships of equal value:

- **Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Stipend Scholarship**
- **University of Sydney International Stipend Scholarship (USYDIS)**

## What the scholarships cover

- **Annual stipend** of A$44,293 in 2027 (A$42,754 in 2026), tax-free
- **100% of tuition fees** for up to 14 research periods (three and a half years)
- **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)** with the RTP Fee Offset
- **Relocation allowance** to help you move to Sydney
- **Thesis allowance** to help with thesis costs

RTP Stipend winners automatically receive the RTP Fee Offset (tuition and health cover). USYDIS winners automatically receive the University of Sydney Tuition Fee Scholarship.

## Who can apply

- International students starting, or already enrolled in, a **higher degree by research** (PhD or MPhil) at the University of Sydney
- **All nationalities** are eligible
- An excellent academic record, usually a first class honours degree or a master's with a strong research component

Preference may go to applicants who:

- are enrolling, or enrolled, in a PhD
- have not previously received an Australian Government or University research scholarship at the same level
- have not already completed a research degree at the same level

## Application deadlines for 2027

| Start of study | Scholarship deadline | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Research Periods 1 and 2 (early 2027) | 11 September 2026 (closed) | November to December 2026 |
| Research Periods 3 and 4 (mid-2027) | **18 December 2026** | From 26 February 2027 |

## How to apply

1. **Find a supervisor.** Browse the University of Sydney's research areas and contact an academic whose work matches your research interests. Share a short research proposal and your CV.
2. **Apply for admission** to your PhD or MPhil program through the Sydney Courses application portal, and upload all required documents by the scholarship deadline.
3. **Submit the scholarship application form** on the official scholarship page by 18 December 2026.
4. **Wait for the outcome.** The university will contact successful applicants.

## Documents you usually need

- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV, including research experience and publications
- Research proposal
- Evidence of English language proficiency (such as IELTS or TOEFL)
- Referee details or reports
- A letter of support from your proposed supervisor, if available

## Tips for a strong application

- **Contact supervisors early.** A supervisor's support makes a big difference in a competitive round.
- **Show research potential.** Highlight your publications, research projects, honours or master's thesis, and conference presentations.
- **Write a clear research proposal.** Explain your question, methods and why your research matters.
- **Check English requirements** and book your test early.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed scholarship.

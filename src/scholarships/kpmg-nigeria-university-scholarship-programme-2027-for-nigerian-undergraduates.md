**KPMG Nigeria**, one of Nigeria's leading professional services firms, runs the **KPMG Nigeria University Scholarship Programme (KUSP)** as part of its corporate social responsibility work. The scholarship supports bright Nigerian students from public secondary schools through their university education.

The last call closed on **3 July 2025**. The next call is expected around **mid-2027**, so start preparing your documents now.

## What the scholarship offers

- Financial sponsorship for selected students through their degree programme
- Open to students in **any course of study** at a federal institution

KPMG does not publish the exact award amount.

## Eligibility

- Nigerian citizen
- Completed secondary education at a **public** secondary school (state, federal or military)
- At least **5 distinctions in WASSCE or NECO**, including Mathematics and English, in one sitting
- A **UTME score of 230 or above**
- Admission or provisional admission to a **federal university** in Nigeria
- A full-time **100 level or 200 level** student (not part-time or distance learning)
- A minimum **CGPA of 3.5/5.0 or 3.0/4.0**

## Documents to prepare

- WASSCE or NECO certificate
- UTME (JAMB) result slip
- University admission letter
- University academic transcript
- Birth certificate or other proof of age

## How to apply

1. Watch KPMG Nigeria's official website and social media pages for the 2027 call.
2. Apply through KPMG Nigeria's official channel when the call opens.
3. Shortlisted applicants will be asked to submit their documents.

**Warning:** applying is free. Ignore anyone who asks you to pay to apply or to "secure" a slot.

## Also from KPMG Nigeria: Seyi Bickersteth Scholarship

KPMG Nigeria also runs the **Seyi Bickersteth Scholarship Programme** for financially needy 100-level students at federal universities studying accounting, business administration, economics, actuarial science, insurance or finance.

- A yearly grant for the four-year course
- Internships and mentoring
- Top graduates may be considered for jobs at KPMG and partner firms

**Rakuno Gakuen University (RGU)** is a private university in **Ebetsu, Hokkaido, Japan**, known for **dairy science, agriculture, veterinary medicine and food science**. Its **International Guest Researcher Programme 2027** invites academic staff from partner institutions to Japan for a funded research visit and joint research.

**This is not a student scholarship.** It is for working academics only.

## What the programme offers

- **University accommodation** during your stay
- A **living allowance of up to JPY 100,000**, depending on the approved budget
- The chance to do **joint research** with an RGU host professor

Travel costs and insurance are not clearly mentioned, so confirm with RGU before you apply.

## Research fields

- Agricultural, plant, soil and crop science
- Animal science, genetics and animal health
- Veterinary medicine
- Livestock nutrition
- Food science and food safety
- Environmental science and sustainable agriculture

## Who can apply

- **Professors, associate professors, assistant professors and lecturers** in a **full-time academic post**. **Students are not eligible.**
- Your institution has an **academic cooperation agreement with RGU**
- Relevant research experience
- **Prior approval from an RGU host researcher** and a joint research plan
- Japanese or English good enough for daily life and research
- Willing to **present or publish** the results within one year of the visit
- You have not received this award before
- Open to **all nationalities**

## Documents you need

- Application form
- CV
- Recommendation letter from your institution
- Acceptance letter from your RGU host
- Evidence of earlier joint research (if any)
- Research plan
- Two photographs (one for the visa)
- Health certificate (for stays longer than 3 months)

## How to apply

1. **Check** that your university has a cooperation agreement with Rakuno Gakuen University.
2. **Find an RGU professor** whose research matches yours and contact them.
3. **Write a joint research plan** together and get their acceptance letter.
4. **Submit the full application** to RGU's international office before the deadline.

## Deadline

**24 November 2026.** Confirm the exact date on the official RGU page.

The **Summer Undergraduate Research (SUR) Programme** at the **University of Lausanne (UNIL)** gives talented biology and medicine students from around the world **8 weeks of real lab research** in Switzerland. It is run by UNIL's Faculty of Biology and Medicine and is designed for students who are thinking about a research career.

## Programme dates

**5 July – 27 August 2027**, Lausanne, Switzerland. You must attend the whole programme.

## What is covered

- A **scholarship / living stipend** for all selected participants
- **Free accommodation**
- **Travel allowance:** up to **CHF 500** for European students and up to **CHF 1,200** for non-European students
- **Visa assistance** if you need a Swiss visa

## Research areas

Lab placements across UNIL's **biology and medicine** research groups. These include molecular and cell biology, neuroscience, immunology, cancer research, genetics, ecology and evolution.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- Studying **biology, medicine** or a closely related biological science
- You have completed **2 years** of a bachelor's degree and will be in your **third year** during the programme
- An **excellent academic record** (listings mention a GPA above about 3.75, or the top 5% of your class)
- **Good written and spoken English**
- **No previous research experience is needed**

**Not eligible:** physical therapy, nursing, dentistry and veterinary science students.

## How to apply

1. Visit the official UNIL SUR programme page and read the guidelines.
2. Look at the UNIL research groups and choose **two labs** where you would like to work.
3. Prepare your transcript, CV and personal statement.
4. Ask for your **recommendation letters**.
5. Complete the **online application**. There is **no application fee**.

Selection is based on academic performance, your personal statement and your recommendation letters.

## Deadline

Recent listings give **15 November 2026** (one says 5 November). The 2026 edition closed on 31 December 2025. **Confirm the exact date on the official UNIL page** and apply as soon as possible.

## Tips

- Read about the labs you choose and explain clearly why their research interests you.
- Show your interest in research, even through class projects or lab practicals.
- Ask your recommenders early.

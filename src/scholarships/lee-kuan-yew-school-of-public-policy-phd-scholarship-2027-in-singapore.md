The **Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy (LKYSPP)** at the National University of Singapore (NUS), one of Asia's leading public policy schools, offers the **LKYSPP PhD Scholarship** to outstanding students joining its PhD in Public Policy. The scholarship is open to Singaporeans and international students, and it comes with **no service bond**.

## What the scholarship offers

A monthly stipend, which rises by S$500 after you pass the PhD Qualifying Examination (usually within the first 18–24 months):

| Student | Before qualifying exam | After qualifying exam |
|---|---|---|
| International students | S$2,200 a month | S$2,700 a month |
| Singapore Permanent Residents | S$2,400 a month | S$2,900 a month |
| Singapore Citizens | S$2,800 a month | S$3,300 a month (plus CPF contributions) |

- Typical award period: **three years**
- **No service bond** after graduation

## Who can apply

- **All nationalities**, including international students
- A **relevant master's degree**
- An academic background in the **social sciences** and a strong interest in public policy and international affairs
- **At least two years of work experience** is preferred
- English proficiency: if your degree was not taught in English, you will usually need IELTS Academic (around 7.0) or TOEFL iBT
- GRE scores are optional

## Documents you need

- Master's degree certificate and official transcripts (plus bachelor's records)
- A research proposal that fits LKYSPP faculty research areas
- CV
- A writing sample
- Two academic or professional referees
- English test results, if required

## How to apply

1. **Develop your research idea** and read about LKYSPP faculty to find possible supervisors.
2. **Prepare your documents**, including your research proposal and writing sample.
3. **Contact your referees early** so their reports arrive on time.
4. **Apply online** through the NUS Graduate Admission System (GradApp), choosing the PhD in Public Policy.
5. **Opt in to the LKYSPP PhD Scholarship** in the application form.
6. **Submit by 10 December 2026** for the August 2027 intake.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications open | 1 August 2026 |
| Application and scholarship deadline | **10 December 2026** |
| PhD starts | August 2027 |

## Tips for a strong application

- **Write a focused research proposal.** Show a clear policy question, why it matters and how you will study it.
- **Match your topic to LKYSPP faculty**, and mention who you would like to work with.
- **Use your work experience.** Show how your professional experience shaped your research interests.
- **Pick a strong writing sample**, ideally a research paper or thesis chapter.
- Applying is free through the official NUS portal. Never pay anyone who promises a scholarship.

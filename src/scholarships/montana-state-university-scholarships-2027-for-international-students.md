**Montana State University (MSU)** in Bozeman, USA, offers scholarships to international students starting a bachelor's degree. The main award is **automatic**: you're considered when you apply for admission.

> **Important:** These scholarships cover **part** of your costs. MSU estimates the total cost for international undergraduates at **about $54,220 a year** (2025/26), including about **$31,214** in tuition. Plan how you'll pay the rest.

## 1. International Undergraduate Merit Scholarship
- **$5,000 to $12,000 per year** for first-year students
- **Transfer students** with a 2.5+ GPA: **$7,000** in tuition aid per year
- **Automatic.** No separate application. You're considered when you apply for admission.
- You need a cumulative GPA of at least **2.5 on a 4.0 scale**
- Renewable each year if you keep a **3.0 GPA**, study full-time and remain a non-resident
- Awards are given **in the order admission offers are made**, so apply early
- You must receive **full admission** (not conditional)

## 2. MSU Presidential Scholarship
- **Annual tuition waivers**, a **generous stipend** and other benefits for **four years**
- Based on academic achievement, leadership and personal qualities
- **Separate application**, which also serves as your Honors College application
- **Deadline:** early December each year, for **fall** first-year entry
- Not open to students with conditional admission or 12+ college credits

## Admission deadlines (international undergraduates)
- **Spring semester:** 15 November
- **Fall semester:** 1 July
- MSU recommends applying at least **six months** before your start date.

## How to apply
1. **Apply to MSU** as an international undergraduate (application fee: **US$38** online).
2. Submit your **transcripts** (with certified English translations if needed) and **proof of English proficiency**.
3. For the **Presidential Scholarship**, submit its separate application by **early December**.
4. Once you have full admission, the merit scholarship is offered **automatically** if you qualify.

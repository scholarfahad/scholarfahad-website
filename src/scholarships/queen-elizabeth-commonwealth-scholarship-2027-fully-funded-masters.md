The **Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarships (QECS)** are run by the **Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU)**. They fund talented young people from Commonwealth countries to study a **Master's degree** at a university in **another low- or middle-income Commonwealth country**.

The next round (**Cycle 1**, for courses starting between July and October 2027) is expected to open in **November or December 2026**.

## What the scholarship covers

- Full tuition fees
- A living allowance (stipend) for the length of the award
- Return economy flights to the host country
- A one-off arrival allowance
- A research support grant (on request, subject to approval)

Master's programmes can last **up to two years**.

## Where you can study

Host universities change every cycle. The 2026/27 Cycle 1 hosts were in:

- **Africa:** Kenya, Uganda, Eswatini, Mauritius
- **Asia:** Bangladesh, Sri Lanka
- **Caribbean:** Jamaica, Barbados, Trinidad and Tobago, Antigua and Barbuda

Only the universities and courses listed on the ACU website for the current cycle are eligible.

## Eligibility

- A **citizen of a Commonwealth country** (for example Nigeria, Ghana, Cameroon, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, India or Pakistan), or a refugee with status in a Commonwealth country
- You must study in a country **other than your own**
- At least a **2:1 (Second Class Upper) bachelor's degree** or equivalent
- You must have **already graduated** (final-year students cannot apply)
- **No age limit**
- Meet the host university's English language requirements

**Not covered:** PhDs, undergraduate degrees, part-time study and distance learning.

## How to apply

1. Check the list of host universities and courses for the current cycle on the ACU website.
2. Read your chosen university's admission requirements.
3. Create a **myACU account** and complete the QECS application.
4. Apply separately to the university for admission to the course.
5. You can apply to more than one host country, but only one application per university is considered.

## Timeline

- **Cycle 1** (courses starting July to October): applications open in November/December. Last year's deadline was **14 January 2026**. Results are announced in July.
- **Cycle 2** (courses starting January to February): applications open in March/April.

Deadlines are not extended, so apply early.

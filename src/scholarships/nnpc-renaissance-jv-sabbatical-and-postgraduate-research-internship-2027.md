Renaissance Africa Energy Company Limited, operator of the **NNPC/Renaissance Joint Venture** (formerly the Shell Petroleum Development Company JV), invites applications for its **2027 Sabbatical and Postgraduate Research Internship Programme**.

The programme gives Nigerian university lecturers and postgraduate students the chance to spend a year working on real industry challenges with Renaissance's teams in Port Harcourt.

This programme is open to **Nigerian universities, lecturers and students only**.

## Programme options

### 1. Sabbatical attachment (university lecturers)

**Who can apply:** university lecturers at the rank of **Senior Lecturer and above**.

**Disciplines:**

- **Environment:** impact assessment and biodiversity, environmental toxicology, air quality assessment, hydrobiology, water chemistry, hydrogeology, ecology
- **Corporate relations:** social performance, Host Community Development Trust (HCDT), stakeholder relations
- **Community health:** consultant community health, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics
- **Nigerian content development:** professors in any engineering discipline interested in process or project engineering
- **Exploration:** geology, geophysics, and AI in exploration workflows
- **Centres of Excellence:** geology, geophysics and petroleum engineering (University of Benin); marine/offshore engineering and naval architecture (Rivers State University)

### 2. Postgraduate research internship (university students)

**Who can apply:** Nigerian students currently studying for a **Master's, MPhil or PhD**.

**Disciplines:**

- **Environment:** impact assessment and environmental compliance monitoring
- **Occupational health:** health promotion, epidemiology, statistics
- **Pipeline availability:** PhD candidates in mechanical, chemical, materials or oil and gas engineering, with at least three years of pipeline engineering experience (design, construction or integrity assessment)
- **Exploration:** PhD candidates in geology or geophysics, with an AI-related exploration workflow

## Duration

Both the sabbatical and the research internship last **12 months** and cannot be renewed. **Previous participants are not eligible.**

## Documents you need

**All applicants:**

- An application letter stating your team or discipline of interest
- A CV with your phone number, email address and contact details for three referees

**Sabbatical applicants (lecturers):**

- A titled one-page summary of how you will add value to Renaissance's business during your sabbatical

**Research internship applicants (students):**

- A research proposal covering the background, technical objectives, methodology, data needed and expected outcomes

## How to apply

1. Choose **one team or discipline** from the lists above.
2. Prepare your application letter, CV and either your one-page summary (lecturers) or research proposal (students).
3. **Email your application** to **university-relations@renaissanceafrica.com**.
4. Use your **team or discipline of interest as the email subject line**, for example "Environment – Impact Assessment".
5. Send it before **6 November 2026**. Late applications will not be considered.

## Key dates

## Tips

- **Match your proposal to a real industry problem** in your chosen discipline, and show the data you'd need and the results you expect.
- **Keep your one-page summary practical** (lecturers): explain clearly how your expertise will help Renaissance's work.
- **Save your documents as PDFs** with clear file names.
- Applying is free. Renaissance does not ask for payment or work with agents, so never pay anyone who promises a placement.

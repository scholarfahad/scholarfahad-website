The **DAAD In-Country/In-Region Scholarship** at the **University of Ghana** is funded by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD). It supports talented graduates from **Sub-Saharan Africa** to study an **MPhil** at one of the University of Ghana's world-class research centres in Accra.

- **In-Country:** for Ghanaian students
- **In-Region:** for students from **other Sub-Saharan African countries**

## Programmes at the University of Ghana

1. **West Africa Centre for Crop Improvement (WACCI):** MPhil in **Seed Science and Technology**
2. **West African Centre for Cell Biology of Infectious Pathogens (WACCBIP):** MPhil in **Molecular Cell Biology of Infectious Diseases**

## What the scholarship covers

**All scholars:**
- **Tuition fees**
- **Monthly stipend**
- **Study and research allowance**
- **Thesis printing** costs

**In-Region scholars (from other African countries) also get:**
- **Travel allowance**
- **Health insurance**

## Who can apply

- You are a national (or recognised refugee) of a **Sub-Saharan African country**
- You hold a good **bachelor's degree** in a relevant field (for example agriculture, crop science, biology, biochemistry, microbiology or medical sciences)
- You meet the University of Ghana's admission requirements for the programme
- You plan a career in **teaching and/or research** in Africa
- **Women** and applicants from **less-privileged regions** are especially encouraged to apply

## How to apply

1. Choose **WACCI** or **WACCBIP**.
2. **Download and read the programme's DAAD call document** from the official DAAD listing. It lists the documents and the application method.
3. Prepare your certificates, transcripts, CV, motivation letter and recommendation letters.
4. Apply **exactly as the call says**, and also meet the University of Ghana admission requirements.
5. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**12 November 2026**

## Tips

- In your motivation letter, explain how the MPhil will help you become a lecturer or researcher in Africa.
- Choose the programme that closely matches your first degree.
- Don't wait. The deadline is very close.

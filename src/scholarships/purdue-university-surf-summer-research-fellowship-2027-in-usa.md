**Purdue University's Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)** gives undergraduates from around the world the chance to spend a summer doing real research in **engineering and science** labs at Purdue, in Indiana, USA. Fellows work with faculty mentors and graduate students on cutting-edge projects, then present their work at a research symposium.

## What fellows receive

- A **US$6,500 stipend**, paid every two weeks during the 10-week programme
- A **US$2,500 housing allowance** for students from other universities
- Hands-on research experience and mentoring
- Presentation at the SURF Research Symposium

You arrange your own housing (Purdue offers summer residence and intern housing options).

## Programme dates (summer 2027)

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Remote start | 31 May – 4 June 2027 |
| Move-in | 5–6 June 2027 |
| Orientation and in-person research | 7 June – 30 July 2027 |
| Research symposium | 29 July 2027 |
| Move-out | 31 July – 1 August 2027 |

## Who can apply

- **Full-time undergraduate students** at Purdue, other US universities, or **universities outside the US**
- Completed **at least one semester or quarter** by the time you apply
- A **GPA of 2.8/4.0** or higher
- Enrolled for at least one semester after the programme
- Not taking other courses, internships or summer programmes during SURF

## Documents you need

1. An **academic CV or résumé**
2. An **unofficial transcript**
3. A **statement of purpose**: how you came to your field, why you want SURF, how it fits your career goals, your preparation so far, and any challenges you've overcome
4. **One recommendation letter** from a professor or professional who knows your work well (required for non-Purdue students)

## How to apply

1. Read the information on the official **[Purdue SURF page](https://engineering.purdue.edu/Engr/Research/EURO/students/about-SURF/prospectiveSURFApplicants)**.
2. Prepare your CV, transcript and statement of purpose.
3. Ask your recommender early, so their letter arrives on time.
4. Submit your application online when it opens in early November.
5. **International applicants:** apply by **15 December 2026**.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications open | Early November 2026 |
| International deadline | **15 December 2026** |
| Priority deadline (US applicants) | 15 January 2027 |

## Tips

- **Choose projects that match your skills** and mention them in your statement.
- **Show your research curiosity**, through class projects, lab work or competitions.
- **Plan your visa early.** International fellows need a US visa for the summer.
- Applying is free. Never pay anyone to apply for you.

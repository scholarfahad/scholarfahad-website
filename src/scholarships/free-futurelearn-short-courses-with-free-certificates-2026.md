**FutureLearn** is a UK online learning platform that offers short courses from universities and organisations around the world, including the British Council, Accenture, SOAS University of London, the University of Reading and the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

Most FutureLearn courses are **free to learn**. Some courses also come with a **free digital certificate**, which you can add to your CV or LinkedIn profile.

## Free certificates vs paid certificates

- **Free to learn:** you can study most course materials at no cost.
- **Free certificate:** only on courses where the course page shows **"Digital upgrade: Free"**. These are usually sponsored by a partner organisation.
- **Paid certificate:** on other courses, the certificate costs extra (usually about US$74–149 per course), or comes with a FutureLearn Unlimited subscription.

Free offers change over time, so always **check the course page before you start**.

## Who can join

- **Everyone**, from any country
- No application or deadline
- A free FutureLearn account and an internet connection

## How to enrol and get your certificate

1. Go to [futurelearn.com/courses](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses) and **create a free account**.
2. **Search for the course** by name.
3. On the course page, **check that "Digital upgrade" says "Free"**.
4. Click **Join course** and choose the free digital upgrade.
5. **Complete the course:** mark at least 90% of the steps as complete and score at least 70% on any tests.
6. **Download your certificate** from your "Achievements" page.

## Tips

- Click **"Mark as complete"** on every step, or the course won't count as finished.
- **Finish one course at a time.** Short courses take only a few hours a week.
- **Add your certificates to your CV and LinkedIn.** They show employers and scholarship committees that you keep learning.
- Never pay a third party to "register" you for a free course.

Sciences Po in Paris, one of the world's top universities for politics, international affairs and public policy, is part of the **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program**. It **fully funds** talented young Africans with financial need to complete a **two-year master's degree**, preparing them to lead change in Africa.

> **Deadline:** The 2027 dates have not been published yet. Last year, applications opened in **early October** and closed on **4 January 2026**. Expect a similar deadline in **early January 2027**, so start preparing now.

## What the scholarship covers
The programme covers the **full financial needs** of selected Scholars for **both years** of the master's, together with:
- Support and mentoring throughout your studies
- Leadership and community activities with other Mastercard Foundation Scholars
- A waiver of the Sciences Po application fee for eligible applicants

## Who can apply
You must meet **all** of these:
- **Citizen of an African country.** You are not eligible if you also hold a citizenship from outside Africa.
- **Bachelor's degree** (completed or in progress) from one of Sciences Po's **partner universities**, or enrolled in a partner **bridging programme**. Check the official list first.
- **Financial need**, with supporting documents
- **Under 35** when the master's begins
- **Not already holding or studying for a master's degree**
- A clear **commitment to change in Africa**, especially in public policy and major political, environmental and social transitions
- Not a current Sciences Po student

## Eligible programmes
- **Two-year master's programmes** at any of Sciences Po's seven graduate schools (for example International Affairs, Public Affairs, Journalism, Law, Management and Innovation, Urban Studies, Research)
- **Not eligible:** one-year master's, dual degrees and joint master's programmes

## Language
The programme has no separate language test, but you must meet the **English or French** requirement of the master's you choose (usually at least **C1**).

## How to apply
1. **Check the partner list.** Confirm your bachelor's university or bridging programme is on Sciences Po's list.
2. **Request eligibility** for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program on the Sciences Po admissions platform.
3. **Apply to a two-year master's** at Sciences Po through the international graduate admissions procedure.
4. **Submit financial documents** showing your need.
5. Wait for the admission decision first, then the scholarship decision (last year: admissions from mid-December to early April, scholarship results in early May).

## Keep in mind
- The offer is only valid for the year you are selected. **Deferring makes you ineligible.**
- If your university is not a partner, look at other options such as the **Émile Boutmy Scholarship** for non-EU students.

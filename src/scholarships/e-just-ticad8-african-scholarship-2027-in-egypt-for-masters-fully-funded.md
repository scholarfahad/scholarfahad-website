The **E-JUST TICAD8 African Scholarships for Science, Technology and Innovation (STI)** are fully funded master's scholarships at the **Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST)** in **New Borg El Arab, Alexandria, Egypt**. They are offered under a partnership between the **Government of Egypt** and the **Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA)**, as part of Japan's TICAD8 commitments to Africa.

E-JUST follows a Japanese-style education model, with research labs, small classes and strong links to Japanese universities.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition**
- **Accommodation**
- **Monthly stipend**
- **Medical care**
- **Return international flights** between your home country and Egypt

**Duration:** 2 years for the MSc, plus up to **6 months of preparatory courses** if needed.

Scholars must study **full-time** and cannot work elsewhere during the scholarship.

## Fields of study (MSc)

- **Engineering**
- **Computer Science and Information Technology**
- **Basic and Applied Sciences**
- **International Business**
- **Humanities and Heritage Science**

## Who can apply

- You are a **citizen of an African country** (not Egypt)
- Your **current home address is in Africa**
- You are a **researcher or university instructor**, or expect to become one, and need an MSc in science, engineering or a related field
- You are **not serving in the military**
- You meet E-JUST's admission requirements and pass an **academic interview and oral exam**
- **Preference** goes to applicants who will return to their job at a home institution. They need an **endorsement form** from that institution.

## How to apply

1. Choose your **MSc programme** on the E-JUST website.
2. If you are employed, ask your institution for an **endorsement**.
3. Prepare your degree certificate, transcripts, CV, statement of purpose, recommendation letters and English proof.
4. On the E-JUST website, go to **International Admissions** and click **"Apply now"**.
5. Fill in the online form and upload all documents.
6. Shortlisted applicants take an **online interview and oral exam**.

## Deadline

The **Fall 2027** call has not been published yet. Last year's deadline was **15 February 2026**, so expect a similar date in **mid-February 2027**. Start preparing now.

## Tips

- Explain in your statement how the MSc will help you teach or do research in your home country.
- An endorsement from your university or employer can strengthen your application.
- Prepare well for the interview. Review the basics of your field.

The **Erasmus Mundus Master's in Journalism, Media and Globalisation (Mundus Journalism)** is a 2-year master's degree taught across top European universities. It is one of the world's leading international journalism programmes, and the **Erasmus Mundus Scholarship** pays for everything for the best applicants of **any nationality**.

## How the programme works

**Year 1:** Aarhus University and the Danish School of Media and Journalism, in **Denmark**

**Year 2:** you choose one specialism:
- **Cultures and Contexts:** LMU Munich, Germany
- **Politics and Communication:** University of Amsterdam, Netherlands
- **Authoritarianism and Transition:** Charles University, Prague, Czechia
- **Crisis and Conflict:** City St George's, University of London, UK

You graduate with a joint degree recognised across Europe.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** for both years (worth EUR 21,000 for non-EU students)
- **EUR 1,400 per month** for up to **24 months**, for housing, food, travel, visa and other costs
- **Free health and liability insurance**

About **10–15 scholarships** are expected for 2027–2029.

Students who don't win the full scholarship are expected to qualify for an **Erasmus+ stipend of up to EUR 6,000** for the second year.

## Who can apply

- Students of **any nationality**
- You have a **bachelor's degree** (at least 3 years of full-time study) in a relevant subject, or will finish it by **1 August 2027**
- You have at least **3 months of journalism experience**
- You can show **English proficiency** with an accepted test, unless you are exempt

You **cannot** get the scholarship if you already hold a master's of 2 years or more (after a 3-year bachelor's), or if you have had an Erasmus Mundus scholarship before.

## Selection

Scholarships go to the top-ranked applicants, based on:
- Academic performance
- Journalism and media experience
- The quality of your **Application Reflections** (the written part of your application)

## How to apply

1. Read the admission requirements and choose your **year-2 specialism**.
2. Prepare your documents: transcripts, diploma, English test result, CV and proof of journalism experience.
3. Write your **Application Reflections** carefully. This is a key part of selection.
4. Apply online through the Mundus Journalism application process. The same application covers admission and the scholarship.
5. Submit before **10 January 2027, 13:00 CET**.

Results are expected by **1 March 2027**, and the programme starts on **1 September 2027**.

## Tips

- Show real journalism work: published articles, radio or TV pieces, internships or student media. Add links where you can.
- In your Application Reflections, explain why your chosen specialism fits your career goals.
- Book your English test early so your result is ready before the deadline.

**Florida State University (FSU)**, a top public university in Tallahassee, Florida, offers international first-year students a **University Out-of-State Tuition Waiver**. It is a merit award that removes the most expensive part of tuition.

## What it covers

- **100% of the out-of-state portion of tuition**, about **$18,452 per year** (based on 30 credit hours)
- Worth about **$73,808 over four years** if you keep it
- **No separate application.** Admitted international first-year students are considered automatically.

## What it does NOT cover

- The **in-state portion** of tuition and university fees
- **Housing, food, health insurance, books and travel**
- Study abroad programmes

International students must also **show proof of funds** for their first year of study before FSU can issue the I-20 for the student visa. This is a big help, but it is **not a fully funded scholarship**.

## Who can get it

- International students **admitted as first-year (freshman) undergraduates**
- Chosen on **academic performance and test scores**
- Funds are limited, so strong and early applicants have the best chance

## Keeping the waiver

- Take **15 credit hours** each semester
- Keep an FSU GPA of at least **3.0**
- Make steady progress towards your degree

## Admission requirements for international students

- Online application (application fee: **$30**)
- Official transcripts and an evaluation of your secondary school results by a credential evaluation agency (this can be waived in some cases)
- **SAT, ACT or CLT** scores (self-reported)
- English test: **IELTS 6.5**, TOEFL iBT 80 (or 4.0 on the new scale), **Duolingo 125**, or PTE 55. Ask admissions whether you can be exempt if you were educated in English.
- Essay and résumé / list of activities
- Proof of financial support for the first year

## Deadline

FSU encourages students to **apply for admission by 1 December 2026** for Fall 2027, because waiver funds are limited. Early action has usually closed around **15 October**. Check the current dates on FSU's admissions website.

## Already studying at FSU?

Current international undergraduates with a **GPA of 3.5 or higher** can apply each semester for a separate **International Undergraduate Tuition Waiver** from FSU's Center for Global Engagement. It covers up to 12 credit hours of out-of-state tuition.

## How to apply

1. Read the international first-year requirements: https://admissions.fsu.edu/international
2. Apply online and pay the $30 fee.
3. Send your transcripts, test scores and English results.
4. You'll be considered for the tuition waiver automatically.

Questions: admissions@fsu.edu · +1 (850) 644-6200

## Tips

- Strong grades and a good SAT/ACT score matter most for this award.
- Plan your budget honestly. The remaining costs are still significant, so look for outside scholarships too.
- Never pay agents for "guaranteed" FSU scholarships.

**California State Polytechnic University, Pomona (Cal Poly Pomona, or CPP)** is a public university near Los Angeles, known for engineering, agriculture, business, hospitality and science. CPP offers **more than 400 scholarships** to its students, and you can be considered for many of them with **one general application**.

> **Scholarship season: 1 October to 2 March.** Submit the general application on the **Bronco Scholarship Portal** by **2 March** to be considered for next year's awards. Confirm the exact dates on the portal.
>
> **Please note:** these are mostly **partial scholarships** that help with university costs. They aren't usually full scholarships, so plan your other funding too.

## Who can apply

- **Students admitted to Cal Poly Pomona.** You must be admitted before you can apply.
- **Undergraduate and graduate students**
- **International students:** some scholarships are marked as **open to international students**. Use the portal's filter to find them.
- Each scholarship has its **own eligibility criteria**, such as major, grades, financial need or background.

## Key dates

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| General application opens | 1 October |
| General application deadline | **2 March** |
| Selection | 3 March to 30 June |
| Payments begin | From May, no earlier than 10 days before the semester starts |

The portal also stays open **year-round** for CSU systemwide and other scholarships.

## How to apply

1. **Apply to Cal Poly Pomona** and get admitted.
2. Log in to the **Bronco Scholarship Portal** and complete the **general application**.
3. Write your **personal statement (750 words or less)** about your academic goals, career plans and why a scholarship would help you.
4. Check which scholarships you match, and complete any **extra questions or short statements** they ask for, by each deadline.

## Tips

- **Apply early in the season.** Some scholarships have their own deadlines before 2 March.
- **Write a strong personal statement.** It's used for many scholarships at once, so it's worth the effort. Read our [motivation letter guide](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/).
- **International students:** focus on scholarships marked as open to international students, and check your **visa funding requirements**. US universities usually ask for proof of funds before issuing the I-20.
- **Don't confuse universities.** Cal Poly Pomona is different from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo (California Polytechnic State University), which has its own scholarships.

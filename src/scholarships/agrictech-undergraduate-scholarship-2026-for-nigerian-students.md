The **AgricTech Undergraduate Scholarship** is run by the **AgricTech Empowerment Network**, a Nigerian NGO that supports young people building careers in agriculture. It helps agriculture students in Nigeria pay for their studies and connects them with mentorship and training.

## What the scholarship covers

- Support for **tuition and academic expenses**
- Certification as an **AgricTech Scholar**
- Mentorship and training
- Possible internship opportunities
- A feature on the NGO's platforms

The organisers have not published a fixed amount.

## Who can apply

- You are a student at a recognised **Nigerian university, polytechnic or college of education**
- You are in **200 level (2nd year) or above**
- You study **agriculture, agricultural technology or a related course**
- **Grades:** First Class or Distinction holders get priority. Second Class Upper or Upper Credit holders may also be considered, based on passion, the video and relevant experience.
- The scholarship focuses on students from **selected South-West and North-Central states**. Check the official page to confirm your state is eligible.

## Documents you need

- **CV or resume**
- **Academic transcript.** An official copy or a student copy of at least your most recent session, signed or stamped by your registry or department.
- **A 2-minute introduction video** about your passion for agriculture
- **A certificate from an agriculture course on the FAO eLearning Academy.** These courses are free online.

## How to apply

1. Complete a free agriculture-related course on the **FAO eLearning Academy** (elearning.fao.org) and download your certificate.
2. Record a clear 2-minute video introducing yourself and explaining your passion for agriculture.
3. Get your transcript signed or stamped by your department or registry.
4. Fill in the online application form on the AgricTech Empowerment Network website and upload your documents.
5. Submit before the deadline.

## Deadline

**30 November 2026.** Applications may close early once enough have been received, so apply as soon as you can.

## Tips

- Start the FAO course today. It is the step that takes longest.
- In your video, be specific: what area of agriculture excites you, and what do you want to build or change?
- Applying should be free. Do not pay anyone who asks for money to process your scholarship.

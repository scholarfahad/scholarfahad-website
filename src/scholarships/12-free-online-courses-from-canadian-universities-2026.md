Top Canadian universities put some of their courses online for anyone in the world to take, **free of charge**. You learn at your own pace, from home, with no application, no IELTS and no deadline.

Here are 12 good ones to start with.

## Free to learn, but check the certificate

| Platform | Learning | Certificate |
|---|---|---|
| **McGill (own website)** | Free | **Free** attestation of completion |
| **edX** | Free to audit | Paid ("verified certificate") |
| **Coursera** | Free to audit on most courses | Paid, but **financial aid** is available |

**Audit** means you can watch the lessons and read the materials without paying. On Coursera, look for the **Financial aid available** link on the course page if you want the certificate but can't afford it.

## 1. Personal Finance Essentials (McGill University)

Budgeting, borrowing, saving, investing and buying a home. Takes a few hours, in English or French. **Completing all the modules earns a free McGill attestation of completion.**

Enrol: https://mcgillpersonalfinance.com/

## University of British Columbia (UBC) on edX

2. **Excel for Everyone: Core Foundations**: the Excel skills almost every job asks for
3. **How to Code: Simple Data**: a beginner-friendly start to programming
4. **Business Foundations**: an introduction to how businesses work
5. **Introduction to Marketing**: branding, customers and strategy
6. **Climate Change: The Science**: what's really happening to the climate
7. **Introduction to Quantum Computing**: for curious science students

Find them here: https://www.edx.org/school/ubcx

## University of Alberta on Coursera

8. **Indigenous Canada**: Indigenous histories and perspectives in Canada, 12 modules. Rated 4.8 by over 23,000 learners.
9. **Dino 101: Dinosaur Paleobiology**: one of the most popular science courses online
10. **Astro 101: Black Holes**: an introduction to astronomy

Find them here: https://www.coursera.org/ualberta

## University of Toronto on Coursera

11. **Introduction to Psychology**: how we think, feel and behave

Find it here: https://www.coursera.org/utoronto

## McMaster University on Coursera

12. **Learning How to Learn**: proven techniques to study smarter, co-created with UC San Diego. Very useful before exams or a master's degree.

Find it here: https://www.coursera.org/mcmaster

## How to enrol

1. Open the link for the course you want.
2. Create a free account on the platform: McGill's site, edX or Coursera.
3. Choose the **free / audit** option. Don't click "Buy" or "Subscribe" unless you want a paid certificate.
4. On Coursera, if you want the certificate, click **Financial aid available** and fill in the form.

## Tips

- Pick one or two courses and finish them, rather than starting ten.
- Add completed courses to your CV and LinkedIn under "Courses" or "Certifications".
- Online courses from known universities show initiative on scholarship applications, especially if they relate to the course you're applying for.

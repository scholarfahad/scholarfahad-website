The \*\*Mastercard Foundation Associates Program\*\* helps young Africans move from university into meaningful work. Associates get \*\*12-month paid work placements\*\* with employers across Africa, plus \*\*mentorship and employability training\*\*, to build leadership, job and entrepreneurship skills.

\> \*\*How it works:\*\* there's no single deadline. Roles are posted in \*\*rounds\*\* through regional recruitment partners. Register with the partner for your region now, so you're ready when the next call opens.

\## What Associates get

\- A \*\*12-month paid placement\*\* with an employer in Africa, or a remote role

\- \*\*Hands-on work experience\*\* in your field

\- \*\*Mentorship\*\* and \*\*employability training\*\*

\- Support from \*\*Talent Corps\*\*, a network of Mastercard Foundation alumni who offer career guidance

\## Who can apply

\- \*\*Young people aged 18 to 35\*\* living in Africa

\- \*\*Recent graduates\*\*, with \*\*0 to 2 years\*\* of work experience

\- \*\*Alumni\*\* of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program and other Foundation programmes

\- \*\*Refugees and internally displaced people\*\* are welcome

The programme \*\*prioritises young women, people with disabilities, and refugees and displaced youth\*\*.

Each region may have extra requirements. For example, in South Africa applicants must be aged 18 to 34, unemployed and hold at least a diploma or first degree.

\## Where to apply

Apply through the partner that covers \*\*where you live\*\*:

| Region | Recruitment partner |

|---|---|

| Anglophone West Africa (including Nigeria and Ghana) | \*\*Jobberman\*\* |

| East Africa | \*\*Shortlist\*\* |

| Southern Africa | \*\*YES (Youth Employment Service)\*\* |

| Francophone Africa | \*\*Talent2Africa\*\* |

| Remote roles | \*\*Talenteum\*\* |

The programme is coordinated by \*\*LEAP Africa\*\*, whose website lists the current links for each region.

\## How to apply

1\. \*\*Find your regional partner\*\* in the table above.

2\. \*\*Create a profile\*\* on the partner's platform and complete it fully.

3\. \*\*Watch for Mastercard Foundation Associates roles\*\*, and apply only for roles \*\*in your country of residence\*\* or \*\*remote roles\*\*.

4\. Keep your profile active and check regularly. New roles appear during each call.

\## Tips

\- \*\*Prepare a strong one-page CV\*\* now, so you can apply quickly when roles appear.

\- \*\*Follow the Mastercard Foundation Transitions Community on LinkedIn\*\* for application tips and announcements.

\- \*\*Beware of fake recruiters.\*\* The programme never asks for payment. Apply only through the official partners listed on the Mastercard Foundation and LEAP Africa websites.

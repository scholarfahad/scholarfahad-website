The **NNPC Ltd/TotalEnergies International Scholarship (TIS)** is a fully funded master's scholarship for young **Nigerians** to study in **France**. It is offered by the joint venture between **NNPC Limited** and **TotalEnergies EP Nigeria**, and has sponsored more than 150 Nigerian graduates since 2004.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** for the master's programme (1–2 years)
- **Monthly living stipend**
- **Accommodation** (provided, or a housing allowance)
- **Return flights** between Nigeria and France
- **Visa fee** reimbursement
- **Comprehensive medical insurance**

## Eligible fields

Master's programmes related to the **oil and gas industry** in:
- **Engineering**
- **Geosciences**
- **Management Science**

Programmes at **public universities** in France are an added advantage. You can search for programmes on the **Campus France Nigeria** website.

## Who can apply

- **Nigerian citizens only**
- A first degree with at least **Second Class Upper** (2:1)
- You graduated **within the last 5 years**
- You have **completed NYSC** (discharge or exemption certificate)
- Less than **3 years** of work experience after graduation

## Documents you need

- Degree certificate and transcripts
- NYSC discharge or exemption certificate
- CV
- Statement of purpose
- **Two recommendation letters**

## How to apply

1. When the call opens, go to the official portal: **csr-ngscholarship.totalenergies.com**
2. Create an account (switch the page to English if it opens in French).
3. Fill in your personal, academic and professional details.
4. Upload your documents and submit before the deadline.
5. Shortlisted candidates take an **online CBT** (quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning, logical thinking and current affairs).

## When to apply

The **2027/2028 call has not opened yet**. Last year's call opened in **December 2025** and closed on **31 January 2026**. Expect a similar window this year. **Start preparing your documents now.**

## Important

- **Applying is free.** TotalEnergies does not charge fees or use agents. Never pay anyone who promises to help you get selected.
- Apply only through the official portal.

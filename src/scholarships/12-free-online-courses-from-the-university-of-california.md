The **University of California (UC)** system includes some of the best public universities in the world. Several UC campuses offer free online courses on **edX** and **Coursera**, so anyone, anywhere, can learn from UC professors at their own pace.

You can study the course materials for free (called "auditing"). If you want a certificate to add to your CV or LinkedIn, you can pay for one, or apply for financial aid on Coursera.

## 12 free online courses from UC universities

### UC Berkeley (on edX)

1. **The Science of Happiness:** learn research-backed ways to build a happier, more meaningful life.
2. **How to Write an Essay:** improve your academic writing, from planning to editing. Useful for scholarship essays!
3. **Foundations of Data Science:** an introduction to data analysis and programming with Python.
4. **Blockchain Technology:** understand how blockchain works and how it's used.
5. **Effective Business Writing:** write clear, professional emails, reports and documents.

👉 [See all UC Berkeley courses on edX](https://www.edx.org/school/uc-berkeleyx)

### UC San Diego (on Coursera)

6. **Learning How to Learn:** one of the world's most popular online courses, with proven techniques to study smarter.
7. **Data Structures and Algorithms:** build strong programming and problem-solving skills.
8. **Object Oriented Programming in Java:** learn Java through hands-on projects.

👉 [See all UC San Diego courses on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/ucsandiego)

### UC Irvine (on Coursera)

9. **Academic English: Writing:** improve your English writing for university study.
10. **Project Management:** learn how to start, plan and manage projects successfully.

👉 [See all UC Irvine courses on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/uci)

### UC Davis (on Coursera)

11. **SQL for Data Science:** learn SQL, one of the most in-demand data skills.
12. **Geographic Information Systems (GIS):** create maps and analyse spatial data.

👉 [See all UC Davis courses on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/ucdavis)

## Benefits

- **Free to learn:** audit the course materials at no cost
- **Self-paced:** study whenever it suits you
- **Taught by University of California professors**
- **Optional certificates** to add to your CV, LinkedIn or scholarship applications
- **Financial aid on Coursera** for learners who can't afford the certificate fee

## Who can join

- **Everyone**, from any country
- No application or deadline
- You only need an internet connection and a free edX or Coursera account

## How to enrol

1. **Open the course page** on edX or Coursera using the links above.
2. **Create a free account** or sign in.
3. **Choose to audit the course for free.** On Coursera, look for the "Audit" option. On edX, choose the free track.
4. **Want a certificate?** Pay for the verified track, or on Coursera click "Financial aid available" and complete the short application. It usually takes about two weeks to be approved.

## Tips

- **Pick one or two courses** and finish them, rather than starting many at once.
- **Set a weekly study schedule** and stick to it.
- **Add your certificates to your CV.** They show scholarship committees that you're motivated to learn.
- Courses are free on the official platforms. Never pay a third party to "register" you for a free course.

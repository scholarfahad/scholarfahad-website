The **Vidyasirimedhi Institute of Science and Technology (VISTEC)** is a research-focused graduate institute in Rayong, Thailand. It is accepting applications for Master's and PhD programmes starting in 2027. **Every admitted student receives a full scholarship**, worth up to 5 million baht in total, with **no obligation to work for anyone after graduation**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **All university fees waived**
- **Monthly stipend of up to 20,000 baht**
- **On-campus housing or dormitory**
- **Research budget of up to 500,000 baht**
- **Study equipment support of up to 30,000 baht**
- **PhD students:** 1 million baht to do research abroad for up to one year
- Outstanding PhD students can later apply for the **Royal Srimedhi Scholarship**, which adds another 1 million baht for up to two years of research abroad
- Some programmes offer double degrees with leading overseas universities

## Schools

- School of Energy Science and Engineering (ESE)
- School of Molecular Science and Engineering (MSE)
- School of Biomolecular Science and Engineering (BSE)
- School of Information Science and Technology (IST): computer science, AI and data science

## Eligibility

- Open to Thai and international students
- **Master's:** a relevant Bachelor's degree, or be in your final undergraduate year
- **PhD:** a relevant Master's degree, or be in your final Master's year
- Final-year students must show they have completed their degree by 31 July 2027
- An official TOEFL, IELTS or CU-TEP score, submitted by the interview date

## Required documents

- CV
- Academic transcript
- Statement of purpose (can be sent before the interview)
- Two recommendation letters (can be sent before the interview)
- Official English test score
- Optional: publications, awards, portfolio

## How to apply

1. Choose your school and programme on the VISTEC admission website.
2. Register on the online admission portal. Each person can create a maximum of 2 application forms.
3. Upload your documents and submit before the deadline.
4. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an interview and written exam.

## Key dates

- Application period: **1 October 2026 to 15 February 2027**
- Interview invitations: 22 February 2027
- Interview and written exam: 27 February 2027
- Results: 31 March 2027

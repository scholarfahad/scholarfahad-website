The **Taiwan Scholarship**, funded by Taiwan's **Ministry of Education (MOE)**, supports outstanding international students to take a full **bachelor's, master's or PhD** degree at a Taiwanese university. Many programmes are taught in **English**.

> **Applications open 1 February 2027** and run until **30 April 2027**. Your local Taiwan representative office sets the exact deadline for your country, which may be earlier. Prepare your documents now.

## What the scholarship covers
- **Tuition and basic fees:** up to **NT$40,000 per semester** (you pay any amount above this)
- **Monthly stipend:**
  - **Bachelor's:** NT$15,000 a month
  - **Master's and PhD:** NT$20,000 a month

**Not covered:** accommodation, insurance, internet, administration fees and thesis supervision fees.

## How long it lasts
- **Bachelor's:** up to 4 years
- **Master's:** up to 2 years
- **PhD:** up to 4 years
- Maximum 5 years in total per person

## Who can apply
- **Foreign nationals** (not ROC citizens or overseas compatriot students; applicants from mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau are not eligible)
- Strong academic results
- Not already studying at a Taiwanese university
- **Language:** for English-taught programmes, an English test such as TOEFL or IELTS; for Chinese-taught programmes, **TOCFL Level 3** or higher
- You must apply separately for **admission** to a Taiwanese university

## Documents (typical)
- Scholarship application form
- Passport
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- Proof that you have applied to a Taiwanese university
- Language certificate
- **Two sealed reference letters**

Your local Taiwan office may ask for more.

## How to apply
1. **Find your local Taiwan representative office** (Taipei Economic and Cultural Office or Taipei Trade Office) that covers your country, and check its 2027 notice, usually published by late January.
2. **Apply for admission** to a Taiwanese university by its own deadline.
3. **Submit your scholarship application** online at the Taiwan Scholarship portal and/or to your local office, as instructed.
4. **Attend the interview** if invited.
5. If selected, send your university admission letter to the office by the deadline it gives you.

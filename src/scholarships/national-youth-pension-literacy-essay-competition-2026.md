As part of **National Pension Week 2026**, Nigeria's pension industry, including the **National Pension Commission (PenCom)**, has launched the **National Youth Pension Literacy Essay Competition**. Young Nigerians are invited to share their vision for retirement in Nigeria, with **₦2.5 million in total cash prizes**, plus laptops and trophies.

> **Deadline: 20 October 2026.** Winners will be announced on **26 October 2026**. The official page says the rules are still under review, so check npw.ng for any updates before you submit.

## Essay topic

**"My Dream for a Great Retirement Experience in Nigeria"**

Write about what a good retirement in Nigeria should look like, and how today's choices, policies and innovations could make it happen.

## Categories and prizes

| Category | Who can enter | Word limit | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Secondary school students in Nigeria | **500 words** | **₦1,000,000** + laptop + trophy + certificate |
| **B** | Tertiary students (university, polytechnic, college of education) and **NYSC members** | **1,000 words** | **₦1,500,000** + laptop + trophy + certificate |

## Rules

- **One essay per person**
- Your essay must have a **title**
- Upload as **PDF, DOCX or TXT** (maximum 10 MB)
- Entrants under 18 need **parent or guardian consent**
- The essay must be **your own original work**. Essays substantially written or rewritten by **AI tools are not allowed**, and any limited AI help for research or brainstorming must be disclosed.
- Shortlisted entrants may need to show a **student ID or NYSC identification**, and may be asked to **explain their essay** to confirm they wrote it

## How essays are judged

**Category A (secondary):** originality and creativity (30%), understanding of pensions and retirement (25%), relevance to the topic (20%), organisation and clarity (15%), grammar and writing (10%)

**Category B (tertiary and NYSC):** research and understanding (25%), critical thinking (25%), practical recommendations (20%), innovation and originality (15%), structure and writing (15%)

## How to enter

1. Go to the **official competition page** on npw.ng (link above).
2. Choose your **category**.
3. Fill in your details and your school, institution or NYSC information.
4. Enter your **essay title** and upload your essay.
5. Submit **before 20 October 2026**.

## Tips for a winning essay

- **Learn the basics first.** Read about Nigeria's Contributory Pension Scheme, Retirement Savings Accounts (RSAs) and the role of PenCom, so your essay shows real understanding.
- **Make it personal and specific.** Describe what a great retirement looks like for someone in your family or community, then explain what needs to change.
- **Offer practical ideas** (especially in Category B), such as pension awareness for young workers, coverage for informal sector workers, or using technology to make pensions easier to manage.
- **Stay under the word limit**, and proofread carefully.
- **Write it yourself.** Judges check originality, and finalists may have to discuss their essays.

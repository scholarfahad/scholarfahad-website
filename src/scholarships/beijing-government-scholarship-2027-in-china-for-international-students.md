The **Beijing Government Scholarship (BGS)** is offered by the **Beijing Municipal Government** to attract outstanding international students to universities in Beijing. It supports **bachelor's, master's and PhD** students, as well as preparatory students, visiting students and research scholars.

You do not apply to the government directly. You apply to a **participating Beijing university**, which selects scholarship winners.

## Scholarship categories

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Category A** (fully funded) | Tuition, accommodation, **monthly living allowance** (about RMB 2,500–3,500, depending on level and university) and comprehensive medical insurance |
| **Category B** | Tuition, accommodation and medical insurance |
| **Category C** | Tuition and medical insurance |

**Not covered:** international flights and visa costs.

**Duration:** the standard length of your degree. Each year your university reviews your performance, and your category can change or your scholarship can be stopped.

## Participating universities

More than **50 universities in Beijing**, including Tsinghua University, Peking University, Beihang University, Beijing Normal University, Beijing Institute of Technology, Renmin University, BUPT, UIBE, Beijing Foreign Studies University and China Agricultural University.

## Who can apply

- **Non-Chinese citizens** in good health
- **Age limits** on enrolment: under **25** (bachelor's), under **35** (master's), under **40** (PhD)
- **Grades:** usually an average of 80+ or GPA 3.0+ for Categories B and C. Category A generally needs a GPA of 3.6+ or a degree from a top-ranked university.
- **Language:**
  - English-taught programmes: TOEFL 85+ or IELTS 6.5+ (or proof of English)
  - Chinese-taught degree programmes: **HSK 4** or higher
- Master's and PhD applicants need **two recommendation letters** from associate professors or higher
- You cannot hold another Chinese government or university scholarship at the same time

## Documents you usually need

- Passport
- Highest degree certificate and transcripts (with translations)
- **Study plan** (master's) or **research proposal** (PhD)
- Two recommendation letters (master's and PhD)
- English or HSK certificate
- Foreigner Physical Examination Form
- Police clearance certificate (some universities)

## How to apply

1. Choose a **participating Beijing university** and programme. The full list is on the "Studying in Beijing" section of the Beijing government's English website.
2. Apply on the university's **international admissions portal**.
3. In the scholarship section, choose the **Beijing Government Scholarship**.
4. Upload your documents and submit before **that university's deadline**.
5. The university reviews your application and tells you the result.

## Deadlines

Each university sets its own deadline, usually between **December 2026 and April 2027**. Some top universities close as early as **December**, so check your chosen university now.

## Tips

- Apply to more than one university to improve your chances.
- A strong study plan or research proposal can help you get Category A.
- If you study in Chinese, take the HSK exam early.

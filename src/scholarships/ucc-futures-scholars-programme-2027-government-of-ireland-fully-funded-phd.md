University College Cork (UCC) in Ireland has opened the **UCC Futures Scholars Programme (GoI)** for PhD study starting in October 2027. The programme is funded through Research Ireland's Empower Programme, and successful candidates are recognised as **Government of Ireland Postgraduate Scholarship** holders.

The programme is open to **all nationalities**, has **no age limit**, and covers **any discipline**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **€25,000 stipend per year** (tax-free living allowance)
- **Tuition fees:** up to €5,750 per year, and UCC waives the remaining non-EU fee for international scholars
- **At least €5,000 per year** for research costs
- Up to **4 years** of funding for new PhD students

Scholars pay only the small annual student capitation fee.

## Eligibility

- Open to applicants of any nationality, with no age limit
- A First Class or Upper Second Class (2.1) honours degree, or a Master's degree (or the international equivalent)
- If you are still finishing your degree, you must meet all requirements by 30 September 2027
- You must meet UCC's postgraduate English language requirements
- You must study full-time and live in Ireland during the award
- **Not eligible:** people who already hold a PhD, have previously held a doctoral scholarship, or are permanent staff at a university or research organisation

## How to apply

1. **Find a UCC supervisor** in your field and email them as soon as possible with your CV and a short research idea. The **supervisor submits the application** through UCC's system, so you cannot apply alone.
2. Each supervisor can support only **one** applicant, so contact supervisors early.
3. Work with your supervisor on the application form and a **research proposal of up to 2,700 words**.
4. Prepare your transcripts, English test results (if needed) and an **ORCID iD**.
5. Shortlisted candidates take an online interview with a 3-minute presentation.

## Key dates

- **Application deadline:** 11 December 2026, 5:00 pm Irish time
- Shortlisting: mid-January 2027
- Interviews: 9 to 12 February 2027
- Results: end of February 2027
- PhD starts: 1 October 2027

Some departments ask for expressions of interest earlier (for example, by mid-November), so check with your preferred department now.

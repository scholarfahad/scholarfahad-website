The Monbukagakusho (MEXT) scholarship is offered by Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology. It supports international students at many levels, including undergraduate, research student and graduate study.

## What it covers

MEXT is fully funded. It generally covers tuition and admission fees, a monthly allowance, and a return flight to Japan. Some categories include a period of Japanese language training before your degree starts.

## Two ways to apply

1. **Embassy recommendation.** Apply through the Japanese embassy or consulate in your country. The embassy runs document screening, written exams in some categories, and interviews.
2. **University recommendation.** Some Japanese universities nominate students directly, often those already in contact with the university or on exchange.

## Who can apply

Rules depend on the category, but commonly include:

- Citizenship of a country with diplomatic relations with Japan
- Age limits that differ by category (for example, research student applicants are usually under 35)
- Academic qualifications that meet the category's entry level
- Willingness to learn Japanese

## Documents you'll need

- Application form
- Field of study and research plan (for research and graduate categories)
- Academic transcripts and graduation certificate
- Recommendation letter
- Medical certificate
- Language certificates, if you have them

## Tips

For research categories, your research plan carries a lot of weight. Contact potential supervisors in Japan early, and show how your topic fits their work.

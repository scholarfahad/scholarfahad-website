The Fulbright Foreign Student Program lets graduate students, young professionals and artists from abroad study and do research in the United States. It is run in each country by a Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy.

## What it covers

Fulbright grants generally fund tuition, a return airfare, a living stipend and health insurance. The exact package depends on your country and the type of grant.

## Who can apply

Eligibility and selection are set separately in each country. Common requirements include:

- Citizenship of the country where you apply
- A completed bachelor's degree
- Strong English language skills
- Ability to return home after the grant ends

## How to apply

Find the Fulbright page for your country through the official Fulbright foreign student website. It lists the local deadline, the documents needed and whether you apply through a Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy.

## Documents you'll need

Requirements vary by country but usually include:

- Online application
- Study or research objectives essay
- Personal statement
- Transcripts
- Three letters of reference
- English test results (TOEFL or similar), and sometimes the GRE or GMAT

## Tips

Fulbright is also about cultural exchange. Besides academic goals, show how you'll share your culture in the US and how you'll use the experience when you return.

**ExxonMobil** is one of the world's largest energy and chemical companies. Every year it offers **paid internships** for university students at its offices around the world. Interns work on real projects in engineering, science, technology and business, with mentoring from experienced staff.

ExxonMobil does not run one single global internship programme. Instead, each country office **posts its own internship roles** on the official careers portal, with its own requirements and deadlines.

## What interns usually get

- A **monthly salary or stipend** (the amount depends on the country)
- **Real project work** with a professional team
- **Mentoring and training**
- Strong experience for your CV, and sometimes a route to a graduate job

**Example:** the ExxonMobil Brazil internship pays a monthly stipend of BRL 2,400 plus a transport allowance and life insurance, for up to 2 years.

## Common fields

- Engineering (chemical, mechanical, electrical, petroleum and more)
- Geoscience and science
- IT, data and software
- Finance, accounting, supply chain and business

## Who can usually apply

- **University students**, normally in the middle or final years of a bachelor's or master's degree
- **Strong academic results** (many roles ask for a 2:1 or equivalent)
- **Good English**; some countries also want the local language
- You must be **allowed to work or study in the country** where the internship is based. Most roles do not offer visa sponsorship.

## Recent internship locations

Recent openings have included Brazil, Argentina, Australia, China, Malaysia and Thailand, among others. New roles appear throughout the year.

## How to apply

1. Go to the official ExxonMobil careers portal: **jobs.exxonmobil.com**.
2. Search for **"intern"** or **"internship"**.
3. Filter by **your country** or the country where you are allowed to work.
4. Open a role and read the requirements and deadline carefully.
5. Create an account, upload your CV and documents, and click **Apply**.

## Deadlines

Each internship has its **own deadline**. Many roles for the next year are posted between July and November, so check the portal regularly.

## Tips

- Apply only for roles in countries where you have the right to work.
- Tailor your CV to the role. Highlight relevant courses, projects and technical skills.
- ExxonMobil never charges a fee to apply. Ignore anyone who asks for payment.

The **Tokyo Global Partner Scholarship (Tokyo GP)** is a fully funded scholarship from the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government**, offered at **Tokyo Metropolitan University (TMU)**. It brings talented international students to Tokyo for a master's or PhD. The aim is for them to become future partners who connect Tokyo with their home countries.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Monthly stipend of JPY 150,000**
- **Full tuition** for the standard study period
- **Admission fee** and entrance exam fee waived
- **International airfare** to Japan, and a return ticket after you graduate (conditions apply)
- **Housing referral** service (you pay rent and meals from your stipend)
- Japanese language classes

**Duration:** 2 years (master's) or 3 years (PhD)

## Fields of study

Humanities, Law and Politics, Management, Science, Urban Environmental Sciences, Systems Design and Human Health Sciences. Each graduate school decides which programmes take Tokyo GP scholars.

## Who can apply

You must meet TMU's graduate entry requirements **and** one of these conditions:

- You are studying (bachelor's for a master's, master's for a PhD) at a **university in an Asian country or region**, in one of **Tokyo's sister or friendship cities**, or at a **TMU partner university**
- **Or** you work at a research institution, government body or company in one of those places

Also:
- **No upper age limit.** Working professionals and people who already hold a master's can apply.
- You should be committed to building links between Tokyo and your home country.
- You must be willing to learn basic Japanese for daily life.
- You cannot hold another scholarship at the same time.

Language requirements vary by graduate school. Some programmes are taught in English, and others need Japanese.

## How to apply

1. **Choose a TMU master's or PhD programme** and read its application guidelines.
2. **Contact a prospective supervisor** and get their permission to apply. Many schools require this first.
3. **Complete any pre-screening** your graduate school asks for.
4. **Prepare your documents.** These usually include transcripts, degree certificates, a research plan, a statement of purpose, a CV, recommendation letters and language certificates.
5. **Submit your application**, often by post, following the graduate school's instructions.
6. **Take the written and/or oral exam or interview.**

## When to apply

- **October 2027 entry:** applications usually open around **February** and close around **April 2027**. Exams are in May and results in June.
- **April 2028 entry:** pre-screening is around August, applications in October, exams in November and results in December.

Each graduate school sets its own exact dates, so check the guidelines for your programme.

## Tips

- Start contacting professors now. Supervisor approval is the step that takes longest.
- Write a clear research plan that shows how your work connects to Tokyo or to cooperation between Japan and your country.
- Check carefully that your university or employer is in an eligible place before you apply.

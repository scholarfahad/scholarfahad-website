**China University of Petroleum (Beijing) (CUP)** is one of China's top universities for **petroleum, energy and geoscience**. Every year it welcomes international students on the **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)** for bachelor's, master's and PhD study.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition** (paid directly by the government)
- **Free dormitory** (double room on campus)
- **Comprehensive medical insurance**
- **Monthly stipend** (CSC rates):
  - Master's: **RMB 3,000**
  - PhD: **RMB 3,500**
  - Bachelor's: a lower monthly stipend (CSC rate)
- One year of **Chinese language training** if your programme needs it

**Flights are usually not covered.**

## Ways to apply

| Route | How | Period (last cycle) |
|---|---|---|
| **CSC Type A** | Through the **Chinese Embassy** (or your country's scholarship agency) in your country, plus a CUP pre-admission letter | About December to March |
| **CSC Type B** | Through **CUP directly**, agency number **11414** | **25 October to 15 June** |

There is also a **Beijing Government Scholarship** route through the CUP portal.

## Who can apply

- **Non-Chinese citizens** in good health, with no criminal record
- **Age limits:** under **25** (bachelor's), under **35** (master's), under **40** (PhD)
- **Bachelor's:** high school diploma, average above 70% (GPA 3.0/5.0), plus a qualifying **CSCA** test score
- **Master's:** bachelor's degree. **PhD:** master's degree.
- **Language:**
  - English-taught: **IELTS 6.0–6.5** or **TOEFL 75–80** (native speakers or English-medium graduates may be exempt)
  - Chinese-taught: **HSK 4**

## Documents you usually need

- Passport bio page
- Highest degree certificate and transcripts (with translations)
- Language certificate (IELTS, TOEFL or HSK)
- **Study plan or research proposal** (master's and PhD)
- **Two recommendation letters** (master's and PhD)
- Physical examination form
- Non-criminal record certificate
- CSCA score report (bachelor's)

## How to apply (Type B)

1. **Contact professors** at CUP in your field (helpful for master's and PhD).
2. **Register** on the CUP international student portal and apply, choosing the CSC scholarship.
3. Pay the **application fee** (about **RMB 400 / USD 70**; not needed for Type A).
4. Download your **pre-admission letter**.
5. Apply on the **CSC portal** (studyinchina.csc.edu.cn) using agency number **11414**, uploading the same documents.
6. Wait for results (usually June to July), then apply for an X1 study visa.

## Deadline

The full 2027 call has not been published yet. Last year's **Type B** deadline was **15 June**, so expect **mid-June 2027**. Apply early, because reviews often happen in batches.

## Tips

- A professor's acceptance letter can greatly improve your chances.
- Choose a field that matches CUP's strengths: petroleum engineering, geology, chemical engineering or energy.
- Get your medical exam and police certificate early. They take time.

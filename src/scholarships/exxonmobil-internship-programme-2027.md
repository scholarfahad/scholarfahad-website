**ExxonMobil**, one of the world's largest energy companies, runs paid internships for university students in several countries. Each country posts its own roles on the ExxonMobil careers site, and each one has its own requirements.

## Open now: Singapore 2027 engineering internship

ExxonMobil Singapore is accepting applications for its **1H 2027 internship**, including the **summer internship from 27 May to 27 August 2027**.

- **Allowance:** S$2,200 per month (about US$1,700)
- **Who can apply:** students taking any engineering or STEM degree. Leadership in co-curricular activities is preferred.
- **Where:** ExxonMobil's manufacturing sites on Jurong Island or Pioneer Road, Singapore
- **Roles:** process, electrical, machinery, control systems, instruments and controls, process control applications, analyser, fixed equipment and inspection, and environmental engineering
- **Assessment:** a final review by your supervisor and a final presentation or report

The posting doesn't list a closing date, so apply early.

[Apply for the Singapore 2027 internship](https://jobs.exxonmobil.com/job/Singapore-2027-Internship-Combined-Manufacturing-Engineering-%281H2027%29-627596/1427845900/)

## Nigeria: graduate internship (closed for now)

ExxonMobil's **Graduate Intern** programme in Lagos is a one-year paid programme for Nigerian graduates who have completed NYSC. The 2026 round asked for:

- A minimum Second Class Upper in accounting, business, social science, engineering, law or a related field
- An NYSC discharge or exemption certificate, with no more than 2 years since completing NYSC

**The official posting is now closed.** If a website says it is still open, check the official ExxonMobil careers site first. Set a job alert there for "Lagos" so you're notified when the next round opens.

## Other countries

ExxonMobil also posts internships and co-ops in places like the **United States**, **Australia** and **Argentina**. Most are for students enrolled at universities in that country who can legally work there.

To find them, go to the careers site and search **"intern"** with your country.

## Before you apply

- **Check the right to work.** Most internships are for students who can legally work in that country. Read each posting before applying.
- **Never pay anyone.** ExxonMobil never charges fees to apply or to get an interview. Messages asking for payment "to process your internship" are scams.
- **Tailor your CV** to the role: projects, lab work, internships and leadership.
- **Apply early.** Rolling roles close once they are filled.

## How to apply

1. Go to the ExxonMobil careers site: https://jobs.exxonmobil.com/
2. Search "internship" or "intern" and filter by your country.
3. Apply online with your CV and academic transcript.
4. Create a job alert so new roles come to your email.

Nusa Putra University in Sukabumi, West Java, Indonesia, is accepting applications for its **International Undergraduate Scholarships 2027/28**. International high school graduates can study an English-taught bachelor's degree with full tuition support.

## Scholarship tracks

**1. Genusian Scholarship (fully funded)**
- Full tuition for 8 semesters (4 years)
- Monthly living allowance
- Accommodation
- Visa and residence permit fees
- Indonesian health insurance (BPJS)
- Free Indonesian language course (BIPA)
- Airport pickup

**2. Islamic Boarding track**
- The same benefits as the Genusian Scholarship
- For practising Muslims who agree to live full-time at a pesantren (Islamic boarding school)

**3. Berlian Scholarship (tuition only)**
- Full tuition for 4 years plus the Indonesian language course
- Does **not** cover living costs, accommodation, visa or health insurance

**Not covered by any track:** passport, international flights and medical checks.

## Eligibility

- Open to international students
- A high school certificate (or equivalent) with a strong academic and conduct record
- **Age limit:** 22 for the Genusian and Islamic Boarding tracks; 26 for the Berlian track
- A passport valid for at least 18 months
- English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo, or a certificate from an English-medium school)
- A recommendation letter and a clean criminal record

## Programmes (English-taught)

Engineering (mechanical, civil, electrical, computer), computer science, information systems, visual communication design, law, accounting, business administration and education.

## Documents

- Passport copy
- Passport-style photo (red background)
- High school certificate and transcript
- Recommendation letter
- English proficiency certificate (if available)

## How to apply

1. Apply online through the Nusa Putra University international admission page and choose your scholarship track.
2. Upload your documents as PDF files.
3. Take the online computer-based test on **13 January 2027**.
4. Attend an interview between **14 and 23 January 2027**.
5. Results are announced on **1 February 2027**.

## Deadline

**31 December 2026**

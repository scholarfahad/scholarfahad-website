The **CEPS Climate Leadership Programme** is run by the **Centre for European Policy Studies (CEPS)**, one of Europe's leading think tanks, based in **Brussels, Belgium**. It trains young people who are already working on climate issues and brings them to Brussels to meet EU policymakers and experts.

Only **8 participants** are selected from around the world.

## Programme structure

### 1. Online training (December 2026 – February 2027)
These sessions are **mandatory**. Topics include:
- How EU climate policy is made
- Climate diplomacy
- Writing policy briefs
- Communicating your ideas

### 2. Brussels week (28 February – 6 March 2027)
- Meetings and workshops in Brussels
- Participation in the **CEPS Ideas Lab** on **4–5 March 2027**, one of Europe's biggest policy events

## What is covered

- **Travel** to and from Brussels
- **Visa** costs
- **Accommodation** during the Brussels week

The call does not say whether meals and local costs are covered. Ask CEPS if you are selected.

## Who can apply

- Young people from **any country**, aged **20 to 30**
- You are already active in **climate policy, advocacy or entrepreneurship**
- You have a **concrete climate initiative**, such as a project, campaign, organisation or business
- You have **good English**
- You can attend **all online sessions** and the **full Brussels week**

CEPS especially welcomes applicants from regions most affected by climate change, including Africa.

## How to apply

1. **Read the official CEPS call** and make sure you can attend both the online training and the Brussels week.
2. **Complete the online application questionnaire.**
3. **Email your CV and one reference letter** (both in English) to the address given in the official call. Use the subject line: *Climate Leadership Programme – [Your Full Name]*.

## Deadline

**10 November 2026, 23:59 CET**

The call also mentions 11 November in one place, but apply by **10 November** to be safe.

## Tips

- Describe your climate initiative clearly: what problem it tackles, what you have achieved so far, and what you want to do next.
- Ask your referee early. They need to know your climate work well.
- Show how the programme will help you grow your project and influence policy in your country.

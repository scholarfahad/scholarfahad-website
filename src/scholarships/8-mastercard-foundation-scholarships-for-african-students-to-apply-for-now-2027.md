The **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program** offers full scholarships to talented young Africans at partner universities in Africa and around the world. Scholarships usually cover **tuition, accommodation, travel, a living stipend and leadership support**, though the exact package varies by university.

Here are **8 opportunities open now or opening soon for 2027**, sorted by deadline, so you can see what to prepare first.

> **Always confirm on the official page.** Each university has its own eligibility rules, application process and dates.

## Deadlines in October and November 2026

**1. University of Cape Town, South Africa (Postgraduate)**
- **Deadline: 31 October 2026** for eligible postgraduate programmes
- Undergraduate applications for 2027 are already closed
- [Official page](https://uct.ac.za/mastercardfdn/apply-mastercard-foundation-scholars-program)

**2. African Leadership College of Higher Education, Mauritius (Undergraduate)**
- **Deadline: 14 November 2026** for international students; **23 January 2027** for Mauritian students
- Full-ride scholarships covering full tuition plus extra financial support for eligible applicants
- [Official page](https://alcheducation.com/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/)

**3. African Leadership University, Rwanda (Undergraduate)**
- **Deadline: 30 November 2026**
- [Official page](https://www.alueducation.com/apply-now/)

## Deadlines in December 2026 and January 2027

**4. Sciences Po, France (Master's)**
- The 2027 cycle runs from **October to mid-December 2026** for Mastercard Foundation eligibility and the application-fee waiver
- Check the official page for the exact timeline
- [Official page](https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/en/fees-funding/bursaries-financial-aid/mastercard-foundation-scholarships/graduate-study/)

**5. University of California, Berkeley, USA (Master's)**
- Applications run from **September 2026 to early January 2027**, depending on the programme; most deadlines fall in December
- To be considered for an **application-fee waiver**, complete the preliminary information by **15 November**
- [Official page](https://mcfscholarsprogram.berkeley.edu/graduate-application-process)

**6. University of Cambridge, UK (Master's)**
- **Open now.** Deadlines depend on the course, generally **early December 2026 or early January 2027**
- Covers **full tuition**, a **maintenance allowance** and other support
- [Official page](https://www.mastercardfoundation.fund.cam.ac.uk/apply/how-apply)

**7. Carnegie Mellon University Africa, Rwanda (Master's)**
- Applications opened **1 October 2026**. **Early deadline: 15 December 2026. Final deadline: 15 January 2027**
- Eligible programmes: **Information Technology**, **Electrical and Computer Engineering** and **Engineering Artificial Intelligence**
- [Official page](https://www.africa.engineering.cmu.edu/impact/mastercard-foundation-scholars.html)

**8. EARTH University, Costa Rica (Undergraduate)**
- Application period: **15 October 2026 to 31 January 2027**
- [Official page](https://admissions.earth.ac.cr/kenya)

## Bonus: Jim Leech Mastercard Foundation Fellowship on Entrepreneurship

**Queen's University, Canada.** This isn't a degree programme, but an **8-month entrepreneurship programme** for African students and recent graduates, with **training, mentorship, financial support** and help developing a business.

- **Deadline: 1 December 2026**
- [Official page](https://www.queensu.ca/innovationcentre/programs/jim-leech-mastercard-foundation-fellowship-entrepreneurship)

## More partner universities

The Mastercard Foundation works with many more universities. See the [full list of partner institutions and their application status](https://mastercardfdn.org/en/what-we-do/our-programs/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/where-to-apply/).

## Tips for applying

- **Start with the closest deadlines.** UCT closes on 31 October, and ALCHE and ALU in November.
- **Show your commitment to giving back.** The Mastercard Foundation looks for young people who want to improve their communities and Africa.
- **Show financial need honestly.** These scholarships are for talented students who couldn't otherwise afford university.
- **Prepare your documents early:** transcripts, recommendation letters, a personal essay and proof of financial circumstances.
- **Never pay anyone to apply.** Mastercard Foundation scholarships are free to apply for through the universities' official websites.

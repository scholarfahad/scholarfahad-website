The **University of Richmond**, a top private liberal arts university in Virginia, USA, is accepting applications for the **Richmond Scholars Program** for students starting in fall 2027. It is the university's most competitive merit scholarship, and **international students are fully considered**.

## What the scholarship covers

- **Full tuition**
- **University housing** (guaranteed on campus)
- **Meal plan**
- Renewable for up to **8 semesters** (4 years)
- A one-time **$5,500 enrichment grant** for an academic activity you choose, such as research, study abroad or an internship
- Faculty mentoring, priority course registration and cohort programmes

Only **25 students** are selected each year.

## Need-based aid for international students

Richmond also aims to meet the **full demonstrated financial need** of admitted international students. In 2025/26 the average aid award to international students was about **$69,900**. Admission is **need-aware** for international students, so asking for aid can affect your chances.

## Eligibility

- First-year undergraduate applicants of any nationality
- An excellent academic record, leadership and community involvement
- English proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo), unless you qualify for a waiver
- SAT/ACT scores are optional for 2027 entry

## How to apply

1. Apply through the **Common Application** or the Coalition Application. The **application fee is waived** for international applicants.
2. There is **no separate scholarship form**. Submitting a complete application by 1 December means you are automatically considered.
3. If you need financial aid, also submit the **CSS Profile** (school code 5569) and the **Certification of Financial Responsibility**.
4. Finalists are notified by early February and interviewed in mid-February 2027.
5. Final decisions are made by 1 March 2027.

## Deadlines

- **Richmond Scholars (all merit scholarships): 1 December 2026**
- Early Decision II and Regular Decision admission: 1 February 2027 (need-based aid only, with no merit scholarship consideration)

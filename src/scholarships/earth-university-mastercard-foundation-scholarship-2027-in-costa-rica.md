EARTH University in Costa Rica offers a hands-on, four-year degree in Agricultural Sciences focused on sustainable development and entrepreneurship. Through the **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program**, talented young people from Africa, Latin America and the Caribbean who could not otherwise afford university can study there on a full scholarship. Scholars have come from 42 countries.

> **Applications are open now** and close on **31 January 2027**.

## What the scholarship covers
- Tuition
- Basic living expenses, including housing and food (EARTH lists the full cost as US$36,856 a year for the January 2026 start, covering tuition, student services, housing, food and medical insurance)
- Medical insurance
- Mentoring, tutoring and leadership development
- Internship opportunities and career services

## Who can apply
- Secondary school graduates, preferably **aged 17 to 30**
- From Africa (including Nigeria), Latin America or the Caribbean
- Strong academic results in your school system
- Leadership and community involvement
- Families who cannot afford university. Full scholarships go to students who would otherwise be unable to attend higher education.
- A commitment to returning home to contribute to your community

## Documents
- Online application form
- Transcripts from your **last three years** of secondary school (not university transcripts)
- Secondary school certificate or proof of completion
- **Three recommendation letters** from people who are not family members
- Supporting documents (awards, certificates; a language certificate is optional)

## How to apply
1. **Create an account** on the EARTH admissions site. Wait two days for your login email and check your spam folder. Do not submit the form twice.
2. **Complete your application** at apply.earth.ac.cr using the password from your "EARTH UNIVERSITY INFORMATION" email.
3. **Review:** three faculty members review each application (this can take up to two months).
4. **Tests:** selected candidates take an aptitude test and a psychometric test, and fill in a socioeconomic form.
5. **Interviews:** group and individual interviews with faculty.
6. **Family socioeconomic study,** then the financial support committee decides.
7. **Admission and immigration:** EARTH helps admitted students get a student permit.

## Timeline
- **15 October – 31 January:** applications received
- **1 February – 15 March:** profile review and socioeconomic forms
- **April:** exams and interviews
- **June:** admission and immigration process
- **January:** classes begin

## Keep in mind
- It is very competitive: about **50 full scholarships** are awarded from **over 1,000 applications** each year.
- EARTH also gives **partial scholarships**, where the family pays part of the cost.
- The university is in **Costa Rica, Central America**, not the Caribbean.

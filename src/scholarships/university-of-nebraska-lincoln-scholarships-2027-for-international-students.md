The **University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL)**, a major public research university in the United States, offers **Global tuition scholarships** to international undergraduate students. Every admitted international student is **automatically considered**, with no separate scholarship application.

## Scholarships for international students

### Global Excellence Scholarship

- **US$15,000 a year** towards UNL tuition (US$7,500 per semester)
- Renewable for **four years** (up to US$60,000 in total)
- For **new first-year** international students

### Global Ambassador Scholarship

- **US$10,000 a year** towards UNL tuition (up to US$40,000 over four years)
- For **first-year and transfer** international students with outstanding academic achievement

You can receive **only one** Global scholarship. The scholarships go towards tuition only, not housing or living costs.

## Who can apply

- **International (non-US)** students who will be classed as Nebraska non-residents
- A strong academic record
- Meet the **English language requirement** for unconditional admission
- Full-time study at the Lincoln campus

## Keeping your scholarship

- **Global Excellence:** enrol full-time, complete 24 UNL credits each year and keep a **3.0 cumulative GPA**
- **Global Ambassador:** complete 24 credits each year and keep a **2.0 cumulative GPA**

## How to apply

1. **Apply for admission** to the University of Nebraska–Lincoln.
2. **Get considered automatically** for Global scholarships once you are admitted.
3. **Meet the English requirement** (by 15 July for fall, or 1 December for spring).
4. **Pay your enrolment deposit** in MyRED by **15 May**.

## Key dates for fall 2027

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Priority date | 1 November 2026 |
| Final date | 1 May 2027 |
| Enrolment deposit | 15 May 2027 |
| English proficiency | 15 July 2027 |

Spring 2027 applicants should apply by 1 December 2026.

## Tips

- **Apply early.** Scholarship funds are limited.
- **Send strong grades and English test results** so you qualify for unconditional admission.
- **Plan your other costs.** The scholarships cover part of the tuition only.
- Applying for these scholarships is free. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed award.

**Liberty Career Academy (LCA)** offers yearly scholarships to help students in Nigeria pay for their education, together with mentorship. There are three award categories: for students in financial need, for top academic performers, and for vocational trainees.

> **Deadline:** 3 November 2026, **11:59 PM West Africa Time**. Applications opened on 20 October 2026.

## The three awards

### 1. Adeola Okungbaye Award
- For **orphans** and students from **single-parent families** with financial need
- Minimum **CGPA 4.0/5.0** (or equivalent), or **JAMB 240/400** if recently admitted

### 2. Academic Excellence Award
- Minimum **CGPA 4.0/5.0** (or equivalent), or **JAMB 270/400** if recently admitted
- **Extracurricular involvement** and **leadership potential**

### 3. Gifted Hands Award
- For **artisans and vocational trainees** aged **18–35**
- Admitted to or enrolled in an **accredited vocational or technical institution** in Nigeria
- **Currently unemployed**, with leadership potential
- Funds go towards your **training, tools or equipment**

**Award amounts:** LCA's page describes financial support for tuition and educational costs but does not list amounts. Last year's awards were reported at ₦200,000 (Adeola Okungbaye), ₦150,000 (merit) and ₦100,000 (Gifted Hands).

## Who can apply (all categories)
- **Nigerian citizens**, or legal residents from another African country studying or living in Nigeria
- Enrolled in **years 1–3** of a four-year programme, **years 1–4** of a five-year programme, or **years 1–5** of a six-year programme (**final-year students are not eligible**)
- A **valid government-issued ID** (or one supported by a direct family member's ID)
- You may apply for **one category only**

## What you need
- WAEC/WASSCE and JAMB results, and your latest CGPA if already in school
- A **500-word personal statement**, written by you. **AI-written statements are disqualified automatically.**
- **Proof** of every achievement you mention (shortlisted applicants must provide evidence and may be interviewed)

## How to apply
1. Choose the **one award** that fits you best.
2. Write your personal statement in your own words.
3. Gather your ID, results and proof of achievements.
4. Apply on LCA's scholarship page before **3 November 2026, 11:59 PM WAT**.

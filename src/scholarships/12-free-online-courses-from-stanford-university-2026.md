You don't need to get into Stanford to learn from Stanford. The university offers more than 100 online courses on **edX**, and you can **audit them for free**. You watch the lessons and learn the material without paying. If you want a certificate, you can pay a fee for a verified certificate.

> **Free vs certificate:** Learning is free. Certificates cost money. On Coursera you can apply for **financial aid** to get the certificate free or cheaper.

## 12 free Stanford courses to start with

### Computer science and programming
**1. Computer Science 101**
A beginner-friendly introduction to how computers and code work. No experience needed.

**2. Algorithms: Design and Analysis (Part 1 and Part 2)**
A classic Stanford course on how to design fast, efficient algorithms. Great preparation for tech interviews.
[Part 1 on edX](https://www.edx.org/learn/algorithms/stanford-university-algorithms-design-and-analysis-part-1)

**3. Databases (several short courses)**
Learn SQL and how databases work, from modelling and theory to semistructured data.
[Databases: Modeling and Theory](https://www.edx.org/learn/databases/stanford-university-databases-modeling-and-theory) · [Databases: Semistructured Data](https://www.edx.org/learn/relational-databases/stanford-university-databases-semistructured-data)

**4. R Programming Fundamentals**
An introduction to R, one of the most popular languages for statistics and data analysis.

**5. Compilers**
An advanced course on how programming languages are translated into machine code.
[Compilers on edX](https://www.edx.org/learn/computer-science/stanford-university-compilers)

### Data science and machine learning
**6. Statistical Learning with Python**
A highly rated introduction to machine learning and statistical modelling, using Python.

**7. Statistical Learning with R**
The same core ideas, taught in R.

**8. Mining Massive Datasets**
How to analyse very large data sets, from recommendation systems to search engines.

**9. Machine Learning (Andrew Ng), on Coursera**
The famous beginner course by Stanford's Andrew Ng, offered by DeepLearning.AI and Stanford Online. About 3 weeks at 10 hours a week, self-paced.
[Supervised Machine Learning on Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/machine-learning)

### Health and society
**10. Introduction to Healthcare Communication Strategies**
For health workers and students who want to communicate better with patients.

**11. International Women's Health and Human Rights**
How health, gender and human rights connect around the world.

**12. Your Body Inside and Out: Using Exercise Physiology to Slow Aging**
The science of exercise and how it keeps the body healthy.

### Bonus: How to Learn Math
A free, self-paced course from Stanford that helps students of all levels become more confident in maths.
[How to Learn Math on edX](https://www.edx.org/learn/math/stanford-university-how-to-learn-math-for-students)

## How to enrol for free
1. Go to [Stanford's page on edX](https://www.edx.org/school/stanfordonline) and pick a course.
2. Create a free edX account.
3. Click **Enrol** and choose the **audit** (free) option.
4. For Coursera courses, click **Enrol for free**, then choose to preview or apply for **financial aid** if you want the certificate.

## Tips
- Add these courses to your CV and LinkedIn. They show initiative to scholarship committees.
- Pick one course and finish it before starting another.
- If a course is archived, you can usually still watch the videos.

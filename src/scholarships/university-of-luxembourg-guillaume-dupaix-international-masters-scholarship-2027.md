The **Guillaume Dupaix International Master's Scholarship** supports excellent international students on two-year master's programmes at the **University of Luxembourg**.

> **Important for Nigerian readers:** This scholarship is **only open to applicants from specific countries**, and **Nigeria is not currently on the list**. If you are from Nigeria, see our other Europe scholarships below.

## What the scholarship gives you
- About **€11,000 per academic year**, for up to **two years**
- Part of it covers your **accommodation** (rooms cost about €500–650 a month); the rest is paid in instalments each semester
- **Up to 30 scholarships** a year, a maximum of 10 per faculty

## Who can apply
Applicants for a full-time, two-year master's at the University of Luxembourg who are from:
- **Western Balkans**
- **Ibero-American countries** (Latin America, Spain and Portugal)
- **Belarus, Egypt, India, Moldova, Russia, Thailand, Turkey, Ukraine, United Kingdom, Vietnam**

Graduate dual-degree students from partner universities in China, Thailand and Vietnam may also qualify.

Also required:
- **Excellent undergraduate results**
- A master's that runs fully in Luxembourg (no compulsory semester abroad)
- Not receiving Luxembourg's State Financial Aid for Higher Education

## How to apply
1. **Apply for a two-year master's** on the University of Luxembourg admissions portal before the deadline (last year: **24 March 2026**).
2. If the programme director rates your application as excellent, you'll receive a **link to the scholarship application**.
3. Submit the scholarship form, a **personal statement** and **two academic recommendation letters**.
4. Finalists are told by **mid-May**.

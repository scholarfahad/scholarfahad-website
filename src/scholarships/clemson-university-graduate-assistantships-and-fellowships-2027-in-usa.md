**Clemson University**, a leading public research university in South Carolina, USA, supports many graduate students through **graduate assistantships** and **fellowships**. These awards can cover a large part of your study costs and give you a monthly income.

## Graduate assistantships

A graduate assistantship is a paid position in which you work part-time for your department while studying full-time.

- **Types of work:** teaching, research or administration
- **Hours:** usually **10 to 28 hours a week**
- **Benefits:** an **annual stipend** (salary) plus a **tuition discount** linked to your hours
- You must stay **enrolled full-time**
- Assistantships are offered and managed by **individual departments, colleges and research centres**

## Fellowships

- **Merit-based awards** that don't require any work
- Funded by Clemson or by outside organisations, such as government agencies and professional associations
- Faculty usually **nominate** students, and selection is competitive
- Some fellowships require a specific research focus, a set course load or time at the sponsoring organisation

## Who can apply

- Students admitted to a **master's or PhD** programme at Clemson
- **International students** are eligible for assistantships offered by their department
- A strong academic record and relevant skills for the teaching or research role

## How to get funding

1. **Choose your programme** and check its admission and funding information on the department website.
2. **Apply for admission** to the Clemson Graduate School before your programme's deadline.
3. **Contact the department** or graduate coordinator to ask about assistantships, and email professors whose research matches your interests about research assistant (GRA) positions.
4. **Check Clemson Joblink** and department websites for assistantship openings.
5. **Ask about fellowships**, since many are by faculty nomination.

## Deadlines

Deadlines **vary by programme**. Many departments make funding decisions with admission, so apply as early as possible for fall 2027 entry and check your programme's website for its exact date.

## Tips

- **Email professors before you apply.** Research assistantships often depend on a professor having funding.
- **Highlight teaching or research experience** in your CV and statement of purpose.
- **Apply early.** Funding is limited and often offered with admission.
- **Ask about health insurance and fees**, which vary by assistantship.
- Applying is free through Clemson's official channels. Never pay anyone who promises an assistantship.

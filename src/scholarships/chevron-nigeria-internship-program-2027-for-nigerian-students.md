Chevron Nigeria Limited (CNL), which operates in a joint venture with NNPC Limited, runs a yearly internship programme for Nigerian students. Interns are placed at Chevron's Lagos office (Lekki) and receive a monthly stipend.

The 2026 call closed on 8 June 2026. The next call is expected around **May to June 2027**, so start preparing your documents now.

## Programme tracks

- **Undergraduate Internship:** for students in a Bachelor's, ND or HND programme where the internship (SIWES/IT) is a compulsory graduation requirement. It lasts 3 to 12 months, depending on your SIWES requirement.
- **Postgraduate Internship:** for students in a Master's, Doctorate or postgraduate professional programme where research or industry experience is a programme requirement. It lasts at least 6 months: up to 12 months for a Master's and up to 24 months for a PhD.
- Chevron also runs separate tracks for newly graduated **pharmacists, medical laboratory scientists and medical radiographers** who hold provisional licences from PCN, MLSCN or RRBN.

## Eligibility

- Open to **Nigerian citizens only**, studying in Nigeria or abroad
- You must be currently enrolled in an eligible programme
- You need a recommendation letter from your institution (a SIWES letter for undergraduates)
- Placements cover engineering, sciences, social sciences, business/management, technology and other fields related to Chevron's operations
- You need good communication and interpersonal skills

## Benefits

- A monthly stipend (the amount is not published)
- Hands-on experience at one of Nigeria's largest energy companies
- Mentoring and exposure to industry work

## How to apply

1. Get your institution's recommendation letter ready (a SIWES letter for undergraduates).
2. When the call opens, create an account on the Chevron careers portal (Workday) and choose the track that matches your programme.
3. Upload your documents and submit before the deadline.
4. Shortlisted candidates take an aptitude test and/or an interview. Final selection depends on performance and available places.

## Deadline

The deadline for the 2027 cycle has not been announced yet. In past cycles, applications closed in early June (5 June 2025 and 8 June 2026). Follow Scholar Fahad for an update when the call opens.

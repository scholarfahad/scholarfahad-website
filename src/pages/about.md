I know what a scholarship can do because I have depended on them myself. A Katsina State Government Scholarship paid for my Bachelor of Science in Accounting at Umaru Musa Yar'adua University in Nigeria, where I graduated with first class honours as the best graduating student, ranked first out of 58. A fully funded master's scholarship then took me to Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia (UIII), where I completed a Master of Finance specialising in Sustainable Finance.

Along the way I saw that talent is rarely the problem. Many capable students miss out because they hear about opportunities too late, or because nobody shows them how to write a strong CV, motivation letter or research proposal. I started Scholar Fahad on Facebook to change that, and today more than {{ site.followers }} people follow along for new opportunities.

## Helping students has always been part of my work

As president of the Nigerian Universities Accounting Students Association at my university, I led a scholarship appeal to the Office of the Accountant General of Katsina State. Ten students facing financial difficulties were selected and fully sponsored. Scholar Fahad grows from the same idea: the right information at the right time can open doors.

Because I write research proposals, academic papers and scholarship applications myself, the guides on this site come from experience, not theory.

## What I believe

- **Scholarship information should be free.** Every listing and guide on this site is free to read, and official scholarships are free to apply for.
- **Official sources come first.** Each listing links to the scholarship provider's own website. Always confirm the current details there before you apply.
- **Your application should be your own work.** My guides and reviews help you write better; I never write applications for you to submit as your own.

## How the site stays free

The site is supported by advertising and by clearly labelled sponsored posts from education partners. Sponsored content never changes how a scholarship is described. Optional [document review services](/services/) also help cover the costs.

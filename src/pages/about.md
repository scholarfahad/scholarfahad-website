Scholar Fahad started as a Facebook page sharing scholarships, job openings and tips for writing CVs, cover letters and research proposals. Today more than {{ site.followers }} people follow along for new opportunities.

This website brings that work together in one place, so you can search past and current scholarships, read each one's eligibility and requirements, and find the official application link without scrolling through a feed.

## What we believe

- **Scholarship information should be free.** Every listing and guide on this site is free to read, and official scholarships are free to apply for.
- **Official sources come first.** Each listing links to the scholarship provider's own website. Always confirm the current details there before you apply.
- **Your application should be your own work.** Our guides and reviews help you write better; we never write applications for you to submit as your own.

## How we keep the site free

The site is supported by advertising and by clearly labelled sponsored posts from education partners. Sponsored content never changes how we describe a scholarship. Optional [document review services](/services/) also help cover our costs.

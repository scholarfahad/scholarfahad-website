## We are not a scholarship provider

{{ site.name }} shares information about scholarships offered by governments, universities and other organisations. We are not affiliated with these providers unless a post clearly says so, and we cannot award scholarships or influence selection.

## Always check the official website

Deadlines, eligibility rules and benefits change every year. We work to keep listings accurate, but the provider's official website is always the final word. Confirm every detail there before you apply.

## No guarantees

Our guides and review services aim to help you write stronger applications, but nobody can guarantee that an application will be successful.

## Sponsored content and ads

Some posts may be sponsored by education partners. These are always labelled "Sponsored". The site also shows advertising, which helps keep all scholarship information free.

## Beware of scams

Official government scholarships do not charge application fees. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed scholarship, visa or admission. If someone contacts you claiming to be from {{ site.name }} and asks for money for a scholarship, it is not us.
